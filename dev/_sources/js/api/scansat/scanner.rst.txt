Scanner
=======


.. class:: Scanner

   A SCANsat scanner on a part. Obtained by calling :func:`ScanSat.scanner` or
   :func:`ScanSat.scanners`.

   .. method:: getPart()

      The part containing this scanner.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: :class:`SpaceCenter.Part`
      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: getScanTypes()

      The scan types of the scanner.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: :class:`ScanSat.ScanType`
      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: getActive()
               setActive(value)

      Returns whether the scanner is switched on, and switches it on or off, as the buttons
      of the part do.

      :Property: Can be read or written
      :rtype: ``boolean``
      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         A resource scanner only switches on when :func:`ScanSat.getInstantResourceScan`
         is off, or :func:`ScanSat.getStockResourceScan` is off.

   .. method:: getFieldOfView()

      The field of view of the scanner at its best altitude, in degrees.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``number``
      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: getMinAltitude()

      The lowest altitude the scanner scans at, in meters.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``number``
      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: getBestAltitude()

      The lowest altitude for the full field of view, in meters.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``number``
      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: getMaxAltitude()

      The highest altitude the scanner scans at, in meters.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``number``
      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: getRequiresLight()

      Whether the scanner only scans in daylight, when :func:`ScanSat.getDaylightCheck`
      is on.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``boolean``
      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: getState()

      The state of the scanner.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: :class:`ScanSat.ScannerState`
      :Game Scenes: Flight




.. class:: ScannerState

   The state of a scanner. See :meth:`ScanSat.Scanner.getState`.


   .. attribute:: off

      Switched off.


   .. attribute:: outOfRange

      Switched on, and outside its minimum and maximum altitudes.


   .. attribute:: inDarkness

      Switched on, and paused as it needs light and the ground below it is in darkness.


   .. attribute:: inRange

      Scanning between its minimum and best altitudes, with a reduced field of view.


   .. attribute:: inBestRange

      Scanning between its best and maximum altitudes, with its full field of view.
