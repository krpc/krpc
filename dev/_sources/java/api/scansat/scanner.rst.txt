.. package:: krpc.client.services.ScanSat

Scanner
=======


.. type:: public class Scanner

   A SCANsat scanner on a part. Obtained by calling :meth:`ScanSat.scanner(SpaceCenter.Part)` or
   :meth:`ScanSat.scanners(SpaceCenter.Vessel)`.

   .. method:: SpaceCenter.Part getPart()


      The part containing this scanner.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: ScanType getScanTypes()


      The scan types of the scanner.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: boolean getActive()

   .. method:: void setActive(boolean value)

      Returns whether the scanner is switched on, and switches it on or off, as the buttons
      of the part do.

      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         A resource scanner only switches on when :meth:`ScanSat.getInstantResourceScan()`
         is off, or :meth:`ScanSat.getStockResourceScan()` is off.

   .. method:: float getFieldOfView()


      The field of view of the scanner at its best altitude, in degrees.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: float getMinAltitude()


      The lowest altitude the scanner scans at, in meters.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: float getBestAltitude()


      The lowest altitude for the full field of view, in meters.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: float getMaxAltitude()


      The highest altitude the scanner scans at, in meters.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: boolean getRequiresLight()


      Whether the scanner only scans in daylight, when :meth:`ScanSat.getDaylightCheck()`
      is on.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: ScannerState getState()


      The state of the scanner.

      :Game Scenes: Flight




.. type:: public enum ScannerState

   The state of a scanner. See :meth:`Scanner.getState()`.


   .. field:: public ScannerState OFF

      Switched off.


   .. field:: public ScannerState OUT_OF_RANGE

      Switched on, and outside its minimum and maximum altitudes.


   .. field:: public ScannerState IN_DARKNESS

      Switched on, and paused as it needs light and the ground below it is in darkness.


   .. field:: public ScannerState IN_RANGE

      Scanning between its minimum and best altitudes, with a reduced field of view.


   .. field:: public ScannerState IN_BEST_RANGE

      Scanning between its best and maximum altitudes, with its full field of view.
