Camera
======


.. type:: krpc_DockingCamera_Camera_t

   A camera from Docking Camera (KURS), on an onboard camera part or a docking port.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_DockingCamera_Camera_Part(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_SpaceCenter_Part_t * returnValue, krpc_DockingCamera_Camera_t instance)

      Get the part containing this camera.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_DockingCamera_Camera_Image(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_bytes_t * returnValue, krpc_DockingCamera_Camera_t instance, krpc_DockingCamera_ImageFormat_t format, int32_t quality)

      The image from the camera, 256 by 256 pixels.
      Throws an exception if the camera is off or not started.

      :Parameters:

       * **format** -- The format of the image.
       * **quality** -- The quality of a JPEG image, from 1 to 100.

      :returns: The image data.
   

      .. note::

         The camera is switched on by setting the ``IsEnabled`` field of its
         ``PartCameraModule`` or ``DockingCameraModule`` to ``true``, which also opens
         the camera window.




.. type:: krpc_DockingCamera_ImageFormat_t

   The file format of an image.


   .. macro:: KRPC_DOCKINGCAMERA_IMAGEFORMAT_PNG

      PNG, which is lossless.


   .. macro:: KRPC_DOCKINGCAMERA_IMAGEFORMAT_JPEG

      JPEG, which is lossy, and smaller and faster to encode than PNG.
