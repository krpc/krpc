DockingCamera
=============


This service provides functionality to interact with
`Docking Camera (KURS) <https://spacedock.info/mod/1542>`_.


.. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_DockingCamera_Available(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue)

   Whether Docking Camera (KURS) is available.

   




.. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_DockingCamera_Camera(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_DockingCamera_Camera_t * returnValue, krpc_SpaceCenter_Part_t part)

   Get the camera on a part.
   Throws an exception if the part is not an onboard camera or a docking port.

   :Parameters:

    * **part** -- The part.
