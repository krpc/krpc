Rendezvous
==========


.. type:: krpc_KerbalEngineerRedux_Rendezvous_t

   Angles and times for a transfer to the vessel's target.

   .. note::

      When the vessel and target orbit different bodies, these are for the orbits of the bodies
      that share a parent.   .. macro:: KRPC_STRUCT_KERBALENGINEERREDUX_RENDEZVOUS

      The service and structure ids of the structure, for :func:`krpc_KRPC_Type_StructTypeById`.


   .. member:: double phase_angle

      The angle from the vessel to the target, around the vessel's orbit, in radians.

      .. macro:: KRPC_FIELD_KERBALENGINEERREDUX_RENDEZVOUS_PHASEANGLE

         The position of the field, for :func:`krpc_KRPC_Expression_GetFieldByIndex`.


   .. member:: double intercept_angle

      The phase angle at which to start a Hohmann transfer to the target, in radians.

      .. macro:: KRPC_FIELD_KERBALENGINEERREDUX_RENDEZVOUS_INTERCEPTANGLE

         The position of the field, for :func:`krpc_KRPC_Expression_GetFieldByIndex`.


   .. member:: double time_to_intercept_angle

      The time until the phase angle equals the intercept angle, in seconds.

      .. macro:: KRPC_FIELD_KERBALENGINEERREDUX_RENDEZVOUS_TIMETOINTERCEPTANGLE

         The position of the field, for :func:`krpc_KRPC_Expression_GetFieldByIndex`.


   .. member:: double angle_to_ascending_node

      The angle to the ascending node with the target's orbit, in radians.

      .. macro:: KRPC_FIELD_KERBALENGINEERREDUX_RENDEZVOUS_ANGLETOASCENDINGNODE

         The position of the field, for :func:`krpc_KRPC_Expression_GetFieldByIndex`.


   .. member:: double angle_to_descending_node

      The angle to the descending node with the target's orbit, in radians.

      .. macro:: KRPC_FIELD_KERBALENGINEERREDUX_RENDEZVOUS_ANGLETODESCENDINGNODE

         The position of the field, for :func:`krpc_KRPC_Expression_GetFieldByIndex`.


   .. member:: double time_to_ascending_node

      The time until the ascending node with the target's orbit, in seconds.

      .. macro:: KRPC_FIELD_KERBALENGINEERREDUX_RENDEZVOUS_TIMETOASCENDINGNODE

         The position of the field, for :func:`krpc_KRPC_Expression_GetFieldByIndex`.


   .. member:: double time_to_descending_node

      The time until the descending node with the target's orbit, in seconds.
