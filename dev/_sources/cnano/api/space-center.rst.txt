Supported Mods
--------------

The SpaceCenter service supports the following mods. None of them needs to be installed, but
some functionality requires one of them, and the docs note where it does.

* `Ferram Aerospace Research`_: :type:`krpc_SpaceCenter_Flight_t` works with the stock
  aerodynamics or with Ferram Aerospace Research, and reads from whichever is in use. Some
  members require it, such as :func:`krpc_SpaceCenter_Flight_LiftCoefficient`, and
  :func:`krpc_SpaceCenter_Part_Lift` and :func:`krpc_SpaceCenter_Part_Drag` are not
  available with it.
* `Extended Action Groups`_ adds action groups 10 to 250 to :type:`krpc_SpaceCenter_Control_t`.
* `RealFuels`_ adds ignitions, ullage and feed pressure to :type:`krpc_SpaceCenter_Engine_t`,
  and tank pressure and boil-off to :type:`krpc_SpaceCenter_Part_t`.
* :type:`krpc_SpaceCenter_Parachute_t` objects support `RealChute`_.
* :type:`krpc_SpaceCenter_Fairing_t` objects support `ProceduralFairings`_.
* :type:`krpc_SpaceCenter_SolarPanel_t` objects support the curved solar panels of
  `Near Future Solar`_.
* :type:`krpc_SpaceCenter_Experiment_t` objects support DMagic's
  `DMModuleScienceAnimateGeneric`_.
* :func:`krpc_SpaceCenter_Engine_ThrustReversed` supports the thrust reversers of
  `Firespitter`_ and `KerbalActuators`_.
* `KSPCommunityPartModules`_ 0.5 or later, or `kOS`_, provides the name tag in
  :func:`krpc_SpaceCenter_Part_Tag`.
* `B9PartSwitch`_ switches a part's subtype when its field is set through
  :type:`krpc_SpaceCenter_PartField_t`.
* `TweakScale Rescaled`_ parts report their scaled masses, resources, thrust, torque and cost.
* `RealAntennas`_ adds the link budget and targeting to :type:`krpc_SpaceCenter_Antenna_t`,
  and data rates to :type:`krpc_SpaceCenter_CommLink_t`.
* The :doc:`RemoteTech service <remote-tech>` covers RemoteTech antennas.

These services cover other mods:

* :doc:`Kerbal Alarm Clock <kerbal-alarm-clock>`
* :doc:`RemoteTech <remote-tech>`
* :doc:`Infernal Robotics Next <infernal-robotics>`
* :doc:`Docking Camera (KURS) <docking-camera>`
