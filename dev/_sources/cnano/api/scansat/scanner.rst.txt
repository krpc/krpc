Scanner
=======


.. type:: krpc_ScanSat_Scanner_t

   A SCANsat scanner on a part. Obtained by calling :func:`krpc_ScanSat_Scanner` or
   :func:`krpc_ScanSat_Scanners`.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_ScanSat_Scanner_Part(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_SpaceCenter_Part_t * returnValue, krpc_ScanSat_Scanner_t instance)

      The part containing this scanner.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_ScanSat_Scanner_ScanTypes(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_ScanSat_ScanType_t * returnValue, krpc_ScanSat_Scanner_t instance)

      The scan types of the scanner.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_ScanSat_Scanner_Active(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_ScanSat_Scanner_t instance)
   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_ScanSat_Scanner_set_Active(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_ScanSat_Scanner_t instance, bool value)

      Returns whether the scanner is switched on, and switches it on or off, as the buttons
      of the part do.

      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         A resource scanner only switches on when :func:`krpc_ScanSat_InstantResourceScan`
         is off, or :func:`krpc_ScanSat_StockResourceScan` is off.

      The field of view of the scanner at its best altitude, in degrees.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

      The lowest altitude the scanner scans at, in meters.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_ScanSat_Scanner_BestAltitude(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_ScanSat_Scanner_t instance)

      The lowest altitude for the full field of view, in meters.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_ScanSat_Scanner_MaxAltitude(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_ScanSat_Scanner_t instance)

      The highest altitude the scanner scans at, in meters.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_ScanSat_Scanner_RequiresLight(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_ScanSat_Scanner_t instance)

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_ScanSat_Scanner_State(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_ScanSat_ScannerState_t * returnValue, krpc_ScanSat_Scanner_t instance)

      The state of the scanner.

      :Game Scenes: Flight




.. type:: krpc_ScanSat_ScannerState_t

   The state of a scanner. See :func:`krpc_ScanSat_Scanner_State`.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_SCANNERSTATE_OFF

      Switched off.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_SCANNERSTATE_OUTOFRANGE

      Switched on, and outside its minimum and maximum altitudes.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_SCANNERSTATE_INDARKNESS

      Switched on, and paused as it needs light and the ground below it is in darkness.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_SCANNERSTATE_INRANGE

      Scanning between its minimum and best altitudes, with a reduced field of view.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_SCANNERSTATE_INBESTRANGE

      Scanning between its best and maximum altitudes, with its full field of view.
