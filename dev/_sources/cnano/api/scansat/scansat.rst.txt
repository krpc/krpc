ScanSat
=======


This service provides the scan data, scanners and maps of
`SCANsat <https://github.com/KSPModStewards/SCANsat>`_.

.. note::

   The readouts at a position return ``nullptr`` where the scans they need have not
   covered it, as the maps of the mod show nothing there.



.. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_ScanSat_Available(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue)

   Whether SCANsat is available.

   




.. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_ScanSat_Coverage(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_SpaceCenter_CelestialBody_t body, krpc_ScanSat_ScanType_t type)

   The fraction of a body covered by scan types, between 0 and 1. For a combination,
   the mean coverage of its scan types, so 1 only once every one of them is complete.
   SCANsat reports at most 0.999 until the coverage is complete.
   Throws an exception if no scan types are given.

   :Parameters:

    * **body** -- The body.
    * **type** -- The scan types.

   :Game Scenes: Space Center, Flight, Tracking Station





.. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_ScanSat_IsCovered(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_SpaceCenter_CelestialBody_t body, double latitude, double longitude, krpc_ScanSat_ScanType_t type)

   Whether at least one of the scan types covers a position on a body. For a
   combination, this is ``true`` where any one of its scan types covers the position,
   and not only where all of them do.
   Throws an exception if no scan types are given.

   :Parameters:

    * **body** -- The body.
    * **latitude** -- The latitude, in degrees.
    * **longitude** -- The longitude, in degrees.
    * **type** -- The scan types.

   :Game Scenes: Space Center, Flight, Tracking Station





.. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_ScanSat_Elevation(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, bool * returnValueIsNull, krpc_SpaceCenter_CelestialBody_t body, double latitude, double longitude)

   The height of the terrain relative to sea level at a position, in meters, or
   ``nullptr`` if no altimetry scan covers it. It is negative over the sea floor. With
   low resolution altimetry only, the height is rounded toward zero to a multiple of
   500 meters.

   :Parameters:

    * **body** -- The body.
    * **latitude** -- The latitude, in degrees.
    * **longitude** -- The longitude, in degrees.

   :Game Scenes: Space Center, Flight, Tracking Station





.. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_ScanSat_Slope(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, bool * returnValueIsNull, krpc_SpaceCenter_CelestialBody_t body, double latitude, double longitude)

   The terrain slope at a position, in degrees, or ``nullptr`` if high resolution
   altimetry does not cover it. The slope is averaged over points 5 meters apart.

   :Parameters:

    * **body** -- The body.
    * **latitude** -- The latitude, in degrees.
    * **longitude** -- The longitude, in degrees.

   :Game Scenes: Space Center, Flight, Tracking Station





.. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_ScanSat_Biome(krpc_connection_t connection, char * * returnValue, bool * returnValueIsNull, krpc_SpaceCenter_CelestialBody_t body, double latitude, double longitude)

   The name of the biome at a position, or ``nullptr`` if the biome scan does not
   cover it.
   Throws an exception if the body has no biomes.

   :Parameters:

    * **body** -- The body.
    * **latitude** -- The latitude, in degrees.
    * **longitude** -- The longitude, in degrees.

   :Game Scenes: Space Center, Flight, Tracking Station





.. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_ScanSat_ResourceAbundance(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, bool * returnValueIsNull, krpc_SpaceCenter_CelestialBody_t body, double latitude, double longitude, const char * resource)

   The abundance of a resource at a position, between 0 and 1, or ``nullptr`` if no
   resource scan covers it. With the low resolution scan only, the abundance is rounded
   down to a multiple of 0.05.
   Throws an exception if SCANsat's resource scanning is off, or SCANsat does not map
   the resource.

   :Parameters:

    * **body** -- The body.
    * **latitude** -- The latitude, in degrees.
    * **longitude** -- The longitude, in degrees.
    * **resource** -- The name of the resource. See :func:`krpc_ScanSat_Resources`.

   :Game Scenes: Space Center, Flight, Tracking Station

.. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_ScanSat_Resources(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_string_t * returnValue)

   The names of the resources SCANsat maps. Empty if SCANsat's resource scanning
   is off.

   




.. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_ScanSat_Map(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_bytes_t * returnValue, krpc_SpaceCenter_CelestialBody_t body, krpc_ScanSat_MapType_t type, int32_t width, krpc_ScanSat_MapProjection_t projection, bool color, const char * resource, krpc_ScanSat_ImageFormat_t format, int32_t quality)

   A map of a body from the scan data, as an image. The map is half as high as it is
   wide. Building it takes several game frames.
   Throws an exception if SCANsat cannot render the map, as when its visual maps are
   switched off.

   :Parameters:

    * **body** -- The body.
    * **type** -- The type of map.
    * **width** -- The width of the image in pixels, between 360 and 1440.
    * **projection** -- The projection.
    * **color** -- Whether the map is in color, or in grayscale.
    * **resource** -- The name of a resource to overlay, or an empty string for none. See :func:`krpc_ScanSat_Resources`.
    * **format** -- The format of the image.
    * **quality** -- The quality of a JPEG image, from 1 to 100.

   :Game Scenes: Space Center, Flight, Tracking Station

.. type:: krpc_ScanSat_ScanType_t

   A set of scan types. Each scan type has its own coverage of a body.

   .. note::

      The values may be combined.   A flags enumeration. :std:ref:`cnano-client-flags` shows how to combine its values.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_SCANTYPE_NONE

      No scan types.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_SCANTYPE_ALTIMETRYLORES

      Low resolution altimetry, the terrain height in steps of 500 meters.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_SCANTYPE_ALTIMETRYHIRES

      High resolution altimetry, the terrain height and slope.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_SCANTYPE_VISUALLORES

      Low resolution visual imaging.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_SCANTYPE_VISUALHIRES

      High resolution visual imaging.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_SCANTYPE_BIOME

      Biomes.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_SCANTYPE_ANOMALY

      The positions of anomalies.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_SCANTYPE_ANOMALYDETAIL

      The positions and names of anomalies, from close range.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_SCANTYPE_RESOURCELORES

      Low resolution resource abundance, in steps of 5%.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_SCANTYPE_RESOURCEHIRES

      High resolution resource abundance.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_SCANTYPE_ALTIMETRY

      Both altimetry scans.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_SCANTYPE_VISUAL

      Both visual scans.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_SCANTYPE_ANOMALIES

      Both anomaly scans.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_SCANTYPE_RESOURCES

      Both resource scans.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_SCANTYPE_ALL

      Every scan type.




.. type:: krpc_ScanSat_Anomaly_t

   An anomaly found by either anomaly scan. See :func:`krpc_ScanSat_Anomalies`.


   .. member:: krpc_nullable_string_t name

      The name of the anomaly, or ``nullptr`` until the anomaly detail scan covers it.

   .. member:: double latitude

      The latitude of the anomaly, in degrees.

   .. member:: double longitude

      The longitude of the anomaly, in degrees.
