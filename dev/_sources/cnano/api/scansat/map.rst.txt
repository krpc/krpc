Map
===


.. type:: krpc_ScanSat_MapType_t

   The type of a map. See :func:`krpc_ScanSat_Map`.

   .. macro:: KRPC_ENUMERATION_SCANSAT_MAPTYPE

      The service and enumeration ids of the enumeration, for
      :func:`krpc_KRPC_Type_EnumerationTypeById` and :func:`krpc_KRPC_Expression_ConstantEnumById`.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_MAPTYPE_ALTIMETRY

      Terrain height, from the altimetry scans.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_MAPTYPE_SLOPE

      Terrain slope, from the altimetry scans.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_MAPTYPE_BIOME

      Biomes, from the biome scan, over the terrain height.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_MAPTYPE_VISUAL

      The surface as seen from orbit, from the visual scans.




.. type:: krpc_ScanSat_MapProjection_t

   The projection of a map. See :func:`krpc_ScanSat_Map`.

   .. macro:: KRPC_ENUMERATION_SCANSAT_MAPPROJECTION

      The service and enumeration ids of the enumeration, for
      :func:`krpc_KRPC_Type_EnumerationTypeById` and :func:`krpc_KRPC_Expression_ConstantEnumById`.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_MAPPROJECTION_RECTANGULAR

      Equirectangular: longitude and latitude map linearly to the horizontal and vertical.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_MAPPROJECTION_KAVRAYSKIYVII

      Kavrayskiy VII, a compromise projection with less distortion at the poles.


   .. macro:: KRPC_SCANSAT_MAPPROJECTION_POLAR

      Two polar views side by side, the southern hemisphere on the left.
