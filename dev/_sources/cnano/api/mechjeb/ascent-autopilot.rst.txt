AscentAutopilot
===============


.. type:: krpc_MechJeb_AscentAutopilot_t

   The ascent autopilot.

   .. note::

      See the `MechJeb wiki <https://github.com/MuMech/MechJeb2/wiki/Ascent-Guidance>`_
      for how to set up this autopilot.

      ascent path, and disabling it disengages the autopilots of both paths.

.. type:: krpc_MechJeb_AscentLaunchMode_t

   The launch mode of the ascent autopilot. See :func:`krpc_MechJeb_AscentAutopilot_LaunchMode`.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_ASCENTLAUNCHMODE_NORMAL

      The autopilot is not performing a timed launch.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_ASCENTLAUNCHMODE_RENDEZVOUS

      A timed launch to rendezvous with the target vessel.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_ASCENTLAUNCHMODE_TARGETPLANE

      A timed launch into the plane of the target.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_ASCENTLAUNCHMODE_UNKNOWN

      A timed launch of another kind, started from MechJeb's window.
