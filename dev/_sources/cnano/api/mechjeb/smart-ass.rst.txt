   The mode of the Smart A.S.S. window. See :func:`krpc_MechJeb_SmartASS_InterfaceMode`.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSINTERFACEMODE_ORBITAL

      The orbital modes.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSINTERFACEMODE_SURFACE

      The surface modes.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSINTERFACEMODE_TARGET

      The target modes.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSINTERFACEMODE_ADVANCED

      The advanced mode.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSINTERFACEMODE_AUTOMATIC

.. type:: krpc_MechJeb_SmartASSAutopilotMode_t

   The autopilot mode of Smart A.S.S. See :func:`krpc_MechJeb_SmartASS_AutopilotMode`.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSAUTOPILOTMODE_OFF

      Off.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSAUTOPILOTMODE_KILLROT

      Stop the vessel's rotation.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSAUTOPILOTMODE_NODE

      Point at the next maneuver node.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSAUTOPILOTMODE_ADVANCED

      Point in the direction set by :func:`krpc_MechJeb_SmartASS_AdvancedReference` and
      :func:`krpc_MechJeb_SmartASS_AdvancedDirection`.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSAUTOPILOTMODE_AUTOMATIC

      Set by MechJeb's autopilots. Cannot be set.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSAUTOPILOTMODE_PROGRADE

      Point orbital prograde.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSAUTOPILOTMODE_RETROGRADE

      Point orbital retrograde.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSAUTOPILOTMODE_NORMALPLUS

      Point orbit normal.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSAUTOPILOTMODE_NORMALMINUS

      Point orbit anti-normal.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSAUTOPILOTMODE_RADIALPLUS

      Point radially outward.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSAUTOPILOTMODE_RADIALMINUS

      Point radially inward.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSAUTOPILOTMODE_SURFACEPROGRADE

      Point along the surface velocity.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSAUTOPILOTMODE_SURFACERETROGRADE

      Point against the surface velocity.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSAUTOPILOTMODE_HORIZONTALPLUS

      Point along the horizontal surface velocity.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSAUTOPILOTMODE_HORIZONTALMINUS

      Point against the horizontal surface velocity.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSAUTOPILOTMODE_SURFACE

      Point in the direction set by :func:`krpc_MechJeb_SmartASS_SurfaceHeading`,
      :func:`krpc_MechJeb_SmartASS_SurfacePitch` and :func:`krpc_MechJeb_SmartASS_SurfaceRoll`.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSAUTOPILOTMODE_VERTICALPLUS

      Point up, normal to the surface.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSAUTOPILOTMODE_TARGETPLUS

      Point at the target.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSAUTOPILOTMODE_TARGETMINUS

      Point away from the target.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSAUTOPILOTMODE_RELATIVEPLUS

      Point along the velocity relative to the target.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSAUTOPILOTMODE_RELATIVEMINUS

      Point against the velocity relative to the target.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSAUTOPILOTMODE_PARALLELPLUS

      Point parallel to the target's orientation. For a docking port, along its axis
      and away from it.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_SMARTASSAUTOPILOTMODE_PARALLELMINUS

      Point antiparallel to the target's orientation. For a docking port, along its axis
      and toward it.



.. type:: krpc_MechJeb_AttitudeReference_t

   A reference frame of Smart A.S.S. See :func:`krpc_MechJeb_SmartASS_AdvancedReference`.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_ATTITUDEREFERENCE_INERTIAL

      The world coordinate system.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_ATTITUDEREFERENCE_ORBIT

      Forward is prograde, left is normal and up is radial out.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_ATTITUDEREFERENCE_ORBITHORIZONTAL

      Forward is the orbital velocity projected onto the surface, and up is the surface normal.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_ATTITUDEREFERENCE_SURFACENORTH

      Forward is north, left is west and up is the surface normal.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_ATTITUDEREFERENCE_SURFACEVELOCITY

      Forward is the surface velocity, and up is the surface normal perpendicular to it.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_ATTITUDEREFERENCE_TARGET

      Forward is toward the target, and up is the vessel's heading perpendicular to it.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_ATTITUDEREFERENCE_RELATIVEVELOCITY

      Forward is the velocity relative to the target.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_ATTITUDEREFERENCE_TARGETORIENTATION

      Forward is the direction the target faces, and up is the target's up.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_ATTITUDEREFERENCE_MANEUVERNODE

      Forward is the burn direction of the next maneuver node.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_ATTITUDEREFERENCE_SUN

      Forward is the orbital velocity of the body that orbits the sun, and up is radial
      out of that orbit.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_ATTITUDEREFERENCE_SURFACEHORIZONTAL

      Forward is the horizontal surface velocity, and up is the surface normal.
