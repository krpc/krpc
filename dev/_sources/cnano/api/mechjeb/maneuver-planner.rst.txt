.. macro:: KRPC_EXCEPTION_MECHJEB_OPERATIONEXCEPTION

   Thrown when an operation cannot make its nodes, such as when it needs a target and
   there is none.

   The service and exception ids of the exception, for :macro:`KRPC_IS_EXCEPTION`. A call
   that fails with it returns :macro:`KRPC_ERROR_SERVER`.

      At the next apoapsis.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_TIMEREFERENCE_CLOSESTAPPROACH

   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_TIMEREFERENCE_COMPUTED

   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_TIMEREFERENCE_EQASCENDING

      At the equatorial ascending node.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_TIMEREFERENCE_EQDESCENDING

      At the equatorial descending node.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_TIMEREFERENCE_EQHIGHESTAD

      At the cheaper of the equatorial ascending and descending nodes.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_TIMEREFERENCE_EQNEARESTAD

      At the nearer of the equatorial ascending and descending nodes.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_TIMEREFERENCE_PERIAPSIS

      At the next periapsis.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_TIMEREFERENCE_RELASCENDING

      At the next ascending node with the target.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_TIMEREFERENCE_RELDESCENDING

      At the next descending node with the target.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_TIMEREFERENCE_RELHIGHESTAD

      At the cheaper of the ascending and descending nodes with the target.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_TIMEREFERENCE_RELNEARESTAD

      At the nearer of the ascending and descending nodes with the target.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_TIMEREFERENCE_XFROMNOW

      After :func:`krpc_MechJeb_TimeSelector_LeadTime`.

^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^


.. type:: krpc_MechJeb_OperationCircularize_t

   Circularize the orbit.

   .. note::

      To raise the periapsis to the apoapsis, set the time to :macro:`KRPC_MECHJEB_TIMEREFERENCE_APOAPSIS`, and to lower the apoapsis to the periapsis, set it to :macro:`KRPC_MECHJEB_TIMEREFERENCE_PERIAPSIS`. These are the cheapest.
