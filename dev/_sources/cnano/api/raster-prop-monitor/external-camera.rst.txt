External Camera
===============


.. type:: krpc_RasterPropMonitor_ExternalCamera_t

   A Raster Prop Monitor external camera: a part that the MFD camera pages show, such as
   the JSI External Radial-mount Camera. Obtained by calling
   :func:`krpc_RasterPropMonitor_Cameras`.

   .. macro:: KRPC_CLASS_RASTERPROPMONITOR_EXTERNALCAMERA

      The service and class ids of the class, for :func:`krpc_KRPC_Type_ClassTypeById`.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_RasterPropMonitor_ExternalCamera_Part(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_SpaceCenter_Part_t * returnValue, krpc_RasterPropMonitor_ExternalCamera_t instance)

      The part the camera is on.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_RasterPropMonitor_ExternalCamera_Name(krpc_connection_t connection, char * * returnValue, krpc_RasterPropMonitor_ExternalCamera_t instance)

      The camera's identifier, e.g. ``ExtCam1``, which the MFD camera pages select it
      by. The player sets the number in the part's menu.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_RasterPropMonitor_ExternalCamera_MinFoV(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_RasterPropMonitor_ExternalCamera_t instance)

      The smallest field of view of the camera, in degrees.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_RasterPropMonitor_ExternalCamera_MaxFoV(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_RasterPropMonitor_ExternalCamera_t instance)

      The largest field of view of the camera, in degrees.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_RasterPropMonitor_ExternalCamera_FoV(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_RasterPropMonitor_ExternalCamera_t instance)
   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_RasterPropMonitor_ExternalCamera_set_FoV(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_RasterPropMonitor_ExternalCamera_t instance, float value)

      The field of view that :func:`krpc_RasterPropMonitor_ExternalCamera_Image` renders at, in degrees. It starts at
      :func:`krpc_RasterPropMonitor_ExternalCamera_MaxFoV`, as on an MFD camera page, and a value set is clamped
      to the camera's range.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_RasterPropMonitor_ExternalCamera_Image(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_bytes_t * returnValue, krpc_RasterPropMonitor_ExternalCamera_t instance, int32_t width, int32_t height, krpc_RasterPropMonitor_ImageFormat_t format, int32_t quality)

      The camera's view as an image, rendered now. Streamed at a rate, it is a video of
      the camera, for which JPEG is smaller and faster.
      Throws an exception if the camera is not on the active vessel.

      :Parameters:

       * **width** -- The width of the image, in pixels.
       * **height** -- The height of the image, in pixels.
       * **format** -- The format of the image.
       * **quality** -- The quality of a JPEG image, from 1 to 100.

      :returns: The image data.
      :Game Scenes: Flight
