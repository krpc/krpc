Camera
======


.. _http-DockingCamera.Camera:

.. rubric:: Camera

A camera from Docking Camera (KURS), on an onboard camera part or a docking port.

A ``Camera`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.


.. http:get:: /docking-camera/camera/(id)/part

   Get the part containing this camera.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Camera <http-DockingCamera.Camera>` object.

   :resjson SpaceCenter.Part value:




.. http:post:: /docking-camera/camera/(id)/image

   The image from the camera, 256 by 256 pixels.
   Throws an exception if the camera is off or not started.

   .. note::

      The camera is switched on by setting the ``IsEnabled`` field of its
      ``PartCameraModule`` or ``DockingCameraModule`` to ``true``, which also opens
      the camera window.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Camera <http-DockingCamera.Camera>` object.

   :reqjson StdLib.ImageFormat format: The format of the image. Optional, defaults to ``"png"``.
   :reqjson int32 quality: The quality of a JPEG image, from 1 to 100. Optional, defaults to ``75``.
   :resjson bytes value: The image data.
