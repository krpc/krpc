Supported Mods
--------------

The SpaceCenter service supports the following mods. None of them needs to be installed, but
some functionality requires one of them, and the docs note where it does.
A property gives whether each one is installed, such as :func:`far_available`.

* `Ferram Aerospace Research`_: :class:`Flight` works with the stock
  aerodynamics or with Ferram Aerospace Research, and reads from whichever is in use. Some
  members require it, such as :func:`Flight::lift_coefficient`, and
  :func:`Part::lift` and :func:`Part::drag` are not
  available with it.
* `Extended Action Groups`_ adds action groups 10 to 250 to :class:`Control`.
* `RealFuels`_ adds ignitions, ullage and feed pressure to :class:`Engine`,
  and tank pressure and boil-off to :class:`Part`.
* :class:`Parachute` objects support `RealChute`_.
* :class:`Fairing` objects support `ProceduralFairings`_.
* :class:`SolarPanel` objects support the curved solar panels of
  `Near Future Solar`_.
* :class:`Experiment` objects support DMagic's
  `DMModuleScienceAnimateGeneric`_.
* :func:`Engine::thrust_reversed` supports the thrust reversers of
  `Firespitter`_ and `KerbalActuators`_.
* `KSPCommunityPartModules`_ 0.5 or later, or `kOS`_, provides the name tag in
  :func:`Part::tag`.
* `B9PartSwitch`_ switches a part's subtype when its field is set through
  :class:`PartField`.
* `TweakScale Rescaled`_ parts report their scaled masses, resources, thrust, torque and cost.
* `RealAntennas`_ adds the link budget and targeting to :class:`Antenna`,
  and data rates to :class:`CommLink`.
* The :doc:`RemoteTech service <remote-tech>` covers RemoteTech antennas.
