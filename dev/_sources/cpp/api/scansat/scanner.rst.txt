Scanner
=======


.. class:: Scanner

   A SCANsat scanner on a part. Obtained by calling :func:`scanner` or
   :func:`scanners`.

   .. function:: SpaceCenter::Part part()

      The part containing this scanner.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: ScanType scan_types()

      The scan types of the scanner.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: bool active()
   .. function:: void set_active(bool value)

      Returns whether the scanner is switched on, and switches it on or off, as the buttons
      of the part do.

      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         A resource scanner only switches on when :func:`instant_resource_scan`
         is off, or :func:`stock_resource_scan` is off.

   .. function:: float field_of_view()

      The field of view of the scanner at its best altitude, in degrees.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float min_altitude()

      The lowest altitude the scanner scans at, in meters.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float best_altitude()

      The lowest altitude for the full field of view, in meters.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float max_altitude()

      The highest altitude the scanner scans at, in meters.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: bool requires_light()

      Whether the scanner only scans in daylight, when :func:`daylight_check`
      is on.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: ScannerState state()

      The state of the scanner.

      :Game Scenes: Flight




.. namespace:: krpc::services::ScanSat
.. enum-struct:: ScannerState

   The state of a scanner. See :func:`Scanner::state`.


   .. enumerator:: off

      Switched off.


   .. enumerator:: out_of_range

      Switched on, and outside its minimum and maximum altitudes.


   .. enumerator:: in_darkness

      Switched on, and paused as it needs light and the ground below it is in darkness.


   .. enumerator:: in_range

      Scanning between its minimum and best altitudes, with a reduced field of view.


   .. enumerator:: in_best_range

      Scanning between its best and maximum altitudes, with its full field of view.
