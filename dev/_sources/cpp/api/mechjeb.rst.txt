MechJeb
=======

Provides functionality to interact with the `MechJeb`_ mod. |MechJeb tested|
Originally written by `Genhis <https://github.com/Genhis/KRPC.MechJeb>`_.
The service acts on the MechJeb of the active vessel, which needs a part with MechJeb. Its
classes follow MechJeb's windows: a setting is found under the window that shows it.

.. seealso::

   The service supports MechJeb's releases. For MechJeb's development builds, use
   `krpc-mechjeb-dev <https://krpc.github.io/krpc-mechjeb-dev/>`_, a best-effort replacement of
   this service, released on its own.
