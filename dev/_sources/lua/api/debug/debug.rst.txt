Debug
=====


.. module:: Debug

Provides functionality to modify the state of the simulation directly, in ways
the game normally does not allow: teleporting a vessel, setting its attitude,
applying forces to its parts, refilling its tanks and enabling the game's cheat
options.

.. note::

   Everything in this service is cheating. It has the same effect on a save as the
   game's own debug menu (Alt+F12), and the same consequences: contracts, records and
   career progress made with it are not earned. It is intended for setting up a
   scenario to fly or a script to test, without flying there first.



.. staticmethod:: set_circular_orbit(body: SpaceCenter.CelestialBody, altitude: number)

   Move the active vessel onto a circular orbit around a body.

   :param body: Body to orbit.
   :param altitude: Altitude of the orbit, in meters above sea level.




.. staticmethod:: set_orbit(body: SpaceCenter.CelestialBody, semi_major_axis: number, eccentricity: number, inclination: number, longitude_of_ascending_node: number, argument_of_periapsis: number, mean_anomaly_at_epoch: number, epoch: number)

   Move the active vessel onto the orbit with the given orbital elements. The angles use
   the same units and conventions as :class:`SpaceCenter.Orbit`.

   :param body: Body to orbit.
   :param semi_major_axis: Semi-major axis of the orbit, in meters.
   :param eccentricity: Eccentricity of the orbit.
   :param inclination: Inclination of the orbit, in radians.
   :param longitude_of_ascending_node: Longitude of the ascending node, in radians.
   :param argument_of_periapsis: Argument of periapsis, in radians.
   :param mean_anomaly_at_epoch: Mean anomaly at epoch, in radians.
   :param epoch: Universal time of the epoch, in seconds.




.. staticmethod:: set_landed(body: SpaceCenter.CelestialBody, latitude: number, longitude: number, altitude?: number)

   Place the active vessel on the surface of a body at the given latitude and longitude,
   and wait for it to come to rest.

   :param body: Body to land on.
   :param latitude: Latitude in degrees.
   :param longitude: Longitude in degrees.
   :param altitude: Height above the terrain to settle at, in meters. Defaults to 0, resting on the surface. Defaults to ``0.0``.




.. staticmethod:: set_flight(body: SpaceCenter.CelestialBody, latitude: number, longitude: number, altitude: number, speed: number, heading: number, pitch?: number, roll?: number, angle_of_attack?: number)

   Place the active vessel in atmospheric flight over the given point: at the given altitude
   and airspeed, pointing along the given heading at the given pitch and roll, and
   let physics resume so that it is flying. Use this to set up an in-air scenario
   without flying the craft up from the runway.

   :param body: Body to fly over.
   :param latitude: Latitude in degrees.
   :param longitude: Longitude in degrees.
   :param altitude: Altitude in meters above sea level.
   :param speed: Airspeed in :math:`\mathrm{m/s}`, relative to the surface.
   :param heading: Compass heading to point along, in degrees (90 is east).
   :param pitch: Pitch above the horizon, in degrees. Defaults to 0 (level). Defaults to ``0.0``.
   :param roll: Roll, in degrees. Defaults to 0 (wings level). Defaults to ``0.0``.
   :param angle_of_attack: Angle of attack in degrees: how far the airspeed vector sits below the nose, in the pitch plane. 0 (the default) puts the airspeed along the nose; a positive value gives a nose-up attitude relative to the flight path, so the flight-path angle is *pitch* minus this. Defaults to ``0.0``.

   .. note::

      The pitch, heading and roll match those reported by the vessel's
      :class:`SpaceCenter.Flight`.




.. staticmethod:: set_position(position: Tuple, reference_frame?: SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame | Types.none)

   Move the active vessel to the given position, leaving its velocity as it is. To put it
   alongside another vessel, pass a position in that vessel's reference frame.

   :param position: Position of the vessel's center of mass, in meters.
   :param reference_frame: Reference frame the position is in. Defaults to the reference frame of the body being orbited.

   .. note::

      This is the inverse of :meth:`SpaceCenter.Vessel.position` in the same
      reference frame.




.. staticmethod:: set_velocity(velocity: Tuple, reference_frame?: SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame | Types.none)

   Set the velocity of the active vessel, leaving it where it is. The vessel is moved onto the
   orbit that passes through its current position at the new velocity, so this works
   in orbit and in the atmosphere alike.

   :param velocity: Velocity in :math:`\mathrm{m/s}`.
   :param reference_frame: Reference frame the velocity is in. Defaults to the reference frame of the body being orbited, which rotates with it, so a zero velocity leaves the vessel at rest relative to the ground.

   .. note::

      This is the inverse of :meth:`SpaceCenter.Vessel.velocity` in the same
      reference frame.




.. staticmethod:: set_pitch_heading_roll(pitch: number, heading: number, roll: number, reference_frame?: SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame | Types.none, vessel?: SpaceCenter.Vessel | Types.none)

   Set the attitude of a vessel to the given pitch, heading and roll in the given
   reference frame, and stop it rotating. Lets a script start from a fixed, still
   pose without first flying the autopilot there.

   :param pitch: Target pitch, in degrees.
   :param heading: Target heading, in degrees.
   :param roll: Target roll, in degrees.
   :param reference_frame: Reference frame the angles are in. Defaults to the vessel's surface reference frame.
   :param vessel: Vessel to rotate. Defaults to the active vessel.

   .. note::

      The angles match those reported by the vessel's
      :class:`SpaceCenter.Flight`.




.. staticmethod:: set_direction(direction: Tuple, roll: number, reference_frame?: SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame | Types.none, vessel?: SpaceCenter.Vessel | Types.none)

   Point a vessel along the given direction in the given reference frame, and stop it
   rotating. This is :meth:`Debug.set_pitch_heading_roll` with a pointing vector instead
   of a pitch and heading, matching the attitude the autopilot holds for the same
   target direction and roll.

   :param direction: Direction to point the nose of the vessel in.
   :param roll: Target roll, in degrees, or ``NaN`` to leave the roll uncontrolled.
   :param reference_frame: Reference frame the direction and roll are in. Defaults to the vessel's surface reference frame.
   :param vessel: Vessel to rotate. Defaults to the active vessel.




.. staticmethod:: apply_rotation(angle: number, axis: Tuple, vessel?: SpaceCenter.Vessel | Types.none)

   Rotate a vessel about the given axis, in its own reference frame, by the given angle.

   :param angle: Angle to rotate by, in degrees.
   :param axis: Axis to rotate about.
   :param vessel: Vessel to rotate. Defaults to the active vessel.




.. staticmethod:: set_angular_velocity(angular_velocity: Tuple, reference_frame?: SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame | Types.none, vessel?: SpaceCenter.Vessel | Types.none)

   Set the angular velocity of a vessel, expressed in the given reference frame. Pass a
   zero vector to stop the vessel rotating.

   :param angular_velocity: Angular velocity, in :math:`\mathrm{rad/s}`. The direction is the axis of rotation and the magnitude is the rate.
   :param reference_frame: Reference frame the angular velocity is in. Defaults to the vessel's surface reference frame.
   :param vessel: Vessel to rotate. Defaults to the active vessel.

   .. note::

      The whole vessel is put into a rigid rotation about its center of mass, so it spins in
      place rather than shearing apart or translating.




.. staticmethod:: add_force(part: SpaceCenter.Part, force: Tuple, position: Tuple, reference_frame: SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame): SpaceCenter.Force

   Exert a constant force on a part, acting at the given position.

   :param part: The part to apply the force to.
   :param force: A vector pointing in the direction that the force acts, with its magnitude equal to the strength of the force in Newtons.
   :param position: The position at which the force acts, as a vector.
   :param reference_frame: The reference frame that the force and position are in.
   :returns: An object that can be used to remove or modify the force.

   .. note::

      The force is removed when the client that added it disconnects.




.. staticmethod:: instantaneous_force(part: SpaceCenter.Part, force: Tuple, position: Tuple, reference_frame: SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame)

   Exert an instantaneous force on a part, acting at the given position.

   :param part: The part to apply the force to.
   :param force: A vector pointing in the direction that the force acts, with its magnitude equal to the strength of the force in Newtons.
   :param position: The position at which the force acts, as a vector.
   :param reference_frame: The reference frame that the force and position are in.

   .. note::

      The force is applied instantaneously in a single physics update.




.. staticmethod:: fill_all_resources(vessel?: SpaceCenter.Vessel | Types.none)

   Fill every resource tank on a vessel to its maximum capacity.

   :param vessel: Vessel to fill. Defaults to the active vessel.




.. staticmethod:: fill_resources(resource_name: string, vessel?: SpaceCenter.Vessel | Types.none)

   Fill the tanks of a single resource on a vessel to their maximum capacity.

   :param resource_name: Name of the resource to fill, for example ``"LiquidFuel"``.
   :param vessel: Vessel to fill. Defaults to the active vessel.

   :Attribute: Can be read or written

   Whether parts survive collisions that would otherwise destroy them.

   :Attribute: Can be read or written




.. attribute:: unbreakable_joints: boolean

   Whether the joints between parts are rigid and cannot break.

   :Attribute: Can be read or written




.. attribute:: ignore_max_temperature: boolean

   Whether parts survive temperatures above their maximum, instead of exploding.

   :Attribute: Can be read or written




.. attribute:: allow_part_clipping: boolean

   Whether parts may be placed so that they intersect one another in the editor.

   :Attribute: Can be read or written




.. attribute:: non_strict_attachment_orientation: boolean

   Whether parts may be attached in any orientation, rather than only the ones their
   attachment nodes allow.

   :Attribute: Can be read or written




.. attribute:: ignore_kerbal_inventory_limits: boolean

   Whether a kerbal's inventory may hold more than its slot and mass limits allow.

   :Attribute: Can be read or written




.. attribute:: ignore_eva_construction_mass_limit: boolean

   Whether EVA construction may move parts heavier than a kerbal can normally carry.

   :Attribute: Can be read or written




.. attribute:: ignore_agency_mindset_on_contracts: boolean

   Whether contracts are offered regardless of the agency mindset that normally
   restricts which ones appear.

   :Attribute: Can be read or written




.. attribute:: pause_on_vessel_unpack: boolean

   Whether the game pauses whenever a vessel comes off rails.

   :Attribute: Can be read or written




.. attribute:: biomes_visible: boolean

   Whether biome boundaries are drawn on the surface of bodies.

   :Attribute: Can be read or written




.. attribute:: gravity_multiplier: number

   Multiplier applied to the force of gravity on every vessel. 1 is normal gravity,
   0 is weightlessness.

   :Attribute: Can be read or written




.. attribute:: funds: number

   The number of funds the player has. Only available in career mode.

   :Attribute: Can be read or written




.. attribute:: science: number

   The amount of science the player has. Only available in career and science mode.

   :Attribute: Can be read or written




.. attribute:: reputation: number

   The player's reputation. Only available in career mode.

   :Attribute: Can be read or written




.. staticmethod:: unlock_technology_tree()

   Research every node in the technology tree and purchase every part in it.
   Only available in career and science mode.





.. staticmethod:: upgrade_facilities()

   Upgrade every building at the space center to its highest level.
   Only available in career mode.





.. staticmethod:: max_kerbal_experience()

   Raise every kerbal in the roster to the highest experience level.
