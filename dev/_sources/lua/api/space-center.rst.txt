Supported Mods
--------------

The SpaceCenter service supports the following mods. None of them needs to be installed, but
some functionality requires one of them, and the docs note where it does.
A property gives whether each one is installed, such as :attr:`SpaceCenter.far_available`.

* `Ferram Aerospace Research`_: :class:`SpaceCenter.Flight` works with the stock
  aerodynamics or with Ferram Aerospace Research, and reads from whichever is in use. Some
  members require it, such as :attr:`SpaceCenter.Flight.lift_coefficient`, and
  :meth:`SpaceCenter.Part.lift` and :meth:`SpaceCenter.Part.drag` are not
  available with it.
* `Extended Action Groups`_ adds action groups 10 to 250 to :class:`SpaceCenter.Control`.
* `RealFuels`_ adds ignitions, ullage and feed pressure to :class:`SpaceCenter.Engine`,
  and tank pressure and boil-off to :class:`SpaceCenter.Part`.
* :class:`SpaceCenter.Parachute` objects support `RealChute`_.
* :class:`SpaceCenter.Fairing` objects support `ProceduralFairings`_.
* :class:`SpaceCenter.SolarPanel` objects support the curved solar panels of
  `Near Future Solar`_.
* :class:`SpaceCenter.Experiment` objects support DMagic's
  `DMModuleScienceAnimateGeneric`_.
* :attr:`SpaceCenter.Engine.thrust_reversed` supports the thrust reversers of
  `Firespitter`_ and `KerbalActuators`_.
* `KSPCommunityPartModules`_ 0.5 or later, or `kOS`_, provides the name tag in
  :attr:`SpaceCenter.Part.tag`.
* `B9PartSwitch`_ switches a part's subtype when its field is set through
  :class:`SpaceCenter.PartField`.
* `TweakScale Rescaled`_ parts report their scaled masses, resources, thrust, torque and cost.
* `RealAntennas`_ adds the link budget and targeting to :class:`SpaceCenter.Antenna`,
  and data rates to :class:`SpaceCenter.CommLink`.
* The :doc:`RemoteTech service <remote-tech>` covers RemoteTech antennas.
