Boats and Submarines#

A hull floats when the water it pushes aside weighs more than the hull does. Flight.displacement is the volume of water the vessel pushes aside when it is fully submerged, and Flight.buoyant_force_at() is the force that volume pushes back with:

body = vessel.orbit.body
flight = vessel.flight()
displacement = flight.displacement
buoyancy = flight.buoyant_force_at(body.ocean_density, body.surface_gravity)

var body = vessel.Orbit.Body;
var flight = vessel.Flight ();
var displacement = flight.Displacement;
var buoyancy = flight.BuoyantForceAt (
    body.OceanDensity, body.SurfaceGravity);

auto body = vessel.orbit().body();
auto flight = vessel.flight();
auto displacement = flight.displacement();
auto buoyancy =
    flight.buoyant_force_at(body.ocean_density(), body.surface_gravity());

krpc_SpaceCenter_Orbit_t orbit;
krpc_SpaceCenter_Vessel_Orbit(conn, &orbit, vessel);
krpc_SpaceCenter_CelestialBody_t body;

krpc_SpaceCenter_Flight_t flight;
krpc_SpaceCenter_Vessel_Flight(conn, &flight, vessel, KRPC_NULL);

double density, gravity, buoyancy;
krpc_SpaceCenter_CelestialBody_OceanDensity(conn, &density, body);
krpc_SpaceCenter_CelestialBody_SurfaceGravity(conn, &gravity, body);
krpc_SpaceCenter_Flight_BuoyantForceAt(conn, &buoyancy, flight, density,
                                       gravity);

    body.getOceanDensity(), body.getSurfaceGravity());

  flight:buoyant_force_at(body.ocean_density, body.surface_gravity)

const flight = await vessel.flight();
const displacement = await flight.getDisplacement();
const buoyancy = await flight.buoyantForceAt(
  await body.getOceanDensity(),
  await body.getSurfaceGravity(),
);

Compare that force with the vessel’s weight. A hull with more buoyancy than its weight floats. A submarine trims its tanks to move between the two. Part.displacement and Part.buoyant_force_at() give the same figures for one part.

Note

Flight.buoyant_force_at() applies SpaceCenter.buoyancy_scalar, which is 1.2 in stock KSP. A buoyant force in the game is a fifth larger than Archimedes’ principle alone gives.

Trim#

A hull at rest carries its center of buoyancy on the same vertical line as its center of mass. A horizontal offset between the two turns the weight and the buoyancy into a couple that rolls or pitches the hull. That offset is the trim.

Read both in the vessel’s surface reference frame. Its x axis points up, so the trim is what the other two axes carry:

flight = vessel.flight(vessel.surface_reference_frame)
center_of_buoyancy = flight.center_of_buoyancy
center_of_mass = flight.center_of_mass

var flight = vessel.Flight (vessel.SurfaceReferenceFrame);
var centerOfBuoyancy = flight.CenterOfBuoyancy;
var centerOfMass = flight.CenterOfMass;

auto flight = vessel.flight(vessel.surface_reference_frame());
auto center_of_buoyancy = flight.center_of_buoyancy();
auto center_of_mass = flight.center_of_mass();

krpc_SpaceCenter_Vessel_SurfaceReferenceFrame(conn, &frame, vessel);
krpc_SpaceCenter_Flight_t flight;
krpc_SpaceCenter_Vessel_Flight(conn, &flight, vessel, frame);

bool out_of_water;
krpc_SpaceCenter_Flight_CenterOfBuoyancy(conn, &center_of_buoyancy,
                                         &out_of_water, flight);
krpc_SpaceCenter_Flight_CenterOfMass(conn, &center_of_mass, flight);

Flight.center_of_buoyancy is null when no part of the vessel is in the water, or the body has no ocean.

The couple with the hull fully submerged is also available as a torque about the center of mass. It is zero when the fully submerged center of buoyancy lies directly above or below the center of mass:

torque = flight.buoyant_torque_at(body.ocean_density, body.surface_gravity)

var torque = flight.BuoyantTorqueAt (
    body.OceanDensity, body.SurfaceGravity);

auto torque = flight.buoyant_torque_at(body.ocean_density(),
                                       body.surface_gravity());

  flight:buoyant_torque_at(body.ocean_density, body.surface_gravity)

  await body.getOceanDensity(),
  await body.getSurfaceGravity(),
);

Flight.buoyant_torque_at() is the torque with the hull fully submerged. It holds steady while the hull rides the waves, where Flight.buoyant_torque follows the live waterline.

In the Editor#

A hull can be trimmed before it is launched. EditorVessel.flight() returns a SimulatedFlight, which has the fully submerged members of Flight. Its SimulatedFlight.surface_reference_frame sets which way is up. Here a hull built nose-up in the vehicle assembly building is simulated floating on its bottom:

Gravity acts along the negative x axis of the surface reference frame. A frame created relative to EditorVessel.reference_frame follows the hull as it is edited.

Submersion#

Flight.submerged_portion is the fraction of the vessel below the waterline, and Flight.depth is how far its center of mass sits below the surface. The same pair is available per part, alongside Part.splashed and the depth of the part’s shallowest and deepest points.

Pressure#

Below the waterline, Flight.static_pressure and Part.static_pressure include the weight of the water. With part pressure limits enabled in the difficulty settings, a part is destroyed when its static plus dynamic pressure exceeds Part.max_pressure:

for part in vessel.parts.all:
    if part.static_pressure + part.dynamic_pressure > part.max_pressure:
        print(part.title, "is over its pressure limit")

foreach (var part in vessel.Parts.All)
    if (part.StaticPressure + part.DynamicPressure >
        part.MaxPressure)
        Console.WriteLine (
            "{0} is over its pressure limit", part.Title);

for (auto part : vessel.parts().all())
  if (part.static_pressure() + part.dynamic_pressure() >
      part.max_pressure())
    std::cout << part.title() << " is over its pressure limit"
              << std::endl;

krpc_list_object_t all = KRPC_NULL_LIST;
krpc_SpaceCenter_Parts_All(conn, &all, parts);
for (size_t i = 0; i < all.size; i++) {
  krpc_SpaceCenter_Part_t part = all.items[i];
  float static_pressure, dynamic_pressure;
  double max_pressure;
  krpc_SpaceCenter_Part_StaticPressure(conn, &static_pressure, part);
  krpc_SpaceCenter_Part_DynamicPressure(conn, &dynamic_pressure, part);
  krpc_SpaceCenter_Part_MaxPressure(conn, &max_pressure, part);
  if (static_pressure + dynamic_pressure > max_pressure) {
    char* title = NULL;
    krpc_SpaceCenter_Part_Title(conn, &title, part);
    printf("%s is over its pressure limit\n", title);
    krpc_free(title);
  }
}
KRPC_FREE_LIST(all);

for (Part part : vessel.getParts().getAll()) {
  if (part.getStaticPressure() + part.getDynamicPressure()
      > part.getMaxPressure()) {
    System.out.println(
        part.getTitle() + " is over its pressure limit");
  }
}

  if part.static_pressure + part.dynamic_pressure > part.max_pressure then
    print(part.title .. ' is over its pressure limit')
  end
end

    (await part.getStaticPressure()) + (await part.getDynamicPressure());
  if (pressure > (await part.getMaxPressure())) {
    console.log(await part.getTitle(), "is over its pressure limit");
  }

The loop reads the parts with separate calls, which can land in different physics ticks. A server side function reads every part in the same tick, with a single call:

over_limit = conn.run(
    lambda: [
        part.title
        for part in vessel.parts.all
        if part.static_pressure + part.dynamic_pressure > part.max_pressure
    ]
)

var overLimit = connection.Run (
    () => vessel.Parts.All
        .Where (part =>
            part.StaticPressure + part.DynamicPressure >
            part.MaxPressure)
        .Select (part => part.Title)
        .ToList ());

CelestialBody.pressure_at() takes a negative altitude for a point under the sea, to plan how deep a dive can go.

Example#

This script reports whether the active vessel floats, and how far its center of buoyancy sits from its center of mass:

import math

import krpc

conn = krpc.connect(name="Buoyancy Trim")
vessel = conn.space_center.active_vessel
body = vessel.orbit.body

if not body.has_ocean:
    raise RuntimeError(body.name + " has no ocean")

flight = vessel.flight(vessel.surface_reference_frame)

# The fully submerged figures, which do not depend on where the vessel is
buoyancy = flight.buoyant_force_at(body.ocean_density, body.surface_gravity)
torque = flight.buoyant_torque_at(body.ocean_density, body.surface_gravity)
weight = vessel.mass * body.surface_gravity

print("Displacement:  %.1f m^3" % flight.displacement)
print("Buoyancy:      %.0f N fully submerged" % buoyancy)
print("Weight:        %.0f N" % weight)
print("Floats:        %s" % str(buoyancy > weight).lower())
print("Torque:        %.0f N m fully submerged" % math.hypot(*torque))

# The trim, read in the surface reference frame. Its x axis points up, so
# the y and z axes span the horizontal. A horizontal offset between the two
# centers tips the hull until they line up again.
center_of_buoyancy = flight.center_of_buoyancy

if center_of_buoyancy is None:
    print("Trim:          vessel is out of the water")
else:
    center_of_mass = flight.center_of_mass
    north = center_of_buoyancy[1] - center_of_mass[1]
    east = center_of_buoyancy[2] - center_of_mass[2]
    print("Submerged:     %.0f%%" % (100 * flight.submerged_portion))
    print("Trim:          %.2f m horizontally" % math.hypot(north, east))
    print("Trim north:    %+.2f m" % north)
    print("Trim east:     %+.2f m" % east)

using System;
using KRPC.Client;
using KRPC.Client.Services.SpaceCenter;

class BuoyancyTrim {
    public static void Main () {
        using (var connection = new Connection ("Buoyancy Trim")) {
            var vessel = connection.SpaceCenter ().ActiveVessel;
            var body = vessel.Orbit.Body;

            if (!body.HasOcean)
                throw new InvalidOperationException (
                    body.Name + " has no ocean");

            var flight = vessel.Flight (vessel.SurfaceReferenceFrame);

            // The fully submerged figures, which do not depend on where
            // the vessel is
            var buoyancy = flight.BuoyantForceAt (
                body.OceanDensity, body.SurfaceGravity);
            var torque = flight.BuoyantTorqueAt (
                body.OceanDensity, body.SurfaceGravity);
            var weight = vessel.Mass * body.SurfaceGravity;
            var torqueSize = Math.Sqrt (torque.Item1 * torque.Item1 +
                                        torque.Item2 * torque.Item2 +
                                        torque.Item3 * torque.Item3);

            Console.WriteLine ("Displacement:  {0:F1} m^3",
                               flight.Displacement);
            Console.WriteLine ("Buoyancy:      {0:F0} N fully submerged",
                               buoyancy);
            Console.WriteLine ("Weight:        {0:F0} N", weight);
            Console.WriteLine ("Floats:        {0}",
                               buoyancy > weight ? "true" : "false");
            Console.WriteLine ("Torque:        {0:F0} N m fully submerged",
                               torqueSize);

            // The trim, read in the surface reference frame. Its x axis
            // points up, so the y and z axes span the horizontal. A
            // horizontal offset between the two centers tips the hull
            // until they line up again.
            var centerOfBuoyancy = flight.CenterOfBuoyancy;

            if (centerOfBuoyancy == null) {
                Console.WriteLine (
                    "Trim:          vessel is out of the water");
            } else {
                var centerOfMass = flight.CenterOfMass;
                var north = centerOfBuoyancy.Item2 - centerOfMass.Item2;
                var east = centerOfBuoyancy.Item3 - centerOfMass.Item3;
                Console.WriteLine ("Submerged:     {0:F0}%",
                                   100 * flight.SubmergedPortion);
                Console.WriteLine ("Trim:          {0:F2} m horizontally",
                                   Math.Sqrt (north * north + east * east));
                Console.WriteLine ("Trim north:    {0:+0.00;-0.00} m",
                                   north);
                Console.WriteLine ("Trim east:     {0:+0.00;-0.00} m",
                                   east);
            }
        }
    }
}

#include <cmath>
#include <cstdio>
#include <krpc.hpp>
#include <krpc/services/space_center.hpp>
#include <stdexcept>
#include <tuple>

int main() {
  auto conn = krpc::connect("Buoyancy Trim");
  krpc::services::SpaceCenter space_center(conn);
  auto vessel = space_center.active_vessel().value();
  auto body = vessel.orbit().body();

  if (!body.has_ocean())
    throw std::runtime_error(body.name() + " has no ocean");

  auto flight = vessel.flight(vessel.surface_reference_frame());

  // The fully submerged figures, which do not depend on where the vessel
  // is
  auto buoyancy =
      flight.buoyant_force_at(body.ocean_density(), body.surface_gravity());
  auto torque = flight.buoyant_torque_at(body.ocean_density(),
                                         body.surface_gravity());
  auto weight = vessel.mass() * body.surface_gravity();

  std::printf("Displacement:  %.1f m^3\n", flight.displacement());
  std::printf("Buoyancy:      %.0f N fully submerged\n", buoyancy);
  std::printf("Weight:        %.0f N\n", weight);
  std::printf("Floats:        %s\n", buoyancy > weight ? "true" : "false");
  std::printf("Torque:        %.0f N m fully submerged\n",
              std::hypot(std::get<0>(torque), std::get<1>(torque),
                         std::get<2>(torque)));

  // The trim, read in the surface reference frame. Its x axis points up,
  // so the y and z axes span the horizontal. A horizontal offset between
  // the two centers tips the hull until they line up again.
  auto center_of_buoyancy = flight.center_of_buoyancy();

  if (!center_of_buoyancy) {
    std::printf("Trim:          vessel is out of the water\n");
  } else {
    auto center_of_mass = flight.center_of_mass();
    auto north =
        std::get<1>(*center_of_buoyancy) - std::get<1>(center_of_mass);
    auto east =
        std::get<2>(*center_of_buoyancy) - std::get<2>(center_of_mass);
    std::printf("Submerged:     %.0f%%\n",
                100 * flight.submerged_portion());
    std::printf("Trim:          %.2f m horizontally\n",
                std::hypot(north, east));
    std::printf("Trim north:    %+.2f m\n", north);

#include <krpc_cnano.h>
#include <krpc_cnano/services/space_center.h>
#include <math.h>
#include <stdio.h>

int main() {
  krpc_connection_t conn;
  if (krpc_open_serial(&conn, "COM0") != KRPC_OK) return 1;
  if (krpc_connect(conn, "Buoyancy Trim") != KRPC_OK) return 1;

  krpc_SpaceCenter_Vessel_t vessel;
  krpc_SpaceCenter_ActiveVessel(conn, &vessel);
  krpc_SpaceCenter_Orbit_t orbit;
  krpc_SpaceCenter_Vessel_Orbit(conn, &orbit, vessel);
  krpc_SpaceCenter_CelestialBody_t body;
  krpc_SpaceCenter_Orbit_Body(conn, &body, orbit);

  bool has_ocean;
  krpc_SpaceCenter_CelestialBody_HasOcean(conn, &has_ocean, body);
  if (!has_ocean) {
    char* name = NULL;
    krpc_SpaceCenter_CelestialBody_Name(conn, &name, body);
    fprintf(stderr, "%s has no ocean\n", name);
    krpc_free(name);
    krpc_disconnect(conn);
    return 1;
  }

  krpc_SpaceCenter_ReferenceFrame_t frame;
  krpc_SpaceCenter_Vessel_SurfaceReferenceFrame(conn, &frame, vessel);
  krpc_SpaceCenter_Flight_t flight;
  krpc_SpaceCenter_Vessel_Flight(conn, &flight, vessel, frame);

  // The fully submerged figures, which do not depend on where the vessel
  // is
  double density, gravity, buoyancy, displacement;
  krpc_SpaceCenter_CelestialBody_OceanDensity(conn, &density, body);
  krpc_SpaceCenter_CelestialBody_SurfaceGravity(conn, &gravity, body);
  krpc_SpaceCenter_Flight_BuoyantForceAt(conn, &buoyancy, flight, density,
                                         gravity);
  krpc_tuple_double_double_double_t torque;
  krpc_SpaceCenter_Flight_BuoyantTorqueAt(conn, &torque, flight, density,
                                          gravity);
  float mass;
  krpc_SpaceCenter_Vessel_Mass(conn, &mass, vessel);
  double weight = mass * gravity;
  krpc_SpaceCenter_Flight_Displacement(conn, &displacement, flight);

  printf("Displacement:  %.1f m^3\n", displacement);
  printf("Buoyancy:      %.0f N fully submerged\n", buoyancy);
  printf("Weight:        %.0f N\n", weight);
  printf("Floats:        %s\n", buoyancy > weight ? "true" : "false");
  printf("Torque:        %.0f N m fully submerged\n",
         sqrt(torque.e0 * torque.e0 + torque.e1 * torque.e1 +
              torque.e2 * torque.e2));

  // The trim, read in the surface reference frame. Its x axis points up,
  // so the y and z axes span the horizontal. A horizontal offset between
  // the two centers tips the hull until they line up again.
  krpc_tuple_double_double_double_t center_of_buoyancy;
  bool out_of_water;
  krpc_SpaceCenter_Flight_CenterOfBuoyancy(conn, &center_of_buoyancy,
                                           &out_of_water, flight);

  if (out_of_water) {
    printf("Trim:          vessel is out of the water\n");
  } else {
    krpc_tuple_double_double_double_t center_of_mass;
    krpc_SpaceCenter_Flight_CenterOfMass(conn, &center_of_mass, flight);
    double north = center_of_buoyancy.e1 - center_of_mass.e1;
    double east = center_of_buoyancy.e2 - center_of_mass.e2;
    double submerged;
    krpc_SpaceCenter_Flight_SubmergedPortion(conn, &submerged, flight);
    printf("Submerged:     %.0f%%\n", 100 * submerged);
    printf("Trim:          %.2f m horizontally\n",
           sqrt(north * north + east * east));
    printf("Trim north:    %+.2f m\n", north);
    printf("Trim east:     %+.2f m\n", east);
  }

  krpc_disconnect(conn);
}

public class BuoyancyTrim {
  public static void main(String[] args) {
    try (Connection connection = Connection.newInstance("Buoyancy Trim")) {
      Vessel vessel =
          SpaceCenter.newInstance(connection).getActiveVessel();
      CelestialBody body = vessel.getOrbit().getBody();

      if (!body.getHasOcean()) {
        throw new IllegalStateException(body.getName() + " has no ocean");
      }

      Flight flight = vessel.flight(vessel.getSurfaceReferenceFrame());

      // The fully submerged figures, which do not depend on where the
      // vessel is
      double density = body.getOceanDensity();
      double gravity = body.getSurfaceGravity();
      double buoyancy = flight.buoyantForceAt(density, gravity);
      Triplet<Double, Double, Double> torque =
          flight.buoyantTorqueAt(density, gravity);
      double weight = vessel.getMass() * gravity;
      double torqueSize = Math.sqrt(
          torque.getValue0() * torque.getValue0()
          + torque.getValue1() * torque.getValue1()
          + torque.getValue2() * torque.getValue2());

      System.out.printf("Displacement:  %.1f m^3%n",
                        flight.getDisplacement());
      System.out.printf("Buoyancy:      %.0f N fully submerged%n",
                        buoyancy);
      System.out.printf("Weight:        %.0f N%n", weight);
      System.out.printf("Floats:        %s%n", buoyancy > weight);
      System.out.printf("Torque:        %.0f N m fully submerged%n",
                        torqueSize);

      // The trim, read in the surface reference frame. Its x axis points
      // up, so the y and z axes span the horizontal. A horizontal offset
      // between the two centers tips the hull until they line up again.
      Triplet<Double, Double, Double> centerOfBuoyancy =
          flight.getCenterOfBuoyancy();

      if (centerOfBuoyancy == null) {
        System.out.println("Trim:          vessel is out of the water");
      } else {
        Triplet<Double, Double, Double> centerOfMass =
            flight.getCenterOfMass();
        double north = centerOfBuoyancy.getValue1()
            - centerOfMass.getValue1();
        double east = centerOfBuoyancy.getValue2()
            - centerOfMass.getValue2();
        System.out.printf("Submerged:     %.0f%%%n",
                          100 * flight.getSubmergedPortion());
        System.out.printf("Trim:          %.2f m horizontally%n",
                          Math.hypot(north, east));
        System.out.printf("Trim north:    %+.2f m%n", north);
        System.out.printf("Trim east:     %+.2f m%n", east);
