Life Support#

service LifeSupportService#

How long the supplies of a vehicle, a habitat zone or a kitten’s suit last. Obtained from VehicleService.GetLifeSupport, HabitatZoneService.GetLifeSupport or KittenService.GetLifeSupport.

Note

The times count down only while KSAService.GetLifeSupportEnabled is true. Air lasts forever in breathable air outside the vehicle.

enum LifeSupportLevel#

How long the crew’s supplies last. See LifeSupportService.GetLevel.

value LIFE_SUPPORT_LEVEL_OK#

More than a day of supplies, or no crew.

value LIFE_SUPPORT_LEVEL_LOW#

A day of supplies or less.

value LIFE_SUPPORT_LEVEL_CRITICAL#

An hour of supplies or less.

value LIFE_SUPPORT_LEVEL_OUT#

A supply has run out.

enum LifeSupportSupply#

value LIFE_SUPPORT_SUPPLY_WATER#

value LIFE_SUPPORT_SUPPLY_FOOD#

Meals.

The connected habitable parts of a vehicle, which share their crew’s supplies. Obtained from VehicleService.GetHabitatZones or HabitatService.GetZone.

Note

Parts join a zone through connections the crew can pass, such as an open docking port hatch.

service MissionService#

The mission planned for a vehicle in the editor’s life support window. Obtained from VehicleService.GetMission.

Note

The game times the legs of the mission from the plan, and checks the supplies of each habitat zone against them. See HabitatZoneService.GetMissionSupplies.

service MissionSuppliesService#

Whether the supplies of a habitat zone last its crew through the vehicle’s mission, as the life support window shows it. Obtained from HabitatZoneService.GetMissionSupplies.

Note

The check takes the legs left in the mission as planned, counted from the vehicle’s launch, and the kittens planned for each leg. The times are from now.

rpc Transfer (Habitat.TransferRequest) returns (Habitat.TransferResponse)#

Moves meals and canisters to another habitat in the same zone. Moves fewer when this habitat holds fewer, or the other has less room.

Habitat.TransferRequest:

  • this (Habitat)

  • to (Habitat): The habitat to move the supplies to.

  • meals (optional sint32): The number of meals to move. Defaults to 0.

  • canisters (optional sint32): The number of canisters to move. Defaults to 0.

Habitat.TransferResponse:

Empty

Note

A kitten in the zone moves the supplies. Throws an exception if life support is disabled, the habitats are in different zones, or the zone has no crew able to move them.
