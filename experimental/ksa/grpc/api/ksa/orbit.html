Orbit#

service OrbitService#

An orbit. Obtained from VehicleService.GetOrbit, BodyService.GetOrbit, BurnService.GetOrbit or OrbitService.GetNextOrbit.

Note

An orbit follows the game’s prediction as the trajectory changes. A predicted orbit, from OrbitService.GetNextOrbit or BurnService.GetOrbit, is a position in the vehicle’s or the burn’s predicted trajectory, and throws an exception while the trajectory is too short to reach it.

enum OrbitTransition#

How an orbit ends. See OrbitService.GetEndTransition.

value ORBIT_TRANSITION_NONE#

The orbit does not end.

value ORBIT_TRANSITION_ENCOUNTER#

The orbit enters the sphere of influence of another body.

value ORBIT_TRANSITION_ESCAPE#

The orbit leaves the sphere of influence of the body being orbited.

value ORBIT_TRANSITION_IMPACT#

The orbit hits the surface of the body being orbited.

value ORBIT_TRANSITION_BURN#

The orbit ends at a planned burn.
