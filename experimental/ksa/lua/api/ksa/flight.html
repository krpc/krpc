Flight#

class KSA.Flight#

Flight telemetry for a vehicle, relative to the surface of the body it orbits. Obtained from KSA.Vehicle.flight.

mean_altitude: number#

The altitude above the mean radius of the body, in meters.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

surface_altitude: number#

The altitude above the terrain or the ocean surface, whichever is higher, in meters.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

bedrock_altitude: number#

The altitude above the terrain, in meters. Over the sea, this is the altitude above the sea floor.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

elevation: number#

The height of the terrain below the vehicle, in meters above the mean radius of the body. Below the sea, this is the height of the sea floor.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

time_to_impact: number#

The time until the vehicle hits the surface on its current trajectory, in seconds. Not a number if its trajectory does not hit the surface.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

Note

The game predicts the trajectory on a worker thread, so a change to it shows here shortly after.

latitude: number#

The latitude of the vehicle, in degrees.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

longitude: number#

The longitude of the vehicle, in degrees.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

biome: string | nil#

The biome below the vehicle, or nil if the body has no biomes.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

speed: number#

The speed of the vehicle relative to the surface, in meters per second.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

horizontal_speed: number#

The horizontal speed of the vehicle relative to the surface, in meters per second.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

vertical_speed: number#

The vertical speed of the vehicle relative to the surface, in meters per second.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

pitch: number#

The pitch of the vehicle relative to the horizon, in degrees. A value between -90° and +90°.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

heading: number#

The heading of the vehicle (its angle relative to north), in degrees. A value between 0° and 360°.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

roll: number#

The roll of the vehicle relative to the horizon, in degrees. A value between -180° and +180°.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

static_pressure: number#

The static atmospheric pressure at the vehicle, in pascals.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

atmosphere_density: number#

The density of the atmosphere at the vehicle, in kilograms per cubic meter.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

static_air_temperature: number#

The temperature of the atmosphere at the vehicle, in kelvin. Zero outside the atmosphere.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

speed_of_sound: number#

The speed of sound in the atmosphere at the vehicle, in meters per second. Zero outside the atmosphere.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

mach: number#

The speed of the vehicle relative to the surface, as a multiple of the speed of sound. Zero outside the atmosphere.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

outside_air: KSA.OutsideAir#

Whether a kitten can breathe the air outside the vehicle.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

dynamic_pressure: number#

The dynamic pressure acting on the vehicle, in pascals.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

depth: number#

The depth of the vehicle’s center of mass below the ocean surface, in meters. Zero above the surface.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

Note

The ocean surface includes its waves.

submerged_portion: number#

The fraction of the vehicle’s volume below the ocean surface, between 0 and 1.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

buoyant_force: number#

The buoyant force on the vehicle from the ocean and the atmosphere, in newtons. It acts against gravity.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

dose_rate: number#

The radiation dose rate at the vehicle, in sieverts per second. Includes any solar particle event under way.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

orbit_average_dose_rate: number#

The radiation dose rate averaged over the vehicle’s orbit, in sieverts per second. Includes any solar particle event under way. Not a number if the body has no magnetosphere, or the orbit dips below the surface.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

solar_wind_speed: number#

The speed of the solar wind at the vehicle, in \(\mathrm{m/s}\). Zero if no star has a solar wind.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

solar_wind_pressure: number#

The dynamic pressure of the solar wind at the vehicle, in pascals.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

solar_wind_northward_field: number#

The northward component of the magnetic field carried by the solar wind at the vehicle, in teslas. A southward field, a negative value, drives geomagnetic storms.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

proton_flux: number#

The flux of solar energetic protons at the vehicle during a solar particle event, in protons per square centimeter per second per steradian. Zero outside an event.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

radiation_storm_level: number#

The level of the radiation storm at the vehicle, from 0 for none to 5 for the most severe. The S level of the game’s space weather window.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

incoming_coronal_mass_ejections: List#

The coronal mass ejections heading for the vehicle, as the game’s space weather window lists them.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

g_force: number#

The acceleration of the vehicle, excluding gravity, in multiples of standard gravity.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

angle_of_attack: number#

The angle between the direction the vehicle points in and its velocity relative to the surface, in the vehicle’s pitch plane, in degrees. Positive when the nose is above the velocity.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

sideslip_angle: number#

The angle between the direction the vehicle points in and its velocity relative to the surface, in the vehicle’s yaw plane, in degrees. Positive when the nose is to the right of the velocity.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

drag: number#

The part of the aerodynamic force along the airflow, in newtons.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

lift: number#

The part of the aerodynamic force across the airflow, in newtons.

Attribute:

Read-only, cannot be set

aerodynamic_force(reference_frame: KSA.ReferenceFrame): Tuple#

The aerodynamic force on the vehicle, in newtons.

Parameters:

reference_frame – The reference frame of the force.

Note

The game computes the force from the surfaces of the vehicle’s hull.

aerodynamic_torque(reference_frame: KSA.ReferenceFrame): Tuple#

The aerodynamic torque on the vehicle about its center of mass, in \(\mathrm{N \cdot m}\). The vector points along the axis of the torque.

Parameters:

reference_frame – The reference frame of the torque.

simulate_aerodynamic_force_at(
    body: KSA.Body,
    position: Tuple,
    velocity: Tuple,
    reference_frame: KSA.ReferenceFrame
): Tuple#

The aerodynamic force the vehicle would have at a given position and velocity, in its current attitude and rotation, in newtons.

Parameters:

  • body – The body whose atmosphere the vehicle flies through.

  • position – The position, in meters.

  • velocity – The velocity, in meters per second.

  • reference_frame – The reference frame of the position, velocity and force.

Note

The atmosphere moves with the body’s rotation.

prograde(reference_frame: KSA.ReferenceFrame): Tuple#

The unit vector of the vehicle’s velocity relative to the surface.

Parameters:

reference_frame – The reference frame of the direction.

normal(reference_frame: KSA.ReferenceFrame): Tuple#

The unit vector normal to the plane of the vehicle’s position and its velocity relative to the surface.

Parameters:

reference_frame – The reference frame of the direction.

radial(reference_frame: KSA.ReferenceFrame): Tuple#

The unit vector pointing radially outward, perpendicular to KSA.Flight:prograde() and KSA.Flight:normal().

Parameters:

reference_frame – The reference frame of the direction.

class KSA.OutsideAir#

Whether a kitten can breathe the air outside a vehicle. See KSA.Flight.outside_air.

no_atmosphere#

Outside the atmosphere, or at a body with none.

no_oxygen#

An atmosphere with no oxygen.

too_thin#

An atmosphere whose oxygen pressure is too low to breathe.

breathable#

Breathable air.
