Flight#

class Flight#

Flight telemetry for a vehicle, relative to the surface of the body it orbits. Obtained from Vehicle.Flight.

double MeanAltitude { get; }#

The altitude above the mean radius of the body, in meters.

Game Scenes:

Flight

double SurfaceAltitude { get; }#

The altitude above the terrain or the ocean surface, whichever is higher, in meters.

Game Scenes:

Flight

double BedrockAltitude { get; }#

The altitude above the terrain, in meters. Over the sea, this is the altitude above the sea floor.

Game Scenes:

Flight

double Elevation { get; }#

The height of the terrain below the vehicle, in meters above the mean radius of the body. Below the sea, this is the height of the sea floor.

Game Scenes:

Flight

float TimeToImpact { get; }#

The time until the vehicle hits the surface on its current trajectory, in seconds. Not a number if its trajectory does not hit the surface.

Game Scenes:

Flight

Note

The game predicts the trajectory on a worker thread, so a change to it shows here shortly after.

Flight

Note

Tuple<double, double, double> AerodynamicTorque (ReferenceFrame referenceFrame)#

The aerodynamic torque on the vehicle about its center of mass, in \(\mathrm{N \cdot m}\). The vector points along the axis of the torque.

Parameters:

  • referenceFrame – The reference frame of the torque.

Game Scenes:

Flight

Tuple<double, double, double> SimulateAerodynamicForceAt (Body body, Tuple<double, double, double> position, Tuple<double, double, double> velocity, ReferenceFrame referenceFrame)#

The aerodynamic force the vehicle would have at a given position and velocity, in its current attitude and rotation, in newtons.

Parameters:

  • body – The body whose atmosphere the vehicle flies through.

  • position – The position, in meters.

  • velocity – The velocity, in meters per second.

  • referenceFrame – The reference frame of the position, velocity and force.

Game Scenes:

Flight

Note

The atmosphere moves with the body’s rotation.

Outside the atmosphere, or at a body with none.

An atmosphere with no oxygen.

An atmosphere whose oxygen pressure is too low to breathe.
