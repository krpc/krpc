Parts#

type krpc_KSA_Part_t#

A part of a vehicle. Obtained from krpc_KSA_Vehicle_Parts().

Note

A part keeps its object when the vehicle it belongs to changes, for example when a decoupler separates it. Throws an exception once the part no longer exists.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Name(krpc_connection_t connection, char **returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)#

The id of the part’s template.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Title(krpc_connection_t connection, char **returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)#

The name of the part, as shown in the game.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Vehicle(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Vehicle_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)#

The vehicle the part belongs to.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Parent(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Part_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)#

The part this part is attached to, or nullptr for the root part.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Children(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_object_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)#

The parts attached to this part.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Sequence(krpc_connection_t connection, int32_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)#

The number of the sequence that activates the part, or -1 if no sequence does.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Mass(krpc_connection_t connection, float *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)#

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Batteries(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_object_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)#

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_SolarPanels(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_object_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)#

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Generators(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_object_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)#

The part’s generators.

The part’s power consumers.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Lights(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_object_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)#

The part’s lights.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Deployables(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_object_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)#

The part’s deployable sections. A part can have several, such as the doors of a service module.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_MagneticScoops(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_object_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)#

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Seats(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_object_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)#

The part’s crew seats.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Crew(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_object_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)#

The kittens in the part’s seats.

type krpc_KSA_Engine_t#

An engine. Obtained from krpc_KSA_Part_Engine().

Note

An active engine fires when the vehicle’s engines are on, at the vehicle’s throttle. See krpc_KSA_Control_EngineOn() and krpc_KSA_Control_Throttle().

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Engine_Part(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Part_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Engine_t instance)#

The part the engine is in.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Engine_Gimbals(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_object_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Engine_t instance)#

The gimbals that steer the engine’s nozzles. Empty if the engine has none.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Engine_Active(krpc_connection_t connection, bool *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Engine_t instance)#
krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Engine_set_Active(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Engine_t instance, bool value)#

Whether the engine is active, as when its sequence is activated. Setting it returns once the change has applied.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Engine_HasPropellant(krpc_connection_t connection, bool *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Engine_t instance)#

Whether the engine has propellant to burn.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Engine_MinimumThrottle(krpc_connection_t connection, float *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Engine_t instance)#

The lowest throttle the engine runs at, between 0 and 1.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Engine_Propellants(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_string_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Engine_t instance)#

The names of the substances the engine burns. See krpc_KSA_Resource_Name().

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Engine_Thrust(krpc_connection_t connection, float *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Engine_t instance)#

The thrust of the engine at its current throttle and the current atmospheric pressure, in Newtons. Zero when it is not firing.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Engine_AvailableThrust(krpc_connection_t connection, float *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Engine_t instance)#

The thrust of the engine at full throttle and the current atmospheric pressure, in Newtons. Zero when it is inactive or has no propellant.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Engine_SpecificImpulse(krpc_connection_t connection, float *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Engine_t instance)#

The specific impulse of the engine at the current atmospheric pressure, in seconds. Zero when it is inactive or has no propellant.

type krpc_KSA_Gimbal_t#

A gimbal that steers an engine nozzle. Obtained from krpc_KSA_Part_Gimbals() or krpc_KSA_Engine_Gimbals().

Note

A gimbal turns about the y and z axes of its mount, which krpc_KSA_Gimbal_Rotation() gives. The flight computer steers it while an engine it belongs to is krpc_KSA_Gimbal_Active().

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Gimbal_Part(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Part_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Gimbal_t instance)#

The part the gimbal is in.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Gimbal_AngleY(krpc_connection_t connection, float *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Gimbal_t instance)#

The angle of the gimbal about its y axis, in degrees.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Gimbal_AngleZ(krpc_connection_t connection, float *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Gimbal_t instance)#

The angle of the gimbal about its z axis, in degrees.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Gimbal_MaxAngleY(krpc_connection_t connection, float *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Gimbal_t instance)#

The largest angle of the gimbal about its y axis, in degrees. Zero if it does not turn about that axis.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Gimbal_MaxAngleZ(krpc_connection_t connection, float *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Gimbal_t instance)#

The largest angle of the gimbal about its z axis, in degrees. Zero if it does not turn about that axis.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Gimbal_Active(krpc_connection_t connection, bool *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Gimbal_t instance)#

Whether an engine the gimbal steers is active.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Gimbal_Rotation(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_tuple_double_double_double_double_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Gimbal_t instance, krpc_KSA_ReferenceFrame_t referenceFrame)#

The rotation of the gimbal’s mount, whose y and z axes the gimbal turns about.

Parameters:

  • referenceFrame – The reference frame of the rotation.

Whether the thrusters are active. Setting it returns once the change has applied.

The part the decoupler is in.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Decoupler_Enabled(krpc_connection_t connection, bool *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Decoupler_t instance)#

Whether the decoupler can fire.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Decoupler_Decoupled(krpc_connection_t connection, bool *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Decoupler_t instance)#

type krpc_KSA_Parachute_t#

A parachute. Obtained from krpc_KSA_Part_Parachute().

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Parachute_Part(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Part_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Parachute_t instance)#

The part the parachute is in.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Parachute_State(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_ParachuteState_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Parachute_t instance)#

The state of the parachute.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Parachute_Fill(krpc_connection_t connection, float *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Parachute_t instance)#

How far the canopy is open, from 0 to 1.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Parachute_DeployAltitude(krpc_connection_t connection, float *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Parachute_t instance)#

The height above the terrain at which an armed parachute deploys, in meters.

Whether the parachute can be cut away. Main parachutes cannot.

Cut the parachute away. Returns once it is cut. Throws an exception if it cannot be cut. See krpc_KSA_Parachute_CanCut().

type krpc_KSA_ParachuteState_t#

The state of a parachute. See krpc_KSA_Parachute_State().

KRPC_KSA_PARACHUTESTATE_STOWED#

Packed away.

KRPC_KSA_PARACHUTESTATE_ARMED#

Waiting to deploy at krpc_KSA_Parachute_DeployAltitude().

KRPC_KSA_PARACHUTESTATE_LINESTRETCH#

Leaving the pack.

KRPC_KSA_PARACHUTESTATE_REEFED#

Partly open, to limit the opening load.

KRPC_KSA_PARACHUTESTATE_DISREEFING#

Opening from reefed to full.

KRPC_KSA_PARACHUTESTATE_FULL#

Fully open.

KRPC_KSA_PARACHUTESTATE_CUT#

Cut away.

KRPC_KSA_PARACHUTESTATE_RIPPED#

Torn by too much dynamic pressure.

KRPC_KSA_PARACHUTESTATE_SEVEREDLINES#

Lost after its lines broke under too much load.

type krpc_KSA_Deployable_t#

A part that deploys and retracts, such as a landing leg, a solar panel or a service module door. Obtained from krpc_KSA_Part_Deployables().

Note

Deploying or retracting a part moves its symmetric copies with it, as the part’s menu in the game does.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Deployable_Part(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Part_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Deployable_t instance)#

The part the deployable is in.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Deployable_State(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_DeployableState_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Deployable_t instance)#

The state of the part.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Deployable_Deploy(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Deployable_t instance)#

Deploy the part. Returns once it has started to move.

Note

Throws an exception if the part is broken.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Deployable_Retract(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Deployable_t instance)#

Retract the part. Returns once it has started to move.

Note

Throws an exception if the part is broken.

type krpc_KSA_DeployableState_t#

The state of a deployable part. See krpc_KSA_Deployable_State().

KRPC_KSA_DEPLOYABLESTATE_DEPLOYED#

Fully deployed.

KRPC_KSA_DEPLOYABLESTATE_RETRACTED#

Fully retracted.

KRPC_KSA_DEPLOYABLESTATE_DEPLOYING#

Moving toward deployed.

KRPC_KSA_DEPLOYABLESTATE_RETRACTING#

Moving toward retracted.

KRPC_KSA_DEPLOYABLESTATE_BROKEN#

Broken, so it can no longer move.
