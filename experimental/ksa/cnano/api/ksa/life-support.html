Life Support#

type krpc_KSA_LifeSupport_t#

How long the supplies of a vehicle, a habitat zone or a kitten’s suit last. Obtained from krpc_KSA_Vehicle_LifeSupport(), krpc_KSA_HabitatZone_LifeSupport() or krpc_KSA_Kitten_LifeSupport().

Note

The times count down only while krpc_KSA_LifeSupportEnabled() is true. Air lasts forever in breathable air outside the vehicle.

The service and class ids of the class, for krpc_KRPC_Type_ClassTypeById().

The time until the first supply runs out, in seconds. A suit counts its air and scrubber only. Infinite with no crew.

type krpc_KSA_LifeSupportLevel_t#

How long the crew’s supplies last. See krpc_KSA_LifeSupport_Level().

KRPC_ENUMERATION_KSA_LIFESUPPORTLEVEL#

More than a day of supplies, or no crew.

KRPC_KSA_LIFESUPPORTLEVEL_LOW#

A day of supplies or less.

KRPC_KSA_LIFESUPPORTLEVEL_CRITICAL#

An hour of supplies or less.

KRPC_KSA_LIFESUPPORTLEVEL_OUT#

A supply has run out.

type krpc_KSA_LifeSupportSupply_t#

KRPC_KSA_LIFESUPPORTSUPPLY_AIR#

Oxygen to breathe.

KRPC_KSA_LIFESUPPORTSUPPLY_SCRUBBER#

KRPC_KSA_LIFESUPPORTSUPPLY_WATER#

Drinking water.

KRPC_KSA_LIFESUPPORTSUPPLY_FOOD#

type krpc_KSA_Mission_t#

The mission planned for a vehicle in the editor’s life support window. Obtained from krpc_KSA_Vehicle_Mission().

Note

The game times the legs of the mission from the plan, and checks the supplies of each habitat zone against them. See krpc_KSA_HabitatZone_MissionSupplies().

type krpc_KSA_MissionSupplies_t#

Whether the supplies of a habitat zone last its crew through the vehicle’s mission, as the life support window shows it. Obtained from krpc_KSA_HabitatZone_MissionSupplies().

Note

The check takes the legs left in the mission as planned, counted from the vehicle’s launch, and the kittens planned for each leg. The times are from now.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Habitat_Transfer(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Habitat_t instance, krpc_KSA_Habitat_t to, int32_t meals, int32_t canisters)#

Moves meals and canisters to another habitat in the same zone. Moves fewer when this habitat holds fewer, or the other has less room.

Parameters:

  • to – The habitat to move the supplies to.

  • meals – The number of meals to move.

  • canisters – The number of canisters to move.

Note

A kitten in the zone moves the supplies. Throws an exception if life support is disabled, the habitats are in different zones, or the zone has no crew able to move them.
