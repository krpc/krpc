Crew#

type krpc_KSA_Kitten_t#

A kitten in the agency’s roster. Obtained from krpc_KSA_Kittens(), krpc_KSA_Vehicle_Crew() or krpc_KSA_Seat_Kitten().

KRPC_CLASS_KSA_KITTEN#

The service and class ids of the class, for krpc_KRPC_Type_ClassTypeById().

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_Name(krpc_connection_t connection, char **returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

The kitten’s name, which is unique in the roster.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_RosterIndex(krpc_connection_t connection, int32_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

The kitten’s position in the agency’s roster, from 0. See krpc_KSA_Kittens().

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_Vehicle(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Vehicle_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

The vehicle the kitten is in, or nullptr if it is not in a seat.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_Seat(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Seat_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

The seat the kitten is in, or nullptr if it is not in a seat.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_KilledInAction(krpc_connection_t connection, bool *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

Whether the kitten has been killed in action.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_CauseOfDeath(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_DeathCause_t *returnValue, bool *returnValueIsNull, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

What killed the kitten, or nullptr if it is alive.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_MissionCount(krpc_connection_t connection, int32_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

The number of missions the kitten has flown, including the current one.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_MissionTime(krpc_connection_t connection, float *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

The time the kitten has spent on missions, including the current one, in seconds.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_DistanceTraveled(krpc_connection_t connection, float *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

The distance the kitten has traveled on missions, in meters.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_FastestSpeed(krpc_connection_t connection, float *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

The kitten’s highest speed on a mission, in meters per second.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_AcuteDose(krpc_connection_t connection, float *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

The kitten’s acute radiation dose, in sieverts. It heals over time, and causes radiation sickness from 1 sievert and death from 4 sieverts.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_LifetimeDose(krpc_connection_t connection, float *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

The kitten’s lifetime radiation dose, in sieverts. The kitten retires after a mission that takes it past 0.6 sieverts.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_DoseRate(krpc_connection_t connection, float *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

The radiation dose rate the kitten receives, in sieverts per second. The vehicle’s shielding reduces it in a seat, and on EVA the kitten is unshielded. Zero when the kitten is not on a mission.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_RadiationSickness(krpc_connection_t connection, bool *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

Whether the kitten has radiation sickness, which keeps it from flying.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_TimeToRadiationSickness(krpc_connection_t connection, float *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

The time until the kitten gets radiation sickness at its current dose rate, in seconds. Infinite if it never does, or the kitten already has it.

Note

The game forecasts it for a kitten on a mission while krpc_KSA_RadiationEnabled() is true.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_TimeToLethalDose(krpc_connection_t connection, float *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

The time until the kitten takes a lethal radiation dose at its current dose rate, in seconds. Infinite if it never does.

Note

The game forecasts it for a kitten on a mission while krpc_KSA_RadiationEnabled() is true, and only within a day of radiation sickness.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_TimeUntilFitToFly(krpc_connection_t connection, float *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

The time until the kitten has recovered from radiation sickness and can fly, resting at home, in seconds. Zero if the kitten does not have radiation sickness.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_RecoveryTime(krpc_connection_t connection, float *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

The time until the kitten’s acute radiation dose has healed below 1 millisievert, resting at home, in seconds.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_Retired(krpc_connection_t connection, bool *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

Whether the kitten has retired, having reached its lifetime radiation dose limit.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_WorstCondition(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_KittenCondition_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

The kitten’s worst condition across its supplies.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_Condition(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_KittenCondition_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance, krpc_KSA_LifeSupportSupply_t supply)#

The effect on the kitten of going without a supply.

Parameters:

  • supply – The supply.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_TimeWithout(krpc_connection_t connection, float *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance, krpc_KSA_LifeSupportSupply_t supply)#

The time the kitten has gone without a supply, in seconds. It counts down again once the supply returns.

Parameters:

  • supply – The supply.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_TimeToNextCondition(krpc_connection_t connection, float *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance, krpc_KSA_LifeSupportSupply_t supply)#

The further time the kitten can go without a supply before its condition from the supply worsens, in seconds. Infinite once the kitten is dead.

Parameters:

  • supply – The supply.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_Incapacitated(krpc_connection_t connection, bool *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

Whether the kitten is incapacitated by going without a supply. An incapacitated kitten cannot fly the vehicle or move supplies.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_OnEva(krpc_connection_t connection, bool *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

Whether the kitten is on EVA.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_GoOnEva(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

Leave the kitten’s vehicle through its EVA hatch, and return once the kitten is on EVA. The kitten becomes the controlled kitten, see krpc_KSA_ActiveKitten().

Note

Throws an exception if the kitten is not in a seat, or the vehicle has no EVA hatch.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_CanGoOnEva(krpc_connection_t connection, bool *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

Whether the kitten can go on EVA. See krpc_KSA_Kitten_GoOnEva().

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_Board(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Vehicle_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

Board the nearest vehicle with a free seat, and return once the kitten is in the seat. The vehicle becomes the controlled vehicle.

Returns:

The vehicle the kitten boarded.

Note

Throws an exception if the kitten is not on EVA, or no EVA hatch with a free seat is within one meter.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_CanBoard(krpc_connection_t connection, bool *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

Whether the kitten is on EVA and an EVA hatch with a free seat is in range. See krpc_KSA_Kitten_Board().

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_LocomotionMode(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_LocomotionMode_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

How the kitten on EVA is moving. Throws an exception if the kitten is not on EVA.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_Position(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_tuple_double_double_double_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance, krpc_KSA_ReferenceFrame_t referenceFrame)#

The position of the kitten, in meters. For a kitten in a seat, the position of the seat’s part. Throws an exception if the kitten is neither in a seat nor on EVA.

Parameters:

  • referenceFrame – The reference frame of the position.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_Velocity(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_tuple_double_double_double_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance, krpc_KSA_ReferenceFrame_t referenceFrame)#

The velocity of the kitten, in meters per second. For a kitten in a seat, the velocity of the seat’s part. Throws an exception if the kitten is neither in a seat nor on EVA.

Parameters:

  • referenceFrame – The reference frame of the velocity.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_LifeSupport(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_LifeSupport_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

How long the supplies in the kitten’s suit last, or nullptr if the kitten is not on EVA.

Note

The suit is filled from the kitten’s habitat zone when it goes on EVA, and what is left returns to the zone when it boards.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_CarriedMeals(krpc_connection_t connection, int32_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

The number of meals the kitten on EVA carries. Throws an exception if the kitten is not on EVA.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_CarriedCanisters(krpc_connection_t connection, int32_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)#

The number of carbon dioxide scrubber canisters the kitten on EVA carries. Throws an exception if the kitten is not on EVA.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_CanCarry(krpc_connection_t connection, bool *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance, krpc_KSA_Habitat_t habitat)#

Whether the kitten can take supplies from or stow them in a habitat. See krpc_KSA_Kitten_Take() and krpc_KSA_Kitten_Stow().

Parameters:

  • habitat – The habitat.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_Take(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance, krpc_KSA_Habitat_t habitat, int32_t meals, int32_t canisters)#

Take meals and canisters from a habitat within reach, and return once the kitten carries them. Takes fewer when the habitat holds fewer, or the kitten cannot carry more.

Parameters:

  • habitat – The habitat, which must store supplies.

  • meals – The number of meals to take.

  • canisters – The number of canisters to take.

Note

Throws an exception if the kitten is not on EVA or is unwell, life support is disabled, or the habitat stores no supplies or is out of reach. See krpc_KSA_Kitten_CanCarry().

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_Stow(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance, krpc_KSA_Habitat_t habitat, int32_t meals, int32_t canisters)#

Stow the meals and canisters the kitten carries in a habitat within reach, and return once they are stowed. Stows fewer when the kitten carries fewer, or the habitat has less room.

Parameters:

  • habitat – The habitat, which must store supplies.

  • meals – The number of meals to stow.

  • canisters – The number of canisters to stow.

Note

Throws an exception if the kitten is not on EVA or is unwell, life support is disabled, or the habitat stores no supplies or is out of reach. See krpc_KSA_Kitten_CanCarry().

type krpc_KSA_CrewEvent_t#

A kitten’s most urgent coming event: its supplies running low, or its condition worsening. Obtained from krpc_KSA_Vehicle_CrewEvents() or krpc_KSA_Vehicle_NextCrewEvent().

Note

As the game’s crew clock, an event within a day is the worst stage the kitten reaches in that time, and a later event is the soonest. The event is the game’s forecast when it is read from the vehicle. Read it again for a new forecast.

type krpc_KSA_Seat_t#

A crew seat. Obtained from krpc_KSA_Part_Seats().

The service and class ids of the class, for krpc_KRPC_Type_ClassTypeById().

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Seat_Kitten(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Seat_t instance)#
