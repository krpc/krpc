Vehicle#

type krpc_KSA_Vehicle_t#

A vehicle in the current system. Obtained from krpc_KSA_ActiveVehicle() or krpc_KSA_Vehicles().

Note

Docking and undocking replace the vehicles involved, so an object for one of them throws an exception afterwards.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Vehicle_Name(krpc_connection_t connection, char **returnValue, krpc_KSA_Vehicle_t instance)#
krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Vehicle_set_Name(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Vehicle_t instance, const char *value)#

The name of the vehicle, which is unique in the system.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Vehicle_Destroy(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Vehicle_t instance)#

Destroy the vehicle, killing its crew, as abandoning the vehicle does. Returns once the vehicle is gone.

Note

If the vehicle is the active vehicle, the game hands control to a nearby vehicle.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Vehicle_CanRecover(krpc_connection_t connection, bool *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Vehicle_t instance)#

Whether the vehicle can be recovered: at rest on the surface of the home planet.

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Vehicle_Recover(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Vehicle_t instance)#

Recover the vehicle, ending its crew’s missions, as the game’s recover button does. Returns once the vehicle is gone.

Note

Throws an exception if the vehicle cannot be recovered, see krpc_KSA_Vehicle_CanRecover().

krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Vehicle_AddBurn(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Burn_t *returnValue, krpc_KSA_Vehicle_t instance, double ut, float prograde, float normal, float radial)#

Plan a burn, and add it to the vehicle’s burn plan.

Parameters:

  • ut – Universal time of the burn, in seconds.

  • prograde – Change in velocity in the prograde direction, in meters per second.

  • normal – Change in velocity along the orbit normal, in meters per second.

  • radial – Change in velocity radially outward, in meters per second.

Returns:

The burn.

Note

Returns once the flight computer has timed the burn, if it is the next one. Throws an exception if the time is in the past, or beyond the vehicle’s predicted trajectory.

type krpc_KSA_Situation_t#

The situation of a vehicle: its contact with the surface, and whether the game is simulating it with physics or propagating it along its orbit. See krpc_KSA_Vehicle_Situation().

KRPC_KSA_SITUATION_MANEUVERING#

No surface contact, simulated with physics.

KRPC_KSA_SITUATION_FREEFALL#

No surface contact, on rails.

KRPC_KSA_SITUATION_ROLLING#

On terrain, simulated with physics.

KRPC_KSA_SITUATION_LANDED#

At rest on terrain.

KRPC_KSA_SITUATION_SAILING#

In the ocean, simulated with physics.

KRPC_KSA_SITUATION_FLOATING#

At rest in the ocean.

KRPC_KSA_SITUATION_DRAGGING#

On terrain and in the ocean, simulated with physics.

KRPC_KSA_SITUATION_BOTTOMED#

At rest on terrain and in the ocean.
