Resources#

public class Resource#

A substance held in tanks, such as a propellant, in a part or across a whole vehicle. Obtained from Part.getResources() or Vehicle.getResources().

String getName()#

The name of the substance.

float getAmount()#

The mass of the substance held, in kilograms.

float getMax()#

The mass of the substance the tanks can hold, in kilograms.

public class Tank#

A propellant tank. Obtained from Part.getTanks() or Vehicle.getTanks().

Part getPart()#

The part the tank is in.

float getVolume()#

The volume of the tank, in cubic meters.

float getRatedPressure()#

The pressure the tank stores gas at, in pascals. Zero if the tank cannot store gas.

float getFilledFraction()#

The fraction of the tank’s volume that is filled, between 0 and 1.

boolean getPropellantUseEnabled()#
void setPropellantUseEnabled(boolean value)#

Whether engines can draw propellant from the tank.

TankTransferMode getTransferMode()#
void setTransferMode(TankTransferMode value)#

The direction the tank transfers propellant in.

Note

Propellant flows from tanks transferring out to tanks of the same vehicle transferring in, and the transfer draws battery power. Setting a transfer throws an exception if the running version of KSA does not support it.

public enum TankTransferMode#

The direction a tank transfers propellant in. See Tank.getTransferMode().

public TankTransferMode NONE#

No transfer.

public TankTransferMode IN#

Into the tank, from tanks transferring out.

public TankTransferMode OUT#

Out of the tank, to tanks transferring in.

public class FuelLink#

A fuel line between two parts of a vehicle, which lets propellant flow between them. Obtained from Vehicle.getFuelLinks().

Part getPartA()#

One of the parts the link joins.

Part getPartB()#

The other part the link joins.

FuelLinkFlow getFlow()#
void setFlow(FuelLinkFlow value)#

The direction propellant flows through the link.

boolean getEnabled()#
void setEnabled(boolean value)#

Whether propellant can flow through the link.

public enum FuelLinkFlow#

The direction propellant flows through a fuel link. See FuelLink.getFlow().

public FuelLinkFlow A_TO_B#

From FuelLink.getPartA() to FuelLink.getPartB().

public FuelLinkFlow B_TO_A#

From FuelLink.getPartB() to FuelLink.getPartA().

public FuelLinkFlow BOTH#

Either way.
