Body#

public class Body#

A planet, moon or star. See getBodies().

String getName()#

The name of the body.

boolean getIsStar()#

Whether the body is a star.

double getMass()#

The mass of the body, in kilograms.

double getGravitationalParameter()#

The standard gravitational parameter of the body, in cubic meters per second squared.

double getRadius()#

The radius of the body, in meters.

double getAtmosphereRadius()#

The radius of the top of the atmosphere, in meters. Zero for a body with no atmosphere.

boolean getHasAtmosphere()#

Whether the body has an atmosphere.

float getSeaLevelPressure()#

The atmospheric pressure at the body’s mean radius, in pascals. Zero for a body with no atmosphere.

float getSeaLevelDensity()#

The density of the atmosphere at the body’s mean radius, in kilograms per cubic meter. Zero for a body with no atmosphere.

float getScaleHeight()#

The height over which the atmospheric pressure falls by a factor of e, in meters. Zero for a body with no atmosphere.

float pressureAt(double altitude)#

The atmospheric pressure at an altitude, in pascals. Zero for a body with no atmosphere.

Parameters:

  • altitude (double) – Altitude above the body’s mean radius, in meters.

float densityAt(double altitude)#

The density of the atmosphere at an altitude, in kilograms per cubic meter. Zero for a body with no atmosphere.

Parameters:

  • altitude (double) – Altitude above the body’s mean radius, in meters.

boolean getHasOcean()#

Whether the body has an ocean.

float getOceanDensity()#

The density of the ocean, in kilograms per cubic meter. Zero for a body with no ocean.

float getSurfaceGravity()#

The acceleration due to gravity at the body’s mean radius, in meters per second squared.

float getRotationalSpeed()#

The rate at which the body rotates about its axis, in radians per second.

float getRotationalPeriod()#

The time the body takes to rotate once about its axis, relative to the stars, in seconds. Infinite for a body that does not rotate.

double getSphereOfInfluence()#

The radius of the body’s sphere of influence, in meters. Not a number for a body that orbits nothing.

Body getParent()#

The body this body orbits, or null if it orbits nothing.

java.util.List<Body> getSatellites()#

The bodies that orbit this body.

Orbit getOrbit()#

The orbit of the body, or null if it orbits nothing.

double surfaceHeight(double latitude, double longitude)#

The height of the terrain or the sea surface, whichever is higher, at a point on the body, in meters above the body’s mean radius.

Parameters:

  • latitude (double) – Latitude, in degrees.

  • longitude (double) – Longitude, in degrees.

double bedrockHeight(double latitude, double longitude)#

The height of the terrain at a point on the body, in meters above the body’s mean radius. Below the sea, this is the height of the sea floor.

Parameters:

  • latitude (double) – Latitude, in degrees.

  • longitude (double) – Longitude, in degrees.

Note

Zero for a body with no terrain, such as a gas giant.

java.util.List<String> getLaunchSites()#

The names of the launch sites on the body. See launchVehicle(String, String).

java.util.List<String> getBiomes()#

The names of the body’s biomes. Empty for a body with no biomes.

String biomeAt(double latitude, double longitude)#

The biome at a point on the body, or null if the body has no biomes.

Parameters:

  • latitude (double) – Latitude, in degrees.

  • longitude (double) – Longitude, in degrees.

ReferenceFrame getReferenceFrame()#

The reference frame fixed to the body, with its origin at the body’s center. The z axis points to the north pole, the x axis to latitude and longitude zero, and the y axis to latitude zero and longitude 90 degrees east. The frame rotates with the body.

ReferenceFrame getNonRotatingReferenceFrame()#

The reference frame with its origin at the body’s center that does not rotate. The z axis points to the north pole, and the x and y axes are fixed relative to the stars.

org.javatuples.Triplet<Double, Double, Double> position(ReferenceFrame referenceFrame)#

The position of the center of the body, in meters.

Parameters:

  • referenceFrame (ReferenceFrame) – The reference frame of the position.

org.javatuples.Triplet<Double, Double, Double> velocity(ReferenceFrame referenceFrame)#

The velocity of the body, in meters per second.

Parameters:

  • referenceFrame (ReferenceFrame) – The reference frame of the velocity.

org.javatuples.Quartet<Double, Double, Double, Double> rotation(ReferenceFrame referenceFrame)#

The rotation of Body.getReferenceFrame() relative to the given frame.

Parameters:

  • referenceFrame (ReferenceFrame) – The reference frame of the rotation.

org.javatuples.Triplet<Double, Double, Double> angularVelocity(ReferenceFrame referenceFrame)#

The angular velocity of the body, in radians per second. The vector points along the axis of rotation, and its length is the rate of rotation.

Parameters:

  • referenceFrame (ReferenceFrame) – The reference frame of the angular velocity.

org.javatuples.Triplet<Double, Double, Double> surfacePosition(double latitude, double longitude, ReferenceFrame referenceFrame)#

The position of a point on the surface, at the body’s mean radius, in meters.

Parameters:

  • latitude (double) – Latitude, in degrees.

  • longitude (double) – Longitude, in degrees.

  • referenceFrame (ReferenceFrame) – The reference frame of the position.

double latitudeAtPosition(org.javatuples.Triplet<Double, Double, Double> position, ReferenceFrame referenceFrame)#

The latitude of a position, in degrees.

Parameters:

  • position (org.javatuples.Triplet) – Position, in meters.

  • referenceFrame (ReferenceFrame) – The reference frame of the position.

double longitudeAtPosition(org.javatuples.Triplet<Double, Double, Double> position, ReferenceFrame referenceFrame)#

The longitude of a position, in degrees.

Parameters:

  • position (org.javatuples.Triplet) – Position, in meters.

  • referenceFrame (ReferenceFrame) – The reference frame of the position.

double altitudeAtPosition(org.javatuples.Triplet<Double, Double, Double> position, ReferenceFrame referenceFrame)#

The altitude of a position above the body’s mean radius, in meters.

Parameters:

  • position (org.javatuples.Triplet) – Position, in meters.

  • referenceFrame (ReferenceFrame) – The reference frame of the position.
