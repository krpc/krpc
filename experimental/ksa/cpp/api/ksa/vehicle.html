Vehicle#

class Vehicle#

A vehicle in the current system. Obtained from active_vehicle() or vehicles().

Note

Docking and undocking replace the vehicles involved, so an object for one of them throws an exception afterwards.

std::string name()#
void set_name(std::string value)#

The name of the vehicle, which is unique in the system.

void destroy()#

Destroy the vehicle, killing its crew, as abandoning the vehicle does. Returns once the vehicle is gone.

Note

If the vehicle is the active vehicle, the game hands control to a nearby vehicle.

bool can_recover()#

Whether the vehicle can be recovered: at rest on the surface of the home planet.

void recover()#

Recover the vehicle, ending its crew’s missions, as the game’s recover button does. Returns once the vehicle is gone.

Note

Throws an exception if the vehicle cannot be recovered, see Vehicle::can_recover().

Situation situation()#

The situation of the vehicle.

bool on_rails()#

Whether the game moves the vehicle without simulating the forces on it: along its orbit, or held on the surface. True in the freefall, landed, floating and bottomed situations.

bool is_debris()#

Whether the vehicle is debris, such as a spent stage.

bool has_launched()#

Whether the vehicle has launched.

double launch_time()#

The universal time the vehicle was created, in seconds.

float mass()#

The total mass of the vehicle, including propellant, in kilograms.

float propellant_mass()#

The mass of the propellant in the vehicle, in kilograms.

float thrust()#

The thrust of the engines at their current throttle and the current atmospheric pressure, in Newtons.

float available_thrust()#

The thrust of the active engines that have propellant, at full throttle and the current atmospheric pressure, in Newtons.

float specific_impulse()#

The combined specific impulse of the active engines that have propellant, at the current atmospheric pressure, in seconds. Zero when no engine is active.

float peak_g_load()#

The highest smoothed g-load on the vehicle during the last physics step, as a multiple of standard gravity. Zero while the vehicle is Vehicle::on_rails().

float max_g_load()#

The g-load the vehicle can take before it breaks up, as a multiple of standard gravity. Zero while the vehicle is Vehicle::on_rails().

float peak_dynamic_pressure()#

The highest dynamic pressure on the vehicle during the last physics step, in pascals. In the sea, this is the dynamic pressure of the water. Zero while the vehicle is Vehicle::on_rails().

float max_dynamic_pressure()#

The dynamic pressure the vehicle can take before it breaks up, in pascals. Zero while the vehicle is Vehicle::on_rails().

std::vector<Part> parts()#

The vehicle’s parts.

Part root_part()#

The vehicle’s root part, which the other parts are attached to.

std::vector<Part> parts_with_name(std::string name)#

The vehicle’s parts with the given template id. See Part::name().

Parameters:

  • name – The template id.

std::vector<Seat> seats()#

The vehicle’s crew seats.

std::vector<Kitten> crew()#

The kittens in the vehicle’s seats.

int32_t crew_capacity()#

The number of crew seats in the vehicle.

std::vector<Engine> engines()#

The vehicle’s engines.

std::vector<RCS> rcs()#

The vehicle’s RCS thrusters.

std::vector<Decoupler> decouplers()#

The vehicle’s decouplers.

std::vector<Parachute> parachutes()#

The vehicle’s parachutes.

std::vector<Tank> tanks()#

The vehicle’s propellant tanks.

std::vector<Battery> batteries()#

The vehicle’s batteries.

std::vector<SolarPanel> solar_panels()#

The vehicle’s solar panels.

std::vector<Generator> generators()#

The vehicle’s generators.

std::vector<PowerConsumer> power_consumers()#

The vehicle’s power consumers.

float stored_energy()#

The energy stored in the vehicle’s batteries, in joules.

float energy_capacity()#

The energy the vehicle’s batteries can store, in joules.

float net_power()#

The power produced by the vehicle’s solar panels and generators, less the power its parts use, in watts.

std::vector<Light> lights()#

The vehicle’s lights.

std::vector<Deployable> deployables()#

The vehicle’s deployable sections.

std::vector<MagneticScoop> magnetic_scoops()#

The vehicle’s magnetic scoops.

std::vector<FuelLink> fuel_links()#

The vehicle’s fuel links.

std::vector<DockingPort> docking_ports()#

The vehicle’s docking ports.

std::vector<Resource> resources()#

The resources the vehicle holds, one for each substance, totaled over its parts.

float delta_v()#

The change in velocity of all the vehicle’s sequences, in meters per second.

std::vector<Sequence> sequences()#

The vehicle’s sequences, in activation order.

std::optional<Vehicle> target_vehicle()#
void set_target_vehicle(std::optional<Vehicle> value)#

The vehicle targeted by this vehicle, or NULL. Setting it replaces the target, and NULL clears it.

std::optional<Body> target_body()#
void set_target_body(std::optional<Body> value)#

The body targeted by this vehicle, or NULL. Setting it replaces the target, and NULL clears it.

Note

Setting a body that orbits nothing, such as the star, throws an exception.

std::optional<Part> target_part()#
void set_target_part(std::optional<Part> value)#

The part targeted by this vehicle, on the vehicle in Vehicle::target_vehicle(), or NULL. Setting it targets the part and its vehicle, and NULL clears the target.

std::vector<Burn> burns()#

The vehicle’s planned burns, in time order.

Burn add_burn(double ut, float prograde = 0.0f, float normal = 0.0f, float radial = 0.0f)#

Plan a burn, and add it to the vehicle’s burn plan.

Parameters:

  • ut – Universal time of the burn, in seconds.

  • prograde – Change in velocity in the prograde direction, in meters per second.

  • normal – Change in velocity along the orbit normal, in meters per second.

  • radial – Change in velocity radially outward, in meters per second.

Returns:

The burn.

Note

Returns once the flight computer has timed the burn, if it is the next one. Throws an exception if the time is in the past, or beyond the vehicle’s predicted trajectory.

Orbit orbit()#

The current orbit of the vehicle.

Flight flight()#

Flight telemetry for the vehicle, relative to the surface of the body it orbits.

The controls of the vehicle.

Game Scenes:

The auto-pilot of the vehicle.

Game Scenes:

ReferenceFrame reference_frame()#

The reference frame fixed to the vehicle, with its origin at the vehicle’s position. The x axis points forward, the y axis to the right and the z axis down, relative to the vehicle’s control point.

ReferenceFrame surface_reference_frame()#

The reference frame with its origin at the vehicle’s position, and axes aligned with the surface below it. The x axis points east, the y axis north and the z axis up. The frame rotates with the body.

ReferenceFrame orbital_reference_frame()#

The reference frame with its origin at the vehicle’s position, and axes aligned with its orbit. The x axis points prograde, the y axis along the orbit normal and the z axis radially outward, perpendicular to the x axis.

std::tuple<double, double, double> position(ReferenceFrame reference_frame)#

The position of the vehicle, in meters.

Parameters:

  • reference_frame – The reference frame of the position.

std::tuple<double, double, double> velocity(ReferenceFrame reference_frame)#

The velocity of the vehicle, in meters per second.

Parameters:

  • reference_frame – The reference frame of the velocity.

std::tuple<double, double, double> direction(ReferenceFrame reference_frame)#

The unit vector in which the vehicle points, the x axis of Vehicle::reference_frame().

Parameters:

  • reference_frame – The reference frame of the direction.

std::tuple<double, double, double, double> rotation(ReferenceFrame reference_frame)#

The rotation of Vehicle::reference_frame() relative to the given frame.

Parameters:

  • reference_frame – The reference frame of the rotation.

std::tuple<double, double, double> angular_velocity(ReferenceFrame reference_frame)#

The angular velocity of the vehicle, in radians per second. The vector points along the axis of rotation, and its length is the rate of rotation.

Parameters:

  • reference_frame – The reference frame of the angular velocity.

enum struct Situation#

The situation of a vehicle: its contact with the surface, and whether the game is simulating it with physics or propagating it along its orbit. See Vehicle::situation().

enumerator maneuvering#

No surface contact, simulated with physics.

enumerator freefall#

No surface contact, on rails.

enumerator rolling#

On terrain, simulated with physics.

enumerator landed#

At rest on terrain.

enumerator sailing#

In the ocean, simulated with physics.

enumerator floating#

At rest in the ocean.

enumerator dragging#

On terrain and in the ocean, simulated with physics.

enumerator bottomed#

At rest on terrain and in the ocean.

class VehicleDesign#

A vehicle design that can be launched: one of the game’s default vehicles, or a vehicle saved by the player in the editor. Obtained from vehicle_designs().

Note

The mass and delta-v are those the editor shows for the design.

std::string name()#

The name of the design, as passed to launch_vehicle().

bool is_default()#

Whether the design is one of the game’s default vehicles.

int32_t part_count()#

The number of parts in the design.

float mass()#

The total mass of the design, including propellant, in kilograms.

float dry_mass()#

The mass of the design without propellant, in kilograms.

float delta_v()#

The change in velocity of all the design’s sequences, in meters per second.

Note

Each sequence is computed in the environment chosen for it in the editor, in vacuum or at sea level.
