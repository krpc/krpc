Resources#

class Resource#

A substance held in tanks, such as a propellant, in a part or across a whole vehicle. Obtained from Part::resources() or Vehicle::resources().

std::string name()#

The name of the substance.

float amount()#

The mass of the substance held, in kilograms.

float max()#

The mass of the substance the tanks can hold, in kilograms.

class Tank#

A propellant tank. Obtained from Part::tanks() or Vehicle::tanks().

Part part()#

The part the tank is in.

float volume()#

The volume of the tank, in cubic meters.

float rated_pressure()#

The pressure the tank stores gas at, in pascals. Zero if the tank cannot store gas.

float filled_fraction()#

The fraction of the tank’s volume that is filled, between 0 and 1.

bool propellant_use_enabled()#
void set_propellant_use_enabled(bool value)#

Whether engines can draw propellant from the tank.

TankTransferMode transfer_mode()#
void set_transfer_mode(TankTransferMode value)#

The direction the tank transfers propellant in.

Note

Propellant flows from tanks transferring out to tanks of the same vehicle transferring in, and the transfer draws battery power. Setting a transfer throws an exception if the running version of KSA does not support it.

enum struct TankTransferMode#

The direction a tank transfers propellant in. See Tank::transfer_mode().

enumerator none#

No transfer.

enumerator in#

Into the tank, from tanks transferring out.

enumerator out#

Out of the tank, to tanks transferring in.

class FuelLink#

A fuel line between two parts of a vehicle, which lets propellant flow between them. Obtained from Vehicle::fuel_links().

Part part_a()#

One of the parts the link joins.

Part part_b()#

The other part the link joins.

FuelLinkFlow flow()#
void set_flow(FuelLinkFlow value)#

The direction propellant flows through the link.

bool enabled()#
void set_enabled(bool value)#

Whether propellant can flow through the link.

enum struct FuelLinkFlow#

The direction propellant flows through a fuel link. See FuelLink::flow().

enumerator a_to_b#

From FuelLink::part_a() to FuelLink::part_b().

enumerator b_to_a#

From FuelLink::part_b() to FuelLink::part_a().

enumerator both#

Either way.
