Life Support#

class LifeSupport#

How long the supplies of a vehicle, a habitat zone or a kitten’s suit last. Obtained from Vehicle::life_support(), HabitatZone::life_support() or Kitten::life_support().

Note

The times count down only while life_support_enabled() is true. Air lasts forever in breathable air outside the vehicle.

int32_t kittens()#

The number of kittens the supplies are shared between.

float air_mass()#

The mass of oxygen in the crew’s tanks, in kilograms.

float water_mass()#

The mass of water in the crew’s tanks, in kilograms.

int32_t meals()#

The number of meals.

float air_time()#

The time the air lasts, in seconds. Infinite with no crew, or in breathable air.

float scrubber_time()#

The time the carbon dioxide scrubbers last, in seconds. Infinite with no crew.

float water_time()#

The time the water lasts, in seconds. Infinite with no crew.

float food_time()#

The time the food lasts, in seconds. Infinite with no crew.

float time()#

The time until the first supply runs out, in seconds. A suit counts its air and scrubber only. Infinite with no crew.

LifeSupportSupply limiting_supply()#

The supply that runs out first. Air when every time is infinite, as with no crew.

LifeSupportLevel level()#

How long the supplies last, as the game’s life support gauge shows it.

float time_to_next_level()#

The time until LifeSupport::level() next changes, in seconds. Infinite with no crew.

enum struct LifeSupportLevel#

How long the crew’s supplies last. See LifeSupport::level().

enumerator ok#

More than a day of supplies, or no crew.

enumerator low#

A day of supplies or less.

enumerator critical#

An hour of supplies or less.

enumerator out#

A supply has run out.

enum struct LifeSupportSupply#

A supply the crew needs. See LifeSupport::limiting_supply().

enumerator air#

Oxygen to breathe.

enumerator scrubber#

Lithium hydroxide canisters, which scrub carbon dioxide from the air.

enumerator water#

Drinking water.

enumerator food#

Meals.

class HabitatZone#

The connected habitable parts of a vehicle, which share their crew’s supplies. Obtained from Vehicle::habitat_zones() or Habitat::zone().

Note

Parts join a zone through connections the crew can pass, such as an open docking port hatch.

Vehicle vehicle()#

The vehicle the zone is in.

std::vector<Part> parts()#

The parts in the zone.

std::vector<Habitat> habitats()#

The habitats in the zone.

std::vector<Seat> seats()#

The crew seats in the zone.

std::vector<Kitten> crew()#

The kittens in the zone’s seats.

std::vector<HabitatZone> linked_zones()#

The zones that join this one when closed docking port hatches between them open.

std::vector<DockingPort> hatches_to(HabitatZone zone)#

The closed docking port hatches that join this zone to a linked zone when they open.

Parameters:

  • zone – A zone in HabitatZone::linked_zones().

Note

Throws an exception if the zone is not linked to this one.

float habitable_volume()#

The volume the crew can live in, in cubic meters.

float storage_volume()#

The volume for stored supplies, in cubic meters.

LifeSupport life_support()#

How long the zone’s supplies last its crew.

std::optional<MissionSupplies> mission_supplies()#

Whether the zone’s supplies last its crew through the vehicle’s mission. NULL without a feasible mission with time left, or while life_support_enabled() is false.

int32_t planned_kittens(MissionLeg leg)#

The number of kittens planned in the zone for a leg of the vehicle’s mission. The plan defaults to the kittens seated in the zone.

Parameters:

  • leg – The leg.

class Mission#

The mission planned for a vehicle in the editor’s life support window. Obtained from Vehicle::mission().

Note

The game times the legs of the mission from the plan, and checks the supplies of each habitat zone against them. See HabitatZone::mission_supplies().

MissionDestination destination()#

Where the mission goes.

std::optional<Body> body()#

The body the mission goes to, or NULL if it has no destination. For a low orbit, this is the home body.

MissionArrival arrival()#

What the mission does at its destination.

MissionTrip trip()#

Whether the mission comes back.

float stay()#

The time planned at the destination, in seconds.

Note

The game rounds a stay before a return up to the next transfer window home. See Mission::stay_time().

bool custom()#

Whether the mission has a length set by the player, in place of legs timed from its destination. See Mission::custom_length().

float custom_length()#

The length of a mission with Mission::custom() set, in seconds.

bool feasible()#

Whether the game can time the legs of the mission. A mission with no destination, or a flyby with no way back, cannot be timed.

Note

The leg times are zero when this is false.

float out_time()#

The planned time of the way out, in seconds.

float stay_time()#

The planned time at the destination, in seconds.

float back_time()#

The planned time of the way back, in seconds. Zero for a one way trip.

float return_wait()#

The time from arriving at the destination until the next transfer window home, in seconds. Zero when there is no wait.

float total_time()#

The planned length of the mission, in seconds.

float time_left()#

The time left in the mission as planned, counted from the vehicle’s launch, in seconds.

double arrival_ut()#

The universal time the vehicle arrived at the destination body, in seconds. Not a number until it has.

Note

The game records the arrival for a mission to another body.

double departure_ut()#

The universal time the vehicle left the destination body, in seconds. Not a number until it has.

enum struct MissionDestination#

Where a mission goes. See Mission::destination().

enumerator none#

No destination.

enumerator low_orbit#

A low orbit around the home body.

enumerator body#

Another body, given by Mission::body().

enum struct MissionArrival#

What a mission does at its destination. See Mission::arrival().

enumerator flyby#

Flies past it.

enumerator orbit#

Orbits it.

enumerator land#

Lands on it.

enum struct MissionTrip#

Whether a mission comes back. See Mission::trip().

enumerator return_#

Goes and comes back.

enumerator one_way#

Goes and stays.

enum struct MissionLeg#

A leg of a mission. See HabitatZone::planned_kittens().

enumerator out#

The way out.

enumerator stay#

The stay at the destination.

enumerator back#

The way back.

class MissionSupplies#

Whether the supplies of a habitat zone last its crew through the vehicle’s mission, as the life support window shows it. Obtained from HabitatZone::mission_supplies().

Note

The check takes the legs left in the mission as planned, counted from the vehicle’s launch, and the kittens planned for each leg. The times are from now.

MissionSupplyVerdict verdict()#

Whether the supplies last the mission.

float short_by()#

The time by which the first supply to run out falls short of the end of the mission, in seconds. Zero when the supplies last.

LifeSupportSupply first_supply()#

The supply that runs out first.

float time_left(LifeSupportSupply supply)#

The time until a supply runs out with the crew planned for each leg, in seconds. Infinite if it lasts the mission.

Parameters:

  • supply – The supply.

Note

Air lasts while the vehicle is in breathable air.

float end()#

The time until the zone has no crew left in the mission, in seconds.

enum struct MissionSupplyVerdict#

Whether a habitat zone has the supplies for a mission. See MissionSupplies::verdict().

enumerator unused#

The zone has no crew during the mission.

enumerator enough#

The supplies last the mission.

enumerator short_#

A supply runs out before the end of the mission.

class Habitat#

A habitable or storage space in a part, which holds meals and carbon dioxide scrubber canisters. Obtained from Part::habitats().

Part part()#

The part the habitat is in.

HabitatZone zone()#

The habitat zone the habitat is in.

float habitable_volume()#

The volume the crew can live in, in cubic meters.

float storage_volume()#

The volume for stored supplies, in cubic meters.

int32_t meals()#

The number of meals in the habitat.

int32_t canisters()#

The number of carbon dioxide scrubber canisters in the habitat.

int32_t meal_capacity()#

The number of meals the habitat holds when full.

int32_t canister_capacity()#

The number of carbon dioxide scrubber canisters the habitat holds when full.

void transfer(Habitat to, int32_t meals = 0, int32_t canisters = 0)#

Moves meals and canisters to another habitat in the same zone. Moves fewer when this habitat holds fewer, or the other has less room.

Parameters:

  • to – The habitat to move the supplies to.

  • meals – The number of meals to move.

  • canisters – The number of canisters to move.

Note

A kitten in the zone moves the supplies. Throws an exception if life support is disabled, the habitats are in different zones, or the zone has no crew able to move them.
