.. grpc:service:: PartService

   A part of a vehicle. Obtained from :grpc:rpc:`VehicleService.GetParts <krpc.ksa.VehicleService.GetParts>`.

   .. note::

      A part keeps its object when the vehicle it belongs to changes, for example when a
      decoupler separates it. Throws an exception once the part no longer exists.

.. grpc:enum:: ParachuteState

   The state of a parachute. See :grpc:rpc:`ParachuteService.GetState <krpc.ksa.ParachuteService.GetState>`.

   .. grpc:value:: PARACHUTE_STATE_STOWED

      Packed away.

   .. grpc:value:: PARACHUTE_STATE_ARMED

      Waiting to deploy at :grpc:rpc:`ParachuteService.GetDeployAltitude <krpc.ksa.ParachuteService.GetDeployAltitude>`.

   .. grpc:value:: PARACHUTE_STATE_LINE_STRETCH

      Leaving the pack.

   .. grpc:value:: PARACHUTE_STATE_REEFED

      Partly open, to limit the opening load.

   .. grpc:value:: PARACHUTE_STATE_DISREEFING

      Opening from reefed to full.

   .. grpc:value:: PARACHUTE_STATE_FULL

      Fully open.

   .. grpc:value:: PARACHUTE_STATE_CUT

      Cut away.

   .. grpc:value:: PARACHUTE_STATE_RIPPED

      Torn by too much dynamic pressure.

   .. grpc:value:: PARACHUTE_STATE_SEVERED_LINES

      Lost after its lines broke under too much load.
