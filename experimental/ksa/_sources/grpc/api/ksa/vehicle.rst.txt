Vehicle
=======

.. grpc:service:: VehicleService

   A vehicle in the current system. Obtained from :grpc:rpc:`KSAService.GetActiveVehicle <krpc.ksa.KSAService.GetActiveVehicle>`
   or :grpc:rpc:`KSAService.GetVehicles <krpc.ksa.KSAService.GetVehicles>`.

   .. note::

      Docking and undocking replace the vehicles involved, so an object for one of them
      throws an exception afterwards.

         * ``ut`` (``double``): Universal time of the burn, in seconds.
         * ``prograde`` (``optional float``): Change in velocity in the prograde direction, in meters per second. Defaults to ``0.0``.

         trajectory.

.. grpc:enum:: Situation

   The situation of a vehicle: its contact with the surface, and whether the game is
   simulating it with physics or propagating it along its orbit.
   See :grpc:rpc:`VehicleService.GetSituation <krpc.ksa.VehicleService.GetSituation>`.

   .. grpc:value:: SITUATION_MANEUVERING

      No surface contact, simulated with physics.

   .. grpc:value:: SITUATION_FREEFALL

      No surface contact, on rails.

   .. grpc:value:: SITUATION_ROLLING

      On terrain, simulated with physics.

   .. grpc:value:: SITUATION_LANDED

      At rest on terrain.

   .. grpc:value:: SITUATION_SAILING

      In the ocean, simulated with physics.

   .. grpc:value:: SITUATION_FLOATING

      At rest in the ocean.

   .. grpc:value:: SITUATION_DRAGGING

      On terrain and in the ocean, simulated with physics.

   .. grpc:value:: SITUATION_BOTTOMED

      At rest on terrain and in the ocean.
