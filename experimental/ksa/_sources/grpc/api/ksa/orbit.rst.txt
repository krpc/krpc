Orbit
=====

.. grpc:service:: OrbitService

   An orbit. Obtained from :grpc:rpc:`VehicleService.GetOrbit <krpc.ksa.VehicleService.GetOrbit>`, :grpc:rpc:`BodyService.GetOrbit <krpc.ksa.BodyService.GetOrbit>`,
   :grpc:rpc:`BurnService.GetOrbit <krpc.ksa.BurnService.GetOrbit>` or :grpc:rpc:`OrbitService.GetNextOrbit <krpc.ksa.OrbitService.GetNextOrbit>`.

   .. note::

      An orbit follows the game's prediction as the trajectory changes. A predicted orbit, from
      :grpc:rpc:`OrbitService.GetNextOrbit <krpc.ksa.OrbitService.GetNextOrbit>` or :grpc:rpc:`BurnService.GetOrbit <krpc.ksa.BurnService.GetOrbit>`, is a position in the vehicle's or the
      burn's predicted trajectory, and throws an exception while the trajectory is too short to
      reach it.

.. grpc:enum:: OrbitTransition

   How an orbit ends. See :grpc:rpc:`OrbitService.GetEndTransition <krpc.ksa.OrbitService.GetEndTransition>`.

   .. grpc:value:: ORBIT_TRANSITION_NONE

      The orbit does not end.

   .. grpc:value:: ORBIT_TRANSITION_ENCOUNTER

      The orbit enters the sphere of influence of another body.

   .. grpc:value:: ORBIT_TRANSITION_ESCAPE

      The orbit leaves the sphere of influence of the body being orbited.

   .. grpc:value:: ORBIT_TRANSITION_IMPACT

      The orbit hits the surface of the body being orbited.

   .. grpc:value:: ORBIT_TRANSITION_BURN

      The orbit ends at a planned burn.
