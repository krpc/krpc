Camera
======

.. grpc:service:: CameraService

   The game's main camera. Obtained from :grpc:rpc:`KSAService.GetCamera <krpc.ksa.KSAService.GetCamera>`.

   .. grpc:rpc:: GetMode (Camera.GetModeRequest) returns (Camera.GetModeResponse)

      The mode of the camera.

      :Camera.GetModeRequest:
         * ``this`` (:grpc:service:`Camera <krpc.ksa.CameraService>`)

      :Camera.GetModeResponse: ``value`` (:grpc:enum:`CameraMode <krpc.ksa.CameraMode>`)

      .. note::

         Setting :grpc:value:`CAMERA_MODE_IVA <krpc.ksa.CameraMode.CAMERA_MODE_IVA>` while the camera is not focused on a vehicle
         with a crew seat sets :grpc:value:`CAMERA_MODE_ORBIT <krpc.ksa.CameraMode.CAMERA_MODE_ORBIT>` instead, as the game does. A
         change of mode releases the active vehicle's held rotation and translation inputs.

   .. grpc:rpc:: GetDistance (Camera.GetDistanceRequest) returns (Camera.GetDistanceResponse)

      The distance from the camera to the focused object, in meters. Throws an exception
      in any mode other than orbit and map.

      :Camera.GetDistanceRequest:
         * ``this`` (:grpc:service:`Camera <krpc.ksa.CameraService>`)

      :Camera.GetDistanceResponse: ``value`` (``float``)

      .. note::

         The camera moves smoothly to a new distance, and stays at least the focused object's
         radius from it in map mode, and half its radius in orbit mode.

.. grpc:enum:: CameraMode

   The mode of the camera. See :grpc:rpc:`CameraService.GetMode <krpc.ksa.CameraService.GetMode>`.

   .. grpc:value:: CAMERA_MODE_ORBIT

      Orbits the focused object.

   .. grpc:value:: CAMERA_MODE_FREE

      Flies freely.

   .. grpc:value:: CAMERA_MODE_MAP

      The map view.

   .. grpc:value:: CAMERA_MODE_IVA

      Inside the vehicle, from a crew seat.

   .. grpc:value:: CAMERA_MODE_FIXED

      Fixed to a part of the vehicle, such as a docking port.
