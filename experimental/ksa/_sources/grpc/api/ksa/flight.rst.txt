.. grpc:enum:: OutsideAir

   Whether a kitten can breathe the air outside a vehicle. See
   :grpc:rpc:`FlightService.GetOutsideAir <krpc.ksa.FlightService.GetOutsideAir>`.

   .. grpc:value:: OUTSIDE_AIR_NO_ATMOSPHERE

      Outside the atmosphere, or at a body with none.

   .. grpc:value:: OUTSIDE_AIR_NO_OXYGEN

      An atmosphere with no oxygen.

   .. grpc:value:: OUTSIDE_AIR_TOO_THIN

      An atmosphere whose oxygen pressure is too low to breathe.

   .. grpc:value:: OUTSIDE_AIR_BREATHABLE

      Breathable air.
