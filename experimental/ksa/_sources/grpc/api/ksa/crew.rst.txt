.. grpc:enum:: LocomotionMode

   How a kitten on EVA is moving. See :grpc:rpc:`KittenService.GetLocomotionMode <krpc.ksa.KittenService.GetLocomotionMode>`.

   .. grpc:value:: LOCOMOTION_MODE_MMU

      Flying with the maneuvering unit, away from any surface.

   .. grpc:value:: LOCOMOTION_MODE_GROUNDED

      Standing or walking on a surface.

   .. grpc:value:: LOCOMOTION_MODE_AIRBORNE

      In the air above a surface, such as during a jump.

   .. grpc:value:: LOCOMOTION_MODE_TUMBLING

      Tumbling after a fall or an impact.

   .. grpc:value:: LOCOMOTION_MODE_RIGHTING

      Getting back up after tumbling.

   .. grpc:value:: LOCOMOTION_MODE_LADDER

      Holding a ladder or grab rail.

   .. grpc:value:: LOCOMOTION_MODE_SWIMMING

      Swimming in an ocean.
