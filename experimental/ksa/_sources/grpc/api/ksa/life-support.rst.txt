Life Support
============

.. grpc:service:: LifeSupportService

   How long the supplies of a vehicle, a habitat zone or a kitten's suit last. Obtained
   from :grpc:rpc:`VehicleService.GetLifeSupport <krpc.ksa.VehicleService.GetLifeSupport>`, :grpc:rpc:`HabitatZoneService.GetLifeSupport <krpc.ksa.HabitatZoneService.GetLifeSupport>` or
   :grpc:rpc:`KittenService.GetLifeSupport <krpc.ksa.KittenService.GetLifeSupport>`.

   .. note::

      The times count down only while :grpc:rpc:`KSAService.GetLifeSupportEnabled <krpc.ksa.KSAService.GetLifeSupportEnabled>` is true. Air
      lasts forever in breathable air outside the vehicle.

.. grpc:enum:: LifeSupportLevel

   How long the crew's supplies last. See :grpc:rpc:`LifeSupportService.GetLevel <krpc.ksa.LifeSupportService.GetLevel>`.

   .. grpc:value:: LIFE_SUPPORT_LEVEL_OK

      More than a day of supplies, or no crew.

   .. grpc:value:: LIFE_SUPPORT_LEVEL_LOW

      A day of supplies or less.

   .. grpc:value:: LIFE_SUPPORT_LEVEL_CRITICAL

      An hour of supplies or less.

   .. grpc:value:: LIFE_SUPPORT_LEVEL_OUT

      A supply has run out.

.. grpc:enum:: LifeSupportSupply

   A supply the crew needs. See :grpc:rpc:`LifeSupportService.GetLimitingSupply <krpc.ksa.LifeSupportService.GetLimitingSupply>`.

   .. grpc:value:: LIFE_SUPPORT_SUPPLY_AIR

      Oxygen to breathe.

   .. grpc:value:: LIFE_SUPPORT_SUPPLY_SCRUBBER

      Lithium hydroxide canisters, which scrub carbon dioxide from the air.

   .. grpc:value:: LIFE_SUPPORT_SUPPLY_WATER

      Drinking water.

   .. grpc:value:: LIFE_SUPPORT_SUPPLY_FOOD

      Meals.
