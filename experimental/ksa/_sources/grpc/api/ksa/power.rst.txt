.. grpc:service:: AvionicsService

   The avionics of a probe core, which radiation wears out and upsets. Obtained from
   :grpc:rpc:`PartService.GetAvionics <krpc.ksa.PartService.GetAvionics>` or :grpc:rpc:`VehicleService.GetAvionics <krpc.ksa.VehicleService.GetAvionics>`.

   .. note::

      The avionics take a dose only while :grpc:rpc:`KSAService.GetRadiationEnabled <krpc.ksa.KSAService.GetRadiationEnabled>` is true. Switched
      off or on standby, they take half the dose and no upsets.
