Camera
======


.. class:: Camera

   The game's main camera. Obtained from :attr:`KSA.camera <KSA.KSA.camera>`.

   .. attribute:: mode: KSA.CameraMode

      The mode of the camera.

      :Attribute: Can be read or written
      :rtype: :class:`KSA.CameraMode`

      .. note::

         Setting :attr:`KSA.CameraMode.iva` while the camera is not focused on a vehicle
         with a crew seat sets :attr:`KSA.CameraMode.orbit` instead, as the game does. A
         change of mode releases the active vehicle's held rotation and translation inputs.

   .. attribute:: azimuth: number

      The angle of the camera around the focused object, in degrees. Throws an exception
      in any mode other than orbit and map.

      :Attribute: Can be read or written
      :rtype: number

   .. attribute:: elevation: number

      The angle of the camera above the plane it orbits in, in degrees, between -90 and
      90. Throws an exception in any mode other than orbit and map.

      :Attribute: Can be read or written
      :rtype: number

   .. attribute:: distance: number

      The distance from the camera to the focused object, in meters. Throws an exception
      in any mode other than orbit and map.

      :Attribute: Can be read or written
      :rtype: number

      .. note::

         The camera moves smoothly to a new distance, and stays at least the focused object's
         radius from it in map mode, and half its radius in orbit mode.

   .. attribute:: field_of_view: number

      The camera's vertical field of view, in degrees, between 15 and 120.

      :Attribute: Can be read or written
      :rtype: number

   .. attribute:: focused_vehicle: KSA.Vehicle

      Returns the vehicle the camera is focused on, or ``nil`` if it is focused on
      something else, and sets the focus to a vehicle.

      :Attribute: Can be read or written
      :rtype: :class:`KSA.Vehicle` or ``Types.none``

      .. note::

         Setting the focus leaves the active vehicle as it is.

   .. attribute:: focused_body: KSA.Body

      Returns the body the camera is focused on, or ``nil`` if it is focused on
      something else, and sets the focus to a body.

      :Attribute: Can be read or written
      :rtype: :class:`KSA.Body` or ``Types.none``

   .. attribute:: focused_kitten: KSA.Kitten

      Returns the kitten on EVA the camera is focused on, or ``nil`` if it is focused
      on something else, and sets the focus to a kitten on EVA.

      :Attribute: Can be read or written
      :rtype: :class:`KSA.Kitten` or ``Types.none``

      .. note::

         Throws an exception if the kitten is not on EVA.



.. class:: CameraMode

   The mode of the camera. See :attr:`KSA.Camera.mode`.


   .. data:: orbit

      Orbits the focused object.


   .. data:: free

      Flies freely.


   .. data:: map

      The map view.


   .. data:: iva

      Inside the vehicle, from a crew seat.


   .. data:: fixed

      Fixed to a part of the vehicle, such as a docking port.
