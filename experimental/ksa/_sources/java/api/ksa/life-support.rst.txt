Life Support
============


.. type:: public class LifeSupport

   How long the supplies of a vehicle, a habitat zone or a kitten's suit last. Obtained
   from :meth:`Vehicle.getLifeSupport()`, :meth:`HabitatZone.getLifeSupport()` or
   :meth:`Kitten.getLifeSupport()`.

   .. note::

      The times count down only while :meth:`KSA.getLifeSupportEnabled()` is true. Air
      lasts forever in breathable air outside the vehicle.

   .. method:: int getKittens()


      The number of kittens the supplies are shared between.

   

   .. method:: float getAirMass()


      The mass of oxygen in the crew's tanks, in kilograms.

   

   .. method:: float getWaterMass()


      The mass of water in the crew's tanks, in kilograms.

   

   .. method:: int getMeals()


      The number of meals.

   

   .. method:: float getAirTime()


      The time the air lasts, in seconds. Infinite with no crew, or in breathable air.

   

   .. method:: float getScrubberTime()


      The time the carbon dioxide scrubbers last, in seconds. Infinite with no crew.

   

   .. method:: float getWaterTime()


      The time the water lasts, in seconds. Infinite with no crew.

   

   .. method:: float getFoodTime()


      The time the food lasts, in seconds. Infinite with no crew.

   

   .. method:: float getTime()


      The time until the first supply runs out, in seconds. A suit counts its air and
      scrubber only. Infinite with no crew.

   

   .. method:: LifeSupportSupply getLimitingSupply()


      The supply that runs out first. Air when every time is infinite, as with no crew.

   

   .. method:: LifeSupportLevel getLevel()


      How long the supplies last, as the game's life support gauge shows it.

   

   .. method:: float getTimeToNextLevel()


      The time until :meth:`LifeSupport.getLevel()` next changes, in seconds. Infinite with no crew.

   



.. type:: public enum LifeSupportLevel

   How long the crew's supplies last. See :meth:`LifeSupport.getLevel()`.


   .. field:: public LifeSupportLevel OK

      More than a day of supplies, or no crew.


   .. field:: public LifeSupportLevel LOW

      A day of supplies or less.


   .. field:: public LifeSupportLevel CRITICAL

      An hour of supplies or less.


   .. field:: public LifeSupportLevel OUT

      A supply has run out.



.. type:: public enum LifeSupportSupply

   A supply the crew needs. See :meth:`LifeSupport.getLimitingSupply()`.


   .. field:: public LifeSupportSupply AIR

      Oxygen to breathe.


   .. field:: public LifeSupportSupply SCRUBBER

      Lithium hydroxide canisters, which scrub carbon dioxide from the air.


   .. field:: public LifeSupportSupply WATER

      Drinking water.


   .. field:: public LifeSupportSupply FOOD

      Meals.



.. type:: public class HabitatZone

   The connected habitable parts of a vehicle, which share their crew's supplies.
   Obtained from :meth:`Vehicle.getHabitatZones()` or :meth:`Habitat.getZone()`.

   .. note::

      Parts join a zone through connections the crew can pass, such as an open docking
      port hatch.

   .. method:: Vehicle getVehicle()


      The vehicle the zone is in.

   

   .. method:: java.util.List<Part> getParts()


      The parts in the zone.

   

   .. method:: java.util.List<Habitat> getHabitats()


      The habitats in the zone.

   

   .. method:: java.util.List<Seat> getSeats()


      The crew seats in the zone.

   

   .. method:: java.util.List<Kitten> getCrew()


      The kittens in the zone's seats.

   

   .. method:: java.util.List<HabitatZone> getLinkedZones()


      The zones that join this one when closed docking port hatches between them open.

   

   .. method:: java.util.List<DockingPort> hatchesTo(HabitatZone zone)

      The closed docking port hatches that join this zone to a linked zone when they
      open.

      :param HabitatZone zone: A zone in :meth:`HabitatZone.getLinkedZones()`.
   

      .. note::

         Throws an exception if the zone is not linked to this one.

   .. method:: float getHabitableVolume()


      The volume the crew can live in, in cubic meters.

   

   .. method:: float getStorageVolume()


      The volume for stored supplies, in cubic meters.

   

   .. method:: LifeSupport getLifeSupport()


      How long the zone's supplies last its crew.

   

   .. method:: MissionSupplies getMissionSupplies()


      Whether the zone's supplies last its crew through the vehicle's mission. ``null``
      without a feasible mission with time left, or while
      :meth:`KSA.getLifeSupportEnabled()` is false.

   

   .. method:: int plannedKittens(MissionLeg leg)

      The number of kittens planned in the zone for a leg of the vehicle's mission. The
      plan defaults to the kittens seated in the zone.

      :param MissionLeg leg: The leg.
   



.. type:: public class Mission

   The mission planned for a vehicle in the editor's life support window. Obtained from
   :meth:`Vehicle.getMission()`.

   .. note::

      The game times the legs of the mission from the plan, and checks the supplies of each
      habitat zone against them. See :meth:`HabitatZone.getMissionSupplies()`.

   .. method:: MissionDestination getDestination()


      Where the mission goes.

   

   .. method:: Body getBody()


      The body the mission goes to, or ``null`` if it has no destination. For a low
      orbit, this is the home body.

   

   .. method:: MissionArrival getArrival()


      What the mission does at its destination.

   

   .. method:: MissionTrip getTrip()


      Whether the mission comes back.

   

   .. method:: float getStay()


      The time planned at the destination, in seconds.

   

      .. note::

         The game rounds a stay before a return up to the next transfer window home. See
         :meth:`Mission.getStayTime()`.

   .. method:: boolean getCustom()


      Whether the mission has a length set by the player, in place of legs timed from
      its destination. See :meth:`Mission.getCustomLength()`.

   

   .. method:: float getCustomLength()


      The length of a mission with :meth:`Mission.getCustom()` set, in seconds.

   

   .. method:: boolean getFeasible()


      Whether the game can time the legs of the mission. A mission with no destination,
      or a flyby with no way back, cannot be timed.

   

      .. note::

         The leg times are zero when this is false.

   .. method:: float getOutTime()


      The planned time of the way out, in seconds.

   

   .. method:: float getStayTime()


      The planned time at the destination, in seconds.

   

   .. method:: float getBackTime()


      The planned time of the way back, in seconds. Zero for a one way trip.

   

   .. method:: float getReturnWait()


      The time from arriving at the destination until the next transfer window home, in
      seconds. Zero when there is no wait.

   

   .. method:: float getTotalTime()


      The planned length of the mission, in seconds.

   

   .. method:: float getTimeLeft()


      The time left in the mission as planned, counted from the vehicle's launch, in
      seconds.

   

   .. method:: double getArrivalUT()


      The universal time the vehicle arrived at the destination body, in seconds. Not a
      number until it has.

   

      .. note::

         The game records the arrival for a mission to another body.

   .. method:: double getDepartureUT()


      The universal time the vehicle left the destination body, in seconds. Not a number
      until it has.

   .. field:: public MissionDestination LOW_ORBIT

   .. field:: public MissionDestination BODY

.. type:: public enum MissionArrival

   .. field:: public MissionArrival FLYBY

   .. field:: public MissionArrival ORBIT

      Orbits it.


   .. field:: public MissionArrival LAND

.. type:: public enum MissionTrip

   Whether a mission comes back. See :meth:`Mission.getTrip()`.


   .. field:: public MissionTrip RETURN

      Goes and comes back.


   .. field:: public MissionTrip ONE_WAY

.. type:: public enum MissionLeg

   A leg of a mission. See :meth:`HabitatZone.plannedKittens(MissionLeg)`.


   .. field:: public MissionLeg OUT

      The way out.


   .. field:: public MissionLeg STAY

      The stay at the destination.


   .. field:: public MissionLeg BACK

      The way back.



.. type:: public class MissionSupplies

   Whether the supplies of a habitat zone last its crew through the vehicle's mission, as
   the life support window shows it. Obtained from :meth:`HabitatZone.getMissionSupplies()`.

   .. note::

      The check takes the legs left in the mission as planned, counted from the vehicle's
      launch, and the kittens planned for each leg. The times are from now.

   .. method:: MissionSupplyVerdict getVerdict()


      Whether the supplies last the mission.

   

   .. method:: float getShortBy()


      The time by which the first supply to run out falls short of the end of the
      mission, in seconds. Zero when the supplies last.

   

   .. method:: LifeSupportSupply getFirstSupply()


      The supply that runs out first.

   

   .. method:: float timeLeft(LifeSupportSupply supply)

      The time until a supply runs out with the crew planned for each leg, in seconds.
      Infinite if it lasts the mission.

      :param LifeSupportSupply supply: The supply.
   

      .. note::

         Air lasts while the vehicle is in breathable air.

   .. method:: float getEnd()


      The time until the zone has no crew left in the mission, in seconds.

   



.. type:: public enum MissionSupplyVerdict

   Whether a habitat zone has the supplies for a mission. See :meth:`MissionSupplies.getVerdict()`.


   .. field:: public MissionSupplyVerdict UNUSED

      The zone has no crew during the mission.


   .. field:: public MissionSupplyVerdict ENOUGH

      The supplies last the mission.


   .. field:: public MissionSupplyVerdict SHORT

      A supply runs out before the end of the mission.



.. type:: public class Habitat

   A habitable or storage space in a part, which holds meals and carbon dioxide scrubber
   canisters. Obtained from :meth:`Part.getHabitats()`.

   .. method:: Part getPart()


      The part the habitat is in.

   

   .. method:: HabitatZone getZone()


      The habitat zone the habitat is in.

   

   .. method:: float getHabitableVolume()


      The volume the crew can live in, in cubic meters.

   

   .. method:: float getStorageVolume()


      The volume for stored supplies, in cubic meters.

   

   .. method:: int getMeals()


      The number of meals in the habitat.

   

   .. method:: int getCanisters()


      The number of carbon dioxide scrubber canisters in the habitat.

   

   .. method:: int getMealCapacity()


      The number of meals the habitat holds when full.

   

   .. method:: int getCanisterCapacity()


      The number of carbon dioxide scrubber canisters the habitat holds when full.

   

   .. method:: void transfer(Habitat to, int meals, int canisters)

      Moves meals and canisters to another habitat in the same zone. Moves fewer when
      this habitat holds fewer, or the other has less room.

      :param Habitat to: The habitat to move the supplies to.
      :param int meals: The number of meals to move.
      :param int canisters: The number of canisters to move.
   

      .. note::

         A kitten in the zone moves the supplies. Throws an exception if life support is
         disabled, the habitats are in different zones, or the zone has no crew able to
         move them.
