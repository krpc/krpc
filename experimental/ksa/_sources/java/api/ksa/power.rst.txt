.. package:: krpc.client.services.KSA

Power
=====


.. type:: public class Battery

   A battery, which stores electrical energy. Obtained from :meth:`Part.getBatteries()`.

   .. method:: Part getPart()


      The part the battery is in.

   

   .. method:: float getStoredEnergy()


      The energy stored in the battery, in joules.

   

   .. method:: float getEnergyCapacity()


      The energy the battery can store, in joules.

   



.. type:: public class SolarPanel

   A solar panel, which turns sunlight into electrical power. Obtained from :meth:`Part.getSolarPanels()`.

   .. method:: Part getPart()


      The part the solar panel is in.

   

   .. method:: boolean getActive()


      Whether the solar panel can produce power, because it is deployed or does not deploy.

   

   .. method:: float getPower()


      The power the solar panel produces, in watts.

   

   .. method:: float getSunAngle()


      The angle between the solar panel's face and the direction to the sun, in degrees.
      Zero when the panel faces the sun.

   

   .. method:: boolean getOccluded()


      Whether a body blocks the sunlight to the solar panel.

   

   .. method:: Deployable getDeployable()


      The deployable section that opens the solar panel, or ``null`` for a panel that
      does not deploy.

   



.. type:: public class Generator

   A generator, which produces electrical power. Obtained from :meth:`Part.getGenerators()`.

   .. note::

      A generator runs while its :meth:`Generator.getEngine()` is active, and produces power in
      proportion to the engine's throttle.

   .. method:: Part getPart()


      The part the generator is in.

   

   .. method:: Engine getEngine()


      The engine that drives the generator, or ``null`` if it has none.

   

   .. method:: boolean getActive()


      Whether the generator is producing power.

   

   .. method:: float getPower()


      The power the generator produces, in watts.

   



.. type:: public class PowerConsumer

   A power consumer, which uses electrical power, such as a light. Obtained from
   :meth:`Part.getPowerConsumers()`.

   .. method:: Part getPart()


      The part the power consumer is in.

   

   .. method:: boolean getActive()


      Whether the part is using power.

   

   .. method:: float getPower()


      The power the part uses, in watts.

   



.. type:: public class Light

   A light. Obtained from :meth:`Part.getLights()`.

   .. note::

      The vehicle's lights switch on and off together, see :meth:`Control.getLights()`.
      Cabin lights are always on.

   .. method:: Part getPart()


      The part the light is in.

   

   .. method:: boolean getActive()


      Whether the light is on.
