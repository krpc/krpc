Vehicle
=======


.. type:: public class Vehicle

   A vehicle in the current system. Obtained from :meth:`getActiveVehicle()`
   or :meth:`getVehicles()`.

   .. note::

      Docking and undocking replace the vehicles involved, so an object for one of them
      throws an exception afterwards.

   .. method:: String getName()

   .. method:: void setName(String value)

      The name of the vehicle, which is unique in the system.

   

   .. method:: void destroy()

      Destroy the vehicle, killing its crew, as abandoning the vehicle does. Returns once
      the vehicle is gone.

   

      .. note::

         If the vehicle is the active vehicle, the game hands control to a nearby vehicle.

   .. method:: boolean getCanRecover()


      Whether the vehicle can be recovered: at rest on the surface of the home planet.

   

   .. method:: void recover()

      Recover the vehicle, ending its crew's missions, as the game's recover button does.
      Returns once the vehicle is gone.

   

      .. note::

         Throws an exception if the vehicle cannot be recovered, see :meth:`Vehicle.getCanRecover()`.

   .. method:: Situation getSituation()


      The situation of the vehicle.

   

   .. method:: boolean getOnRails()


      Whether the game moves the vehicle without simulating the forces on it: along its
      orbit, or held on the surface. True in the freefall, landed, floating and bottomed
      situations.

   

   .. method:: boolean getIsDebris()


      Whether the vehicle is debris, such as a spent stage.

   

   .. method:: boolean getHasLaunched()


      Whether the vehicle has launched.

   

   .. method:: double getLaunchTime()


      The universal time the vehicle was created, in seconds.

   

   .. method:: float getMass()


      The total mass of the vehicle, including propellant, in kilograms.

   

   .. method:: float getPropellantMass()


      The mass of the propellant in the vehicle, in kilograms.

   

   .. method:: float getThrust()


      The thrust of the engines at their current throttle and the current atmospheric
      pressure, in Newtons.

   

   .. method:: float getAvailableThrust()


      The thrust of the active engines that have propellant, at full throttle and the
      current atmospheric pressure, in Newtons.

   

   .. method:: float getSpecificImpulse()


      The combined specific impulse of the active engines that have propellant, at the
      current atmospheric pressure, in seconds. Zero when no engine is active.

   

   .. method:: float getPeakGLoad()


      The highest smoothed g-load on the vehicle during the last physics step, as a
      multiple of standard gravity. Zero while the vehicle is :meth:`Vehicle.getOnRails()`.

   

   .. method:: float getMaxGLoad()


      The g-load the vehicle can take before it breaks up, as a multiple of standard
      gravity. Zero while the vehicle is :meth:`Vehicle.getOnRails()`.

   

   .. method:: float getPeakDynamicPressure()


      The highest dynamic pressure on the vehicle during the last physics step, in
      pascals. In the sea, this is the dynamic pressure of the water. Zero while the
      vehicle is :meth:`Vehicle.getOnRails()`.

   

   .. method:: float getMaxDynamicPressure()


      The dynamic pressure the vehicle can take before it breaks up, in pascals. Zero
      while the vehicle is :meth:`Vehicle.getOnRails()`.

   

   .. method:: java.util.List<Part> getParts()


      The vehicle's parts.

   

   .. method:: Part getRootPart()


      The vehicle's root part, which the other parts are attached to.

   

   .. method:: java.util.List<Part> partsWithName(String name)

      The vehicle's parts with the given template id. See :meth:`Part.getName()`.

      :param String name: The template id.
   

   .. method:: java.util.List<Seat> getSeats()


      The vehicle's crew seats.

   

   .. method:: java.util.List<Kitten> getCrew()


      The kittens in the vehicle's seats.

   

   .. method:: int getCrewCapacity()


      The number of crew seats in the vehicle.

   

   .. method:: java.util.List<Engine> getEngines()


      The vehicle's engines.

   

   .. method:: java.util.List<RCS> getRCS()


      The vehicle's RCS thrusters.

   

   .. method:: java.util.List<Decoupler> getDecouplers()


      The vehicle's decouplers.

   

   .. method:: java.util.List<Parachute> getParachutes()


      The vehicle's parachutes.

   

   .. method:: java.util.List<Tank> getTanks()


      The vehicle's propellant tanks.

   

   .. method:: java.util.List<Battery> getBatteries()


      The vehicle's batteries.

   

   .. method:: java.util.List<SolarPanel> getSolarPanels()


      The vehicle's solar panels.

   

   .. method:: java.util.List<Generator> getGenerators()


      The vehicle's generators.

   

   .. method:: java.util.List<PowerConsumer> getPowerConsumers()


      The vehicle's power consumers.

   

   .. method:: float getStoredEnergy()


      The energy stored in the vehicle's batteries, in joules.

   

   .. method:: float getEnergyCapacity()


      The energy the vehicle's batteries can store, in joules.

   

   .. method:: float getNetPower()


      The power produced by the vehicle's solar panels and generators, less the power its
      parts use, in watts.

   

   .. method:: java.util.List<Light> getLights()


      The vehicle's lights.

   

   .. method:: java.util.List<Deployable> getDeployables()


      The vehicle's deployable sections.

   

   .. method:: java.util.List<MagneticScoop> getMagneticScoops()


      The vehicle's magnetic scoops.

   

   .. method:: java.util.List<FuelLink> getFuelLinks()


      The vehicle's fuel links.

   

   .. method:: java.util.List<DockingPort> getDockingPorts()


      The vehicle's docking ports.

   

   .. method:: java.util.List<Resource> getResources()


      The resources the vehicle holds, one for each substance, totaled over its parts.

   

   .. method:: float getDeltaV()


      The change in velocity of all the vehicle's sequences, in meters per second.

   

   .. method:: java.util.List<Sequence> getSequences()


      The vehicle's sequences, in activation order.

   

   .. method:: Vehicle getTargetVehicle()

   .. method:: void setTargetVehicle(Vehicle value)

      The vehicle targeted by this vehicle, or ``null``. Setting it replaces the
      target, and ``null`` clears it.

   

   .. method:: Body getTargetBody()

   .. method:: void setTargetBody(Body value)

      The body targeted by this vehicle, or ``null``. Setting it replaces the target,
      and ``null`` clears it.

   

      .. note::

         Setting a body that orbits nothing, such as the star, throws an exception.

   .. method:: Part getTargetPart()

   .. method:: void setTargetPart(Part value)

      The part targeted by this vehicle, on the vehicle in :meth:`Vehicle.getTargetVehicle()`,
      or ``null``. Setting it targets the part and its vehicle, and ``null`` clears
      the target.

   

   .. method:: java.util.List<Burn> getBurns()


      The vehicle's planned burns, in time order.

   

   .. method:: Burn addBurn(double ut, float prograde, float normal, float radial)

      Plan a burn, and add it to the vehicle's burn plan.

      :param double ut: Universal time of the burn, in seconds.
      :param float prograde: Change in velocity in the prograde direction, in meters per second.
      :param float normal: Change in velocity along the orbit normal, in meters per second.
      :param float radial: Change in velocity radially outward, in meters per second.
      :returns: The burn.
   

      .. note::

         Returns once the flight computer has timed the burn, if it is the next one. Throws
         an exception if the time is in the past, or beyond the vehicle's predicted
         trajectory.

   .. method:: Orbit getOrbit()


      The current orbit of the vehicle.

   

   .. method:: Flight getFlight()


      Flight telemetry for the vehicle, relative to the surface of the body it orbits.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: Control getControl()


      The controls of the vehicle.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: AutoPilot getAutoPilot()


      The auto-pilot of the vehicle.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: ReferenceFrame getReferenceFrame()


      The reference frame fixed to the vehicle, with its origin at the vehicle's position.
      The x axis points forward, the y axis to the right and the z axis down, relative to
      the vehicle's control point.

   

   .. method:: ReferenceFrame getSurfaceReferenceFrame()


      The reference frame with its origin at the vehicle's position, and axes aligned with
      the surface below it. The x axis points east, the y axis north and the z axis up.
      The frame rotates with the body.

   

   .. method:: ReferenceFrame getOrbitalReferenceFrame()


      The reference frame with its origin at the vehicle's position, and axes aligned with
      its orbit. The x axis points prograde, the y axis along the orbit normal and the z
      axis radially outward, perpendicular to the x axis.

   

   .. method:: org.javatuples.Triplet<Double,Double,Double> position(ReferenceFrame referenceFrame)

      The position of the vehicle, in meters.

      :param ReferenceFrame referenceFrame: The reference frame of the position.
   

   .. method:: org.javatuples.Triplet<Double,Double,Double> velocity(ReferenceFrame referenceFrame)

      The velocity of the vehicle, in meters per second.

      :param ReferenceFrame referenceFrame: The reference frame of the velocity.
   

   .. method:: org.javatuples.Triplet<Double,Double,Double> direction(ReferenceFrame referenceFrame)

      The unit vector in which the vehicle points, the x axis of
      :meth:`Vehicle.getReferenceFrame()`.

      :param ReferenceFrame referenceFrame: The reference frame of the direction.
   

   .. method:: org.javatuples.Quartet<Double,Double,Double,Double> rotation(ReferenceFrame referenceFrame)

      The rotation of :meth:`Vehicle.getReferenceFrame()` relative to the given frame.

      :param ReferenceFrame referenceFrame: The reference frame of the rotation.
   

   .. method:: org.javatuples.Triplet<Double,Double,Double> angularVelocity(ReferenceFrame referenceFrame)

      The angular velocity of the vehicle, in radians per second. The vector points along
      the axis of rotation, and its length is the rate of rotation.

      :param ReferenceFrame referenceFrame: The reference frame of the angular velocity.
   



.. type:: public enum Situation

   The situation of a vehicle: its contact with the surface, and whether the game is
   simulating it with physics or propagating it along its orbit.
   See :meth:`Vehicle.getSituation()`.


   .. field:: public Situation MANEUVERING

      No surface contact, simulated with physics.


   .. field:: public Situation FREEFALL

      No surface contact, on rails.


   .. field:: public Situation ROLLING

      On terrain, simulated with physics.


   .. field:: public Situation LANDED

      At rest on terrain.


   .. field:: public Situation SAILING

      In the ocean, simulated with physics.


   .. field:: public Situation FLOATING

      At rest in the ocean.


   .. field:: public Situation DRAGGING

      On terrain and in the ocean, simulated with physics.


   .. field:: public Situation BOTTOMED

      At rest on terrain and in the ocean.



.. type:: public class VehicleDesign

   A vehicle design that can be launched: one of the game's default vehicles, or a vehicle
   saved by the player in the editor. Obtained from :meth:`getVehicleDesigns()`.

   .. note::

      The mass and delta-v are those the editor shows for the design.

   .. method:: String getName()


      The name of the design, as passed to :meth:`launchVehicle(String, String)`.

   

   .. method:: boolean getIsDefault()


      Whether the design is one of the game's default vehicles.

   

   .. method:: int getPartCount()


      The number of parts in the design.

   

   .. method:: float getMass()


      The total mass of the design, including propellant, in kilograms.

   

   .. method:: float getDryMass()


      The mass of the design without propellant, in kilograms.

   

   .. method:: float getDeltaV()


      The change in velocity of all the design's sequences, in meters per second.

   

      .. note::

         Each sequence is computed in the environment chosen for it in the editor, in vacuum
         or at sea level.
