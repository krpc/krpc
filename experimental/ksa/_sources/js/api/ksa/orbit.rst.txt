Orbit
=====


.. class:: Orbit

   An orbit. Obtained from :meth:`KSA.Vehicle.getOrbit`, :meth:`KSA.Body.getOrbit`,
   :meth:`KSA.Burn.getOrbit` or :meth:`KSA.Orbit.getNextOrbit`.

   .. note::

      An orbit follows the game's prediction as the trajectory changes. A predicted orbit, from
      :meth:`KSA.Orbit.getNextOrbit` or :meth:`KSA.Burn.getOrbit`, is a position in the vehicle's or the
      burn's predicted trajectory, and throws an exception while the trajectory is too short to
      reach it.

   .. method:: utAtTrueAnomaly(trueAnomaly)

      The next universal time, from now, at which the orbit reaches the given true
      anomaly, in seconds. Not a number if it never does, such as beyond the asymptote of
      a hyperbolic orbit.

      :param trueAnomaly: True anomaly, in radians.
      :type trueAnomaly: ``number``
      :rtype: ``number``
   

   .. method:: trueAnomalyAtRadius(radius)

      The true anomaly at which the orbit reaches the given distance from the center of
      the body, in radians, between 0 and :math:`\pi`. Not a number if the orbit
      never reaches it.

      :param radius: The distance from the center of the body, in meters.
      :type radius: ``number``
      :rtype: ``number``
   

      .. note::

         The orbit also reaches the distance at the negative of this anomaly. Zero for a
         circular orbit at the distance. Throws an exception if the distance is not
         positive.

   .. function:: createFromElements(body, semiMajorAxis, eccentricity, inclination, longitudeOfAscendingNode, argumentOfPeriapsis, meanAnomalyAtEpoch, epoch)

      Create the orbit with the given elements around a body. The angles are those of
      :class:`KSA.Orbit`.

      :param body: The body being orbited.
      :type body: :class:`KSA.Body`
      :param semiMajorAxis: The semi-major axis, in meters. Negative or positive for a hyperbolic orbit.
      :type semiMajorAxis: ``number``
      :param eccentricity: The eccentricity. Below 1 for an elliptical orbit, and above 1 for a hyperbolic one.
      :type eccentricity: ``number``
      :param inclination: The inclination, in radians.
      :type inclination: ``number``
      :param longitudeOfAscendingNode: The longitude of the ascending node, in radians.
      :type longitudeOfAscendingNode: ``number``
      :param argumentOfPeriapsis: The argument of periapsis, in radians.
      :type argumentOfPeriapsis: ``number``
      :param meanAnomalyAtEpoch: The mean anomaly at the epoch, in radians.
      :type meanAnomalyAtEpoch: ``number``
      :param epoch: The universal time of the epoch, in seconds.
      :type epoch: ``number``
      :rtype: :class:`KSA.Orbit`
   

      .. note::

         The orbit is a single conic around *body*, with no
         :meth:`KSA.Orbit.getNextOrbit`. It is held until :meth:`KSA.Orbit.remove` is called on it, or
         the client that created it disconnects.

   .. function:: createFromPositionAndVelocity(body, position, velocity, ut, referenceFrame)

      Create the orbit around a body through the given position and velocity.

      :param body: The body being orbited.
      :type body: :class:`KSA.Body`
      :param position: The position, in meters, relative to where the body is now.
      :type position: ``[number, number, number]``
      :param velocity: The velocity, in meters per second.
      :type velocity: ``[number, number, number]``
      :param ut: The universal time of the position and velocity, in seconds.
      :type ut: ``number``
      :param referenceFrame: The reference frame of the position and velocity.
      :type referenceFrame: :class:`KSA.ReferenceFrame`
      :rtype: :class:`KSA.Orbit`
   

      .. note::

         The orbit is a single conic around *body*, with no
         :meth:`KSA.Orbit.getNextOrbit`. It is held until :meth:`KSA.Orbit.remove` is called on it, or
         the client that created it disconnects.

   .. method:: remove()

      Remove an orbit created by :meth:`KSA.Orbit.createFromElements` or
      :meth:`KSA.Orbit.createFromPositionAndVelocity`.

   

      .. note::

         Throws an exception if the orbit was not created by a client.
