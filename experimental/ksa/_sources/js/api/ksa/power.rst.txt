Power
=====


.. class:: Battery

   A battery, which stores electrical energy. Obtained from :meth:`KSA.Part.getBatteries`.

   .. method:: getPart()

      The part the battery is in.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: :class:`KSA.Part`
   

   .. method:: getStoredEnergy()

      The energy stored in the battery, in joules.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``number``
   

   .. method:: getEnergyCapacity()

      The energy the battery can store, in joules.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``number``
   



.. class:: SolarPanel

   A solar panel, which turns sunlight into electrical power. Obtained from :meth:`KSA.Part.getSolarPanels`.

   .. method:: getPart()

      The part the solar panel is in.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: :class:`KSA.Part`
   

   .. method:: getActive()

      Whether the solar panel can produce power, because it is deployed or does not deploy.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``boolean``
   

   .. method:: getPower()

      The power the solar panel produces, in watts.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``number``
   

   .. method:: getSunAngle()

      The angle between the solar panel's face and the direction to the sun, in degrees.
      Zero when the panel faces the sun.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``number``
   

   .. method:: getOccluded()

      Whether a body blocks the sunlight to the solar panel.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``boolean``
   

   .. method:: getDeployable()

      The deployable section that opens the solar panel, or ``null`` for a panel that
      does not deploy.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: :class:`KSA.Deployable` or ``null``
   



.. class:: Generator

   A generator, which produces electrical power. Obtained from :meth:`KSA.Part.getGenerators`.

   .. note::

      A generator runs while its :meth:`KSA.Generator.getEngine` is active, and produces power in
      proportion to the engine's throttle.

   .. method:: getPart()

      The part the generator is in.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: :class:`KSA.Part`
   

   .. method:: getEngine()

      The engine that drives the generator, or ``null`` if it has none.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: :class:`KSA.Engine` or ``null``
   

   .. method:: getActive()

      Whether the generator is producing power.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``boolean``
   

   .. method:: getPower()

      The power the generator produces, in watts.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``number``
   



.. class:: PowerConsumer

   A power consumer, which uses electrical power, such as a light. Obtained from
   :meth:`KSA.Part.getPowerConsumers`.

   .. method:: getPart()

      The part the power consumer is in.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: :class:`KSA.Part`
   

   .. method:: getActive()

      Whether the part is using power.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``boolean``
   

   .. method:: getPower()

      The power the part uses, in watts.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``number``
   

   .. method:: getHasPowerSwitch()

      Whether the part has a power switch, as a probe core does.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``boolean``
   

   .. method:: getMode()
               setMode(value)

      The setting of the power switch.

      :Property: Can be read or written
      :rtype: :class:`KSA.PowerMode`
   

      .. note::

         Throws an exception if the part has no power switch.

   .. method:: getStandbyInStorms()
               setStandbyInStorms(value)

      Whether the part goes on standby, drawing no power, while a radiation storm is under
      way at the vehicle.

      :Property: Can be read or written
      :rtype: ``boolean``
   

      .. note::

         The game takes a storm of level 1 or worse on the S scale, as
         :meth:`KSA.Flight.getRadiationStormLevel` gives. Throws an exception if the part has
         no power switch.

   .. method:: getStatus()

      The power status of the part.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: :class:`KSA.PowerStatus`
   

      .. note::

         Throws an exception if the part has no power switch.



.. class:: PowerMode

   The setting of a power switch. See :meth:`KSA.PowerConsumer.getMode`.


   .. attribute:: off

      Switched off.


   .. attribute:: auto

      On standby while another control module flies the vehicle, and on when none can.


   .. attribute:: on

      Switched on.



.. class:: PowerStatus

   The power status of a part with a power switch. See :meth:`KSA.PowerConsumer.getStatus`.


   .. attribute:: off

      Switched off.


   .. attribute:: standby

      On standby, while another control module flies the vehicle or a radiation storm is under way.


   .. attribute:: rebooting

      Rebooting after a radiation upset.


   .. attribute:: on

      On, with power.


   .. attribute:: low

      On, with power for less than five minutes.


   .. attribute:: out

      Out of power.


   .. attribute:: failed

      Failed from its radiation dose.



.. class:: PowerForecast

   The charge, draw and supply of the electrical circuit a part is on, and how long its
   batteries last. Obtained from :meth:`KSA.Part.getPowerForecast`.

   .. note::

      A circuit is the parts joined by connections that carry electricity. This is the
      forecast the game shows for a probe core.

   .. method:: getHasBattery()

      Whether the circuit has a battery.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``boolean``
   

   .. method:: getCharge()

      The energy stored in the circuit's batteries, in joules.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``number``
   

   .. method:: getDraw()

      The power the parts on the circuit draw, in watts.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``number``
   

   .. method:: getSupply()

      The power the solar panels and generators on the circuit supply, in watts.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``number``
   

   .. method:: getTimeLeft()

      The time until the batteries run out, in seconds. Infinite while the supply meets
      the draw, and zero when the batteries are empty or there are none.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``number``
   



.. class:: Avionics

   The avionics of a probe core, which radiation wears out and upsets. Obtained from
   :meth:`KSA.Part.getAvionics` or :meth:`KSA.Vehicle.getAvionics`.

   .. note::

      The avionics take a dose only while :func:`KSA.getRadiationEnabled` is true. Switched
      off or on standby, they take half the dose and no upsets.

   .. method:: getPart()

      The part the avionics are in.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: :class:`KSA.Part`
   

   .. method:: getHealth()

      The health of the avionics.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: :class:`KSA.AvionicsHealth`
   

   .. method:: getDose()

      The radiation dose the avionics have taken, in grays of silicon.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``number``
   

   .. method:: getTolerance()

      The dose at which the avionics fail, in grays of silicon.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``number``
   

   .. method:: getDoseRate()

      The rate at which the avionics take a dose, in grays of silicon per second,
      averaged over about an hour. Not a number until the avionics have taken a dose.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``number``
   

   .. method:: getTimeLeft()

      The time until the avionics fail at :meth:`KSA.Avionics.getDoseRate`, in seconds. Infinite
      while they take no dose, and zero once they have failed.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``number``
   

   .. method:: getRebooting()

      Whether the avionics are rebooting after a radiation upset.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``boolean``
   



.. class:: AvionicsHealth

   The health of avionics. See :meth:`KSA.Avionics.getHealth`.


   .. attribute:: ok

      Working.


   .. attribute:: worn

      Working, with a dose of at least four fifths of the tolerance.


   .. attribute:: failed

      Failed for good, at the tolerance.



.. class:: Light

   A light. Obtained from :meth:`KSA.Part.getLights`.

   .. note::

      The vehicle's lights switch on and off together, see :meth:`KSA.Control.getLights`.
      Cabin lights are always on.

   .. method:: getPart()

      The part the light is in.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: :class:`KSA.Part`
   

   .. method:: getActive()

      Whether the light is on.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``boolean``
