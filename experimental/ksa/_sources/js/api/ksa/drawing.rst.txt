Drawing
=======


.. class:: Drawing

   Draws lines, shapes and text in the scene, each placed in a reference frame.

   .. note::

      The objects are drawn every frame until removed, and are removed when the client that
      added them disconnects. Lines are drawn at the game's fixed gizmo width.

   .. function:: addLine(start, end, referenceFrame[, visible])

      Draw a line between two positions.

      :param start: The position of the start of the line, in meters.
      :type start: ``[number, number, number]``
      :param end: The position of the end of the line, in meters.
      :type end: ``[number, number, number]``
      :param referenceFrame: The reference frame of the positions.
      :type referenceFrame: :class:`KSA.ReferenceFrame`
      :param visible: Whether the line is visible. Defaults to ``true``.
      :type visible: ``boolean``
      :rtype: :class:`KSA.Line`
   

   .. function:: addDirection(direction, referenceFrame[, length[, visible]])

      Draw a line from the origin of a reference frame in a direction.

      :param direction: The direction of the line.
      :type direction: ``[number, number, number]``
      :param referenceFrame: The reference frame of the direction.
      :type referenceFrame: :class:`KSA.ReferenceFrame`
      :param length: The length of the line, in meters. Defaults to ``1e+01``.
      :type length: ``number``
      :param visible: Whether the line is visible. Defaults to ``true``.
      :type visible: ``boolean``
      :rtype: :class:`KSA.Line`
   

   .. function:: addPolyline(vertices, referenceFrame[, closed[, visible]])

      Draw a line through a list of positions.

      :param vertices: The positions the line passes through, in meters.
      :type vertices: ``Array`` of ``[number, number, number]``
      :param referenceFrame: The reference frame of the positions.
      :type referenceFrame: :class:`KSA.ReferenceFrame`
      :param closed: Whether the line joins the last position back to the first. Defaults to ``false``.
      :type closed: ``boolean``
      :param visible: Whether the line is visible. Defaults to ``true``.
      :type visible: ``boolean``
      :rtype: :class:`KSA.Polyline`
   

   .. function:: addCircle(center, normal, radius, referenceFrame[, visible])

      Draw a circle.

      :param center: The position of the center of the circle, in meters.
      :type center: ``[number, number, number]``
      :param normal: The direction normal to the plane of the circle.
      :type normal: ``[number, number, number]``
      :param radius: The radius of the circle, in meters.
      :type radius: ``number``
      :param referenceFrame: The reference frame of the position and direction.
      :type referenceFrame: :class:`KSA.ReferenceFrame`
      :param visible: Whether the circle is visible. Defaults to ``true``.
      :type visible: ``boolean``
      :rtype: :class:`KSA.Circle`
   

   .. function:: addArc(center, normal, startDirection, angle, radius, referenceFrame[, visible])

      Draw an arc of a circle.

      :param center: The position of the center of the arc's circle, in meters.
      :type center: ``[number, number, number]``
      :param normal: The direction normal to the plane of the arc.
      :type normal: ``[number, number, number]``
      :param startDirection: The direction from the center to the start of the arc.
      :type startDirection: ``[number, number, number]``
      :param angle: The angle the arc turns through, counterclockwise about the normal, in radians.
      :type angle: ``number``
      :param radius: The radius of the arc, in meters.
      :type radius: ``number``
      :param referenceFrame: The reference frame of the position and directions.
      :type referenceFrame: :class:`KSA.ReferenceFrame`
      :param visible: Whether the arc is visible. Defaults to ``true``.
      :type visible: ``boolean``
      :rtype: :class:`KSA.Arc`
   

   .. function:: addBox(center, size, rotation, referenceFrame[, visible])

      Draw the edges of a box.

      :param center: The position of the center of the box, in meters.
      :type center: ``[number, number, number]``
      :param size: The lengths of the box's edges along its x, y and z axes, in meters.
      :type size: ``[number, number, number]``
      :param rotation: The rotation of the box's axes, as a quaternion of the form ``(x, y, z, w)``.
      :type rotation: ``[number, number, number, number]``
      :param referenceFrame: The reference frame of the position and rotation.
      :type referenceFrame: :class:`KSA.ReferenceFrame`
      :param visible: Whether the box is visible. Defaults to ``true``.
      :type visible: ``boolean``
      :rtype: :class:`KSA.Box`
   

   .. function:: addSphere(center, radius, referenceFrame[, visible])

      Draw a solid sphere.

      :param center: The position of the center of the sphere, in meters.
      :type center: ``[number, number, number]``
      :param radius: The radius of the sphere, in meters.
      :type radius: ``number``
      :param referenceFrame: The reference frame of the position.
      :type referenceFrame: :class:`KSA.ReferenceFrame`
      :param visible: Whether the sphere is visible. Defaults to ``true``.
      :type visible: ``boolean``
      :rtype: :class:`KSA.Sphere`
   

   .. function:: addText(text, position, referenceFrame[, size[, visible]])

      Draw text over the scene, centered on a position.

      :param text: The text.
      :type text: ``string``
      :param position: The position of the center of the text, in meters.
      :type position: ``[number, number, number]``
      :param referenceFrame: The reference frame of the position.
      :type referenceFrame: :class:`KSA.ReferenceFrame`
      :param size: The height of the font, in pixels. Defaults to ``16.0``.
      :type size: ``number``
      :param visible: Whether the text is visible. Defaults to ``true``.
      :type visible: ``boolean``
      :rtype: :class:`KSA.Text`
   

   .. function:: clear([clientOnly])

      Remove the drawing objects.

      :param clientOnly: Whether to remove only the objects added by the calling client. Defaults to ``false``.
      :type clientOnly: ``boolean``
