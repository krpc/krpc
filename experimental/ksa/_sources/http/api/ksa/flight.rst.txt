Flight
======


.. _http-KSA.Flight:

.. rubric:: Flight

Flight telemetry for a vehicle, relative to the surface of the body it orbits.
Obtained from :http:get:`/ksa/vehicle/(id)/flight`.

A ``Flight`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.


.. http:get:: /ksa/flight/(id)/mean-altitude

   The altitude above the mean radius of the body, in meters.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Flight <http-KSA.Flight>` object.

   :resjson double value:

.. _http-KSA.OutsideAir:

.. rubric:: OutsideAir

Whether a kitten can breathe the air outside a vehicle. See
:http:get:`/ksa/flight/(id)/outside-air`.

Passed and returned as the name of one of its values:


``"no_atmosphere"``
   Outside the atmosphere, or at a body with none.


``"no_oxygen"``
   An atmosphere with no oxygen.


``"too_thin"``
   An atmosphere whose oxygen pressure is too low to breathe.


``"breathable"``
   Breathable air.
