Parts
=====


.. _http-KSA.Part:

.. rubric:: Part

A part of a vehicle. Obtained from :http:get:`/ksa/vehicle/(id)/parts`.

.. note::

   A part keeps its object when the vehicle it belongs to changes, for example when a
   decoupler separates it. Throws an exception once the part no longer exists.


A ``Part`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.

   The id of the part's template.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Part <http-KSA.Part>` object.

   :resjson string value:

.. _http-KSA.Engine:

.. rubric:: Engine

An engine. Obtained from :http:get:`/ksa/part/(id)/engine`.

.. note::

   An active engine fires when the vehicle's engines are on, at the vehicle's throttle.
   See :http:get:`/ksa/control/(id)/engine-on` and :http:get:`/ksa/control/(id)/throttle`.


A ``Engine`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.


.. http:get:: /ksa/engine/(id)/part

   The part the engine is in.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Engine <http-KSA.Engine>` object.

   :resjson KSA.Part value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/engine/(id)/gimbals

   The gimbals that steer the engine's nozzles. Empty if the engine has none.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Engine <http-KSA.Engine>` object.

   :resjson list<KSA.Gimbal> value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/engine/(id)/active

   Whether the engine is active, as when its sequence is activated. Setting it returns
   once the change has applied.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Engine <http-KSA.Engine>` object.

   :resjson bool value:

.. http:put:: /ksa/engine/(id)/active

   Whether the engine is active, as when its sequence is activated. Setting it returns
   once the change has applied.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Engine <http-KSA.Engine>` object.

   :reqjson bool value: The new value.




.. http:get:: /ksa/engine/(id)/has-propellant

   Whether the engine has propellant to burn.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Engine <http-KSA.Engine>` object.

   :resjson bool value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/engine/(id)/minimum-throttle

   The lowest throttle the engine runs at, between 0 and 1.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Engine <http-KSA.Engine>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/engine/(id)/propellants

   The names of the substances the engine burns. See :http:get:`/ksa/resource/(id)/name`.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Engine <http-KSA.Engine>` object.

   :resjson list<string> value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/engine/(id)/thrust

   The thrust of the engine at its current throttle and the current atmospheric
   pressure, in Newtons. Zero when it is not firing.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Engine <http-KSA.Engine>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/engine/(id)/available-thrust

   The thrust of the engine at full throttle and the current atmospheric pressure, in
   Newtons. Zero when it is inactive or has no propellant.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Engine <http-KSA.Engine>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/engine/(id)/specific-impulse

   The specific impulse of the engine at the current atmospheric pressure, in seconds.
   Zero when it is inactive or has no propellant.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Engine <http-KSA.Engine>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:post:: /ksa/engine/(id)/available-thrust-at

   The thrust of the engine at full throttle and the given atmospheric pressure, in
   Newtons. Zero when it is inactive or has no propellant.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Engine <http-KSA.Engine>` object.

   :reqjson float pressure: The atmospheric pressure, in pascals.
   :resjson float value:





.. http:post:: /ksa/engine/(id)/specific-impulse-at

   The specific impulse of the engine at the given atmospheric pressure, in seconds.
   Zero when it is inactive or has no propellant.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Engine <http-KSA.Engine>` object.

   :reqjson float pressure: The atmospheric pressure, in pascals.
   :resjson float value:





.. http:get:: /ksa/engine/(id)/max-thrust

   The thrust of the engine at full throttle and the current atmospheric pressure, in
   Newtons, whether or not it is active and has propellant.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Engine <http-KSA.Engine>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/engine/(id)/max-vacuum-thrust

   The thrust of the engine at full throttle in a vacuum, in Newtons, whether or not

   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Engine <http-KSA.Engine>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/engine/(id)/chamber-pressure

   The pressure in the engine's combustion chamber, in pascals. Zero when it is not
   firing.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Engine <http-KSA.Engine>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/engine/(id)/solid

   Whether the engine is a solid rocket motor. Once ignited, a solid motor burns at
   full thrust until its grain runs out.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Engine <http-KSA.Engine>` object.

   :resjson bool value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/engine/(id)/grain-mass

   The mass of the propellant grain of a solid rocket motor, in kilograms.

   .. note::

      Some of the grain is left unburned when the motor burns out. Throws an exception

   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Engine <http-KSA.Engine>` object.

   :resjson float value:





.. _http-KSA.Gimbal:

.. rubric:: Gimbal

A gimbal that steers an engine nozzle. Obtained from :http:get:`/ksa/part/(id)/gimbals` or
:http:get:`/ksa/engine/(id)/gimbals`.

.. note::

   A gimbal turns about the y and z axes of its mount, which :http:post:`/ksa/gimbal/(id)/rotation` gives.
   The flight computer steers it while an engine it belongs to is :http:get:`/ksa/gimbal/(id)/active`.


A ``Gimbal`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.


.. http:get:: /ksa/gimbal/(id)/part

   The part the gimbal is in.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Gimbal <http-KSA.Gimbal>` object.

   :resjson KSA.Part value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/gimbal/(id)/angle-y

   The angle of the gimbal about its y axis, in degrees.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Gimbal <http-KSA.Gimbal>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/gimbal/(id)/angle-z

   The angle of the gimbal about its z axis, in degrees.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Gimbal <http-KSA.Gimbal>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/gimbal/(id)/max-angle-y

   The largest angle of the gimbal about its y axis, in degrees. Zero if it does not
   turn about that axis.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Gimbal <http-KSA.Gimbal>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/gimbal/(id)/max-angle-z

   The largest angle of the gimbal about its z axis, in degrees. Zero if it does not
   turn about that axis.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Gimbal <http-KSA.Gimbal>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/gimbal/(id)/active

   Whether an engine the gimbal steers is active.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Gimbal <http-KSA.Gimbal>` object.

   :resjson bool value:




.. http:post:: /ksa/gimbal/(id)/rotation

   The rotation of the gimbal's mount, whose y and z axes the gimbal turns about.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Gimbal <http-KSA.Gimbal>` object.

   :reqjson KSA.ReferenceFrame reference_frame: The reference frame of the rotation.
   :resjson tuple<double, double, double, double> value:

.. _http-KSA.ParachuteState:

.. rubric:: ParachuteState

The state of a parachute. See :http:get:`/ksa/parachute/(id)/state`.

Passed and returned as the name of one of its values:


``"stowed"``
   Packed away.


``"armed"``
   Waiting to deploy at :http:get:`/ksa/parachute/(id)/deploy-altitude`.


``"line_stretch"``
   Leaving the pack.


``"reefed"``
   Partly open, to limit the opening load.


``"disreefing"``
   Opening from reefed to full.


``"full"``
   Fully open.


``"cut"``
   Cut away.


``"ripped"``
   Torn by too much dynamic pressure.


``"severed_lines"``
   Lost after its lines broke under too much load.

A ``Deployable`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.


.. http:get:: /ksa/deployable/(id)/part

   The part the deployable is in.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Deployable <http-KSA.Deployable>` object.

   :resjson KSA.Part value:

.. _http-KSA.DeployableState:

.. rubric:: DeployableState

The state of a deployable part. See :http:get:`/ksa/deployable/(id)/state`.

Passed and returned as the name of one of its values:


``"deployed"``
   Fully deployed.


``"retracted"``
   Fully retracted.


``"deploying"``
   Moving toward deployed.


``"retracting"``
   Moving toward retracted.


``"broken"``
   Broken, so it can no longer move.



.. _http-KSA.MagneticScoop:

.. rubric:: MagneticScoop

A magnetic scoop, which collects gas from space into the vehicle's tanks. Obtained from
:http:get:`/ksa/part/(id)/magnetic-scoops`.

A ``MagneticScoop`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.
