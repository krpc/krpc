Power
=====


.. _http-KSA.Battery:

.. rubric:: Battery

A battery, which stores electrical energy. Obtained from :http:get:`/ksa/part/(id)/batteries`.

A ``Battery`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.


.. http:get:: /ksa/battery/(id)/part

   The part the battery is in.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Battery <http-KSA.Battery>` object.

   :resjson KSA.Part value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/battery/(id)/stored-energy

   The energy stored in the battery, in joules.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Battery <http-KSA.Battery>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/battery/(id)/energy-capacity

   The energy the battery can store, in joules.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Battery <http-KSA.Battery>` object.

   :resjson float value:

.. _http-KSA.Generator:

   A generator runs while its :http:get:`/ksa/generator/(id)/engine` is active, and produces power in

an object takes it as ``(id)``.

   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Generator <http-KSA.Generator>` object.

   :resjson KSA.Part value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/generator/(id)/engine

   The engine that drives the generator, or ``null`` if it has none.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Generator <http-KSA.Generator>` object.

   :resjson KSA.Engine? value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/generator/(id)/active

   Whether the generator is producing power.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Generator <http-KSA.Generator>` object.

   :resjson bool value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/generator/(id)/power

   The power the generator produces, in watts.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Generator <http-KSA.Generator>` object.

   :resjson float value:





.. _http-KSA.PowerConsumer:

A ``PowerConsumer`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.


.. http:get:: /ksa/power-consumer/(id)/part

   The part the power consumer is in.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`PowerConsumer <http-KSA.PowerConsumer>` object.

   :resjson KSA.Part value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/power-consumer/(id)/active

   Whether the part is using power.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`PowerConsumer <http-KSA.PowerConsumer>` object.

   :resjson bool value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/power-consumer/(id)/power

   The power the part uses, in watts.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`PowerConsumer <http-KSA.PowerConsumer>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/power-consumer/(id)/has-power-switch

   Whether the part has a power switch, as a probe core does.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`PowerConsumer <http-KSA.PowerConsumer>` object.

   :resjson bool value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/power-consumer/(id)/mode

   The setting of the power switch.

   .. note::

      Throws an exception if the part has no power switch.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`PowerConsumer <http-KSA.PowerConsumer>` object.

   :resjson KSA.PowerMode value:

.. http:put:: /ksa/power-consumer/(id)/mode

   The setting of the power switch.

   .. note::

      Throws an exception if the part has no power switch.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`PowerConsumer <http-KSA.PowerConsumer>` object.

   :reqjson KSA.PowerMode value: The new value.




.. http:get:: /ksa/power-consumer/(id)/standby-in-storms

   Whether the part goes on standby, drawing no power, while a radiation storm is under
   way at the vehicle.

   .. note::

      The game takes a storm of level 1 or worse on the S scale, as
      :http:get:`/ksa/flight/(id)/radiation-storm-level` gives. Throws an exception if the part has
      no power switch.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`PowerConsumer <http-KSA.PowerConsumer>` object.

   :resjson bool value:

.. http:put:: /ksa/power-consumer/(id)/standby-in-storms

   Whether the part goes on standby, drawing no power, while a radiation storm is under
   way at the vehicle.

   .. note::

      The game takes a storm of level 1 or worse on the S scale, as
      :http:get:`/ksa/flight/(id)/radiation-storm-level` gives. Throws an exception if the part has
      no power switch.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`PowerConsumer <http-KSA.PowerConsumer>` object.

   :reqjson bool value: The new value.




.. http:get:: /ksa/power-consumer/(id)/status

   The power status of the part.

   .. note::

      Throws an exception if the part has no power switch.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`PowerConsumer <http-KSA.PowerConsumer>` object.

   :resjson KSA.PowerStatus value:





.. _http-KSA.PowerMode:

.. rubric:: PowerMode

The setting of a power switch. See :http:get:`/ksa/power-consumer/(id)/mode`.

Passed and returned as the name of one of its values:


``"off"``
   Switched off.


``"auto"``
   On standby while another control module flies the vehicle, and on when none can.


``"on"``
   Switched on.



.. _http-KSA.PowerStatus:

.. rubric:: PowerStatus

The power status of a part with a power switch. See :http:get:`/ksa/power-consumer/(id)/status`.

Passed and returned as the name of one of its values:


``"off"``
   Switched off.


``"standby"``
   On standby, while another control module flies the vehicle or a radiation storm is under way.


``"rebooting"``
   Rebooting after a radiation upset.


``"on"``
   On, with power.


``"low"``
   On, with power for less than five minutes.


``"out"``
   Out of power.


``"failed"``
   Failed from its radiation dose.



.. _http-KSA.PowerForecast:

.. rubric:: PowerForecast

The charge, draw and supply of the electrical circuit a part is on, and how long its
batteries last. Obtained from :http:get:`/ksa/part/(id)/power-forecast`.

.. note::

   A circuit is the parts joined by connections that carry electricity. This is the
   forecast the game shows for a probe core.


A ``PowerForecast`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.


.. http:get:: /ksa/power-forecast/(id)/has-battery

   Whether the circuit has a battery.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`PowerForecast <http-KSA.PowerForecast>` object.

   :resjson bool value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/power-forecast/(id)/charge

   The energy stored in the circuit's batteries, in joules.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`PowerForecast <http-KSA.PowerForecast>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/power-forecast/(id)/draw

   The power the parts on the circuit draw, in watts.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`PowerForecast <http-KSA.PowerForecast>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/power-forecast/(id)/supply

   The power the solar panels and generators on the circuit supply, in watts.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`PowerForecast <http-KSA.PowerForecast>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/power-forecast/(id)/time-left

   The time until the batteries run out, in seconds. Infinite while the supply meets
   the draw, and zero when the batteries are empty or there are none.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`PowerForecast <http-KSA.PowerForecast>` object.

   :resjson float value:





.. _http-KSA.Avionics:

.. rubric:: Avionics

The avionics of a probe core, which radiation wears out and upsets. Obtained from
:http:get:`/ksa/part/(id)/avionics` or :http:get:`/ksa/vehicle/(id)/avionics`.

.. note::

   The avionics take a dose only while :http:get:`/ksa/radiation-enabled` is true. Switched
   off or on standby, they take half the dose and no upsets.


A ``Avionics`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.


.. http:get:: /ksa/avionics/(id)/part

   The part the avionics are in.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Avionics <http-KSA.Avionics>` object.

   :resjson KSA.Part value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/avionics/(id)/health

   The health of the avionics.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Avionics <http-KSA.Avionics>` object.

   :resjson KSA.AvionicsHealth value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/avionics/(id)/dose

   The radiation dose the avionics have taken, in grays of silicon.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Avionics <http-KSA.Avionics>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/avionics/(id)/tolerance

   The dose at which the avionics fail, in grays of silicon.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Avionics <http-KSA.Avionics>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/avionics/(id)/dose-rate

   The rate at which the avionics take a dose, in grays of silicon per second,
   averaged over about an hour. Not a number until the avionics have taken a dose.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Avionics <http-KSA.Avionics>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/avionics/(id)/time-left

   The time until the avionics fail at :http:get:`/ksa/avionics/(id)/dose-rate`, in seconds. Infinite
   while they take no dose, and zero once they have failed.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Avionics <http-KSA.Avionics>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/avionics/(id)/rebooting

   Whether the avionics are rebooting after a radiation upset.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Avionics <http-KSA.Avionics>` object.

   :resjson bool value:





.. _http-KSA.AvionicsHealth:

.. rubric:: AvionicsHealth

The health of avionics. See :http:get:`/ksa/avionics/(id)/health`.

Passed and returned as the name of one of its values:


``"ok"``
   Working.


``"worn"``
   Working, with a dose of at least four fifths of the tolerance.


``"failed"``
   Failed for good, at the tolerance.
