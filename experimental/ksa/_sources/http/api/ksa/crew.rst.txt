Crew
====


.. _http-KSA.Kitten:

.. rubric:: Kitten

A kitten in the agency's roster. Obtained from :http:get:`/ksa/kittens`,
:http:get:`/ksa/vehicle/(id)/crew` or :http:get:`/ksa/seat/(id)/kitten`.

A ``Kitten`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.


.. http:get:: /ksa/kitten/(id)/name

   The kitten's name, which is unique in the roster.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Kitten <http-KSA.Kitten>` object.

   :resjson string value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/kitten/(id)/roster-index

   The kitten's position in the agency's roster, from 0. See :http:get:`/ksa/kittens`.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Kitten <http-KSA.Kitten>` object.

   :resjson int32 value:

   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Kitten <http-KSA.Kitten>` object.

      Throws an exception if the kitten is not in a seat, or the vehicle has no EVA hatch.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Kitten <http-KSA.Kitten>` object.

   :resjson KSA.Vehicle value: The vehicle the kitten boarded.

.. http:post:: /ksa/kitten/(id)/can-carry

   Whether the kitten can take supplies from or stow them in a habitat. See
   :http:post:`/ksa/kitten/(id)/take` and :http:post:`/ksa/kitten/(id)/stow`.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Kitten <http-KSA.Kitten>` object.

   :reqjson KSA.Habitat habitat: The habitat.
   :resjson bool value:





.. http:post:: /ksa/kitten/(id)/take

   Take meals and canisters from a habitat within reach, and return once the kitten
   carries them. Takes fewer when the habitat holds fewer, or the kitten cannot carry
   more.

   .. note::

      Throws an exception if the kitten is not on EVA or is unwell, life support is
      disabled, or the habitat stores no supplies or is out of reach. See
      :http:post:`/ksa/kitten/(id)/can-carry`.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Kitten <http-KSA.Kitten>` object.

   :reqjson KSA.Habitat habitat: The habitat, which must store supplies.
   :reqjson int32 meals: The number of meals to take. Optional, defaults to ``0``.
   :reqjson int32 canisters: The number of canisters to take. Optional, defaults to ``0``.





.. http:post:: /ksa/kitten/(id)/stow

   Stow the meals and canisters the kitten carries in a habitat within reach, and
   return once they are stowed. Stows fewer when the kitten carries fewer, or the
   habitat has less room.

   .. note::

      Throws an exception if the kitten is not on EVA or is unwell, life support is
      disabled, or the habitat stores no supplies or is out of reach. See
      :http:post:`/ksa/kitten/(id)/can-carry`.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Kitten <http-KSA.Kitten>` object.

   :reqjson KSA.Habitat habitat: The habitat, which must store supplies.
   :reqjson int32 meals: The number of meals to stow. Optional, defaults to ``0``.
   :reqjson int32 canisters: The number of canisters to stow. Optional, defaults to ``0``.






.. _http-KSA.LocomotionMode:

.. rubric:: LocomotionMode

How a kitten on EVA is moving. See :http:get:`/ksa/kitten/(id)/locomotion-mode`.

Passed and returned as the name of one of its values:


``"mmu"``
   Flying with the maneuvering unit, away from any surface.


``"grounded"``
   Standing or walking on a surface.


``"airborne"``
   In the air above a surface, such as during a jump.


``"tumbling"``
   Tumbling after a fall or an impact.


``"righting"``
   Getting back up after tumbling.


``"ladder"``
   Holding a ladder or grab rail.


``"swimming"``
   Swimming in an ocean.



.. _http-KSA.KittenCondition:

.. rubric:: KittenCondition

The effect on a kitten of going without a supply. See :http:post:`/ksa/kitten/(id)/condition`.

Passed and returned as the name of one of its values:


``"healthy"``
   Unaffected.


``"warning"``
   Going without, with no effect yet.


``"degraded"``
   Weakened, and unable to go on EVA.


``"incapacitated"``
   Unable to fly the vehicle or move supplies.


``"dead"``
   Dead.

.. rubric:: DeathCause

Passed and returned as the name of one of its values:


``"unknown"``
   Not recorded.


``"no_oxygen"``
   Lack of oxygen.


``"no_scrubber"``
   Carbon dioxide, with no scrubber canisters left.


``"no_water"``
   Lack of water.


``"no_food"``
   Lack of food.


``"radiation"``
   A lethal radiation dose.


``"ground_impact"``
   Hitting the ground.


``"ocean_impact"``
   Hitting the ocean.


``"collision"``
   A collision.


``"excessive_g_force"``
   Excessive acceleration.


``"aerodynamic_forces"``
   Aerodynamic forces breaking up the vehicle.


``"hydrodynamic_forces"``
   Hydrodynamic forces breaking up the vehicle.


``"fall"``
   A fall on EVA.


``"abandoned"``
   Left in a vehicle that was abandoned.



.. _http-KSA.CrewEvent:

.. rubric:: CrewEvent

A kitten's most urgent coming event: its supplies running low, or its condition
worsening. Obtained from :http:get:`/ksa/vehicle/(id)/crew-events` or
:http:get:`/ksa/vehicle/(id)/next-crew-event`.

.. note::

   As the game's crew clock, an event within a day is the worst stage the kitten reaches
   in that time, and a later event is the soonest. The event is the game's forecast when it
   is read from the vehicle. Read it again for a new forecast.


A ``CrewEvent`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.


.. http:get:: /ksa/crew-event/(id)/kitten

   The kitten.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`CrewEvent <http-KSA.CrewEvent>` object.

   :resjson KSA.Kitten value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/crew-event/(id)/stage

   What happens to the kitten.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`CrewEvent <http-KSA.CrewEvent>` object.

   :resjson KSA.CrewStage value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/crew-event/(id)/hazard

   The cause of the event.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`CrewEvent <http-KSA.CrewEvent>` object.

   :resjson KSA.CrewHazard value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/crew-event/(id)/ut

   The universal time of the event, in seconds.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`CrewEvent <http-KSA.CrewEvent>` object.

   :resjson double value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/crew-event/(id)/time-to

   The time until the event, in seconds. Zero once its time has passed.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`CrewEvent <http-KSA.CrewEvent>` object.

   :resjson float value:





.. _http-KSA.CrewStage:

.. rubric:: CrewStage

What happens to a kitten in a crew event. See :http:get:`/ksa/crew-event/(id)/stage`.

Passed and returned as the name of one of its values:


``"low"``
   The supply runs low.


``"degraded"``
   The kitten is weakened, and unable to go on EVA. For radiation, the kitten gets
   radiation sickness.


``"incapacitated"``
   The kitten is unable to fly the vehicle or move supplies.


``"dead"``
   The kitten dies.

Passed and returned as the name of one of its values:


``"air"``
   Running out of air.


``"scrubber"``
   Running out of carbon dioxide scrubber.


``"water"``
   Running out of water.


``"food"``
   Running out of food.


``"radiation"``
   The radiation dose.

A crew seat. Obtained from :http:get:`/ksa/part/(id)/seats`.

A ``Seat`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.


.. http:get:: /ksa/seat/(id)/part

   The part the seat is in.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Seat <http-KSA.Seat>` object.

   :resjson KSA.Part value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/seat/(id)/kitten

   Returns the kitten in the seat, or ``null`` if the seat is empty, and sets the
   kitten in the seat. Setting ``null`` empties the seat.

   .. note::

      A kitten in another seat of the vehicle moves to this one, within its habitat zone.
      Throws an exception if the kitten is killed in action, retired, on EVA, in another
      vehicle, or incapacitated, or the seat is in another habitat zone.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Seat <http-KSA.Seat>` object.

   :resjson KSA.Kitten? value:

.. http:put:: /ksa/seat/(id)/kitten

   Returns the kitten in the seat, or ``null`` if the seat is empty, and sets the
   kitten in the seat. Setting ``null`` empties the seat.

   .. note::

      A kitten in another seat of the vehicle moves to this one, within its habitat zone.
      Throws an exception if the kitten is killed in action, retired, on EVA, in another
      vehicle, or incapacitated, or the seat is in another habitat zone.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Seat <http-KSA.Seat>` object.

   :reqjson KSA.Kitten? value: The new value.
