Orbit
=====


.. _http-KSA.Orbit:

.. rubric:: Orbit

An orbit. Obtained from :http:get:`/ksa/vehicle/(id)/orbit`, :http:get:`/ksa/body/(id)/orbit`,
:http:get:`/ksa/burn/(id)/orbit` or :http:get:`/ksa/orbit/(id)/next-orbit`.

.. note::

   An orbit follows the game's prediction as the trajectory changes. A predicted orbit, from
   :http:get:`/ksa/orbit/(id)/next-orbit` or :http:get:`/ksa/burn/(id)/orbit`, is a position in the vehicle's or the
   burn's predicted trajectory, and throws an exception while the trajectory is too short to
   reach it.


A ``Orbit`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.

.. http:post:: /ksa/orbit/create-from-elements

   Create the orbit with the given elements around a body. The angles are those of
   :ref:`Orbit <http-KSA.Orbit>`.

   .. note::

      The orbit is a single conic around *body*, with no
      :http:get:`/ksa/orbit/(id)/next-orbit`. It is held until :http:post:`/ksa/orbit/(id)/remove` is called on it, or
      the client that created it disconnects.


   :reqjson KSA.Body body: The body being orbited.
   :reqjson double semi_major_axis: The semi-major axis, in meters. Negative or positive for a hyperbolic orbit.
   :reqjson double eccentricity: The eccentricity. Below 1 for an elliptical orbit, and above 1 for a hyperbolic one.
   :reqjson double inclination: The inclination, in radians.
   :reqjson double longitude_of_ascending_node: The longitude of the ascending node, in radians.
   :reqjson double argument_of_periapsis: The argument of periapsis, in radians.
   :reqjson double mean_anomaly_at_epoch: The mean anomaly at the epoch, in radians.
   :reqjson double epoch: The universal time of the epoch, in seconds.
   :resjson KSA.Orbit value:





.. http:post:: /ksa/orbit/create-from-position-and-velocity

   Create the orbit around a body through the given position and velocity.

   .. note::

      The orbit is a single conic around *body*, with no
      :http:get:`/ksa/orbit/(id)/next-orbit`. It is held until :http:post:`/ksa/orbit/(id)/remove` is called on it, or
      the client that created it disconnects.


   :reqjson KSA.Body body: The body being orbited.
   :reqjson tuple<double, double, double> position: The position, in meters, relative to where the body is now.
   :reqjson tuple<double, double, double> velocity: The velocity, in meters per second.
   :reqjson double ut: The universal time of the position and velocity, in seconds.
   :reqjson KSA.ReferenceFrame reference_frame: The reference frame of the position and velocity.
   :resjson KSA.Orbit value:





.. http:post:: /ksa/orbit/(id)/remove

   Remove an orbit created by :http:post:`/ksa/orbit/create-from-elements` or
   :http:post:`/ksa/orbit/create-from-position-and-velocity`.

   .. note::

      Throws an exception if the orbit was not created by a client.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Orbit <http-KSA.Orbit>` object.







.. _http-KSA.OrbitTransition:

.. rubric:: OrbitTransition

How an orbit ends. See :http:get:`/ksa/orbit/(id)/end-transition`.

Passed and returned as the name of one of its values:


``"none"``
   The orbit does not end.


``"encounter"``
   The orbit enters the sphere of influence of another body.


``"escape"``
   The orbit leaves the sphere of influence of the body being orbited.


``"impact"``
   The orbit hits the surface of the body being orbited.


``"burn"``
   The orbit ends at a planned burn.
