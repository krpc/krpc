Resources
=========


.. _http-KSA.Resource:

.. rubric:: Resource

A substance held in tanks, such as a propellant, in a part or across a whole vehicle.
Obtained from :http:get:`/ksa/part/(id)/resources` or :http:get:`/ksa/vehicle/(id)/resources`.

A ``Resource`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.


.. http:get:: /ksa/resource/(id)/name

   The name of the substance.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Resource <http-KSA.Resource>` object.

   :resjson string value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/resource/(id)/amount

   The mass of the substance held, in kilograms.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Resource <http-KSA.Resource>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/resource/(id)/max

   The mass of the substance the tanks can hold, in kilograms.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Resource <http-KSA.Resource>` object.

   :resjson float value:





.. _http-KSA.Tank:

.. rubric:: Tank

A propellant tank. Obtained from :http:get:`/ksa/part/(id)/tanks` or :http:get:`/ksa/vehicle/(id)/tanks`.

A ``Tank`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.


.. http:get:: /ksa/tank/(id)/part

   The part the tank is in.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Tank <http-KSA.Tank>` object.

   :resjson KSA.Part value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/tank/(id)/volume

   The volume of the tank, in cubic meters.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Tank <http-KSA.Tank>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/tank/(id)/rated-pressure

   The pressure the tank stores gas at, in pascals. Zero if the tank cannot store gas.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Tank <http-KSA.Tank>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/tank/(id)/filled-fraction

   The fraction of the tank's volume that is filled, between 0 and 1.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Tank <http-KSA.Tank>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/tank/(id)/propellant-use-enabled

   Whether engines can draw propellant from the tank.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Tank <http-KSA.Tank>` object.

   :resjson bool value:

.. http:put:: /ksa/tank/(id)/propellant-use-enabled

   Whether engines can draw propellant from the tank.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Tank <http-KSA.Tank>` object.

   :reqjson bool value: The new value.




.. http:get:: /ksa/tank/(id)/transfer-mode

   The direction the tank transfers propellant in.

   .. note::

      Propellant flows from tanks transferring out to tanks of the same vehicle transferring
      in, and the transfer draws battery power. Setting a transfer throws an exception if
      the running version of KSA does not support it.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Tank <http-KSA.Tank>` object.

   :resjson KSA.TankTransferMode value:

.. http:put:: /ksa/tank/(id)/transfer-mode

   The direction the tank transfers propellant in.

   .. note::

      Propellant flows from tanks transferring out to tanks of the same vehicle transferring
      in, and the transfer draws battery power. Setting a transfer throws an exception if
      the running version of KSA does not support it.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Tank <http-KSA.Tank>` object.

   :reqjson KSA.TankTransferMode value: The new value.





.. _http-KSA.TankTransferMode:

.. rubric:: TankTransferMode

The direction a tank transfers propellant in. See :http:get:`/ksa/tank/(id)/transfer-mode`.

Passed and returned as the name of one of its values:


``"none"``
   No transfer.


``"in"``
   Into the tank, from tanks transferring out.


``"out"``
   Out of the tank, to tanks transferring in.



.. _http-KSA.FuelLink:

.. rubric:: FuelLink

A fuel line between two parts of a vehicle, which lets propellant flow between them.
Obtained from :http:get:`/ksa/vehicle/(id)/fuel-links`.

A ``FuelLink`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.


.. http:get:: /ksa/fuel-link/(id)/part-a

   One of the parts the link joins.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`FuelLink <http-KSA.FuelLink>` object.

   :resjson KSA.Part value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/fuel-link/(id)/part-b

   The other part the link joins.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`FuelLink <http-KSA.FuelLink>` object.

   :resjson KSA.Part value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/fuel-link/(id)/flow

   The direction propellant flows through the link.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`FuelLink <http-KSA.FuelLink>` object.

   :resjson KSA.FuelLinkFlow value:

.. http:put:: /ksa/fuel-link/(id)/flow

   The direction propellant flows through the link.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`FuelLink <http-KSA.FuelLink>` object.

   :reqjson KSA.FuelLinkFlow value: The new value.




.. http:get:: /ksa/fuel-link/(id)/enabled

   Whether propellant can flow through the link.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`FuelLink <http-KSA.FuelLink>` object.

   :resjson bool value:

.. http:put:: /ksa/fuel-link/(id)/enabled

   Whether propellant can flow through the link.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`FuelLink <http-KSA.FuelLink>` object.

   :reqjson bool value: The new value.





.. _http-KSA.FuelLinkFlow:

.. rubric:: FuelLinkFlow

The direction propellant flows through a fuel link. See :http:get:`/ksa/fuel-link/(id)/flow`.

Passed and returned as the name of one of its values:


``"a_to_b"``
   From :http:get:`/ksa/fuel-link/(id)/part-a` to :http:get:`/ksa/fuel-link/(id)/part-b`.


``"b_to_a"``
   From :http:get:`/ksa/fuel-link/(id)/part-b` to :http:get:`/ksa/fuel-link/(id)/part-a`.


``"both"``
   Either way.
