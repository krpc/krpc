Camera
======


.. _http-KSA.Camera:

.. rubric:: Camera

The game's main camera. Obtained from :http:get:`/ksa/camera`.

A ``Camera`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.


.. http:get:: /ksa/camera/(id)/mode

   The mode of the camera.

   .. note::

      Setting ``"iva"`` while the camera is not focused on a vehicle
      with a crew seat sets ``"orbit"`` instead, as the game does. A
      change of mode releases the active vehicle's held rotation and translation inputs.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Camera <http-KSA.Camera>` object.

   :resjson KSA.CameraMode value:

.. http:get:: /ksa/camera/(id)/distance

   The distance from the camera to the focused object, in meters. Throws an exception
   in any mode other than orbit and map.

   .. note::

      The camera moves smoothly to a new distance, and stays at least the focused object's
      radius from it in map mode, and half its radius in orbit mode.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Camera <http-KSA.Camera>` object.

   :resjson float value:

.. _http-KSA.CameraMode:

.. rubric:: CameraMode

The mode of the camera. See :http:get:`/ksa/camera/(id)/mode`.

Passed and returned as the name of one of its values:


``"orbit"``
   Orbits the focused object.


``"free"``
   Flies freely.


``"map"``
   The map view.


``"iva"``
   Inside the vehicle, from a crew seat.


``"fixed"``
   Fixed to a part of the vehicle, such as a docking port.
