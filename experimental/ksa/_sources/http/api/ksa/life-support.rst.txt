Life Support
============


.. _http-KSA.LifeSupport:

.. rubric:: LifeSupport

How long the supplies of a vehicle, a habitat zone or a kitten's suit last. Obtained
from :http:get:`/ksa/vehicle/(id)/life-support`, :http:get:`/ksa/habitat-zone/(id)/life-support` or
:http:get:`/ksa/kitten/(id)/life-support`.

.. note::

   The times count down only while :http:get:`/ksa/life-support-enabled` is true. Air
   lasts forever in breathable air outside the vehicle.


A ``LifeSupport`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.


.. http:get:: /ksa/life-support/(id)/kittens

   The number of kittens the supplies are shared between.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`LifeSupport <http-KSA.LifeSupport>` object.

   :resjson int32 value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/life-support/(id)/air-mass

   The mass of oxygen in the crew's tanks, in kilograms.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`LifeSupport <http-KSA.LifeSupport>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/life-support/(id)/water-mass

   The mass of water in the crew's tanks, in kilograms.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`LifeSupport <http-KSA.LifeSupport>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/life-support/(id)/meals

   The number of meals.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`LifeSupport <http-KSA.LifeSupport>` object.

   :resjson int32 value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/life-support/(id)/air-time

   The time the air lasts, in seconds. Infinite with no crew, or in breathable air.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`LifeSupport <http-KSA.LifeSupport>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/life-support/(id)/scrubber-time

   The time the carbon dioxide scrubbers last, in seconds. Infinite with no crew.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`LifeSupport <http-KSA.LifeSupport>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/life-support/(id)/water-time

   The time the water lasts, in seconds. Infinite with no crew.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`LifeSupport <http-KSA.LifeSupport>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/life-support/(id)/food-time

   The time the food lasts, in seconds. Infinite with no crew.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`LifeSupport <http-KSA.LifeSupport>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/life-support/(id)/time

   The time until the first supply runs out, in seconds. A suit counts its air and
   scrubber only. Infinite with no crew.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`LifeSupport <http-KSA.LifeSupport>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/life-support/(id)/limiting-supply

   The supply that runs out first. Air when every time is infinite, as with no crew.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`LifeSupport <http-KSA.LifeSupport>` object.

   :resjson KSA.LifeSupportSupply value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/life-support/(id)/level

   How long the supplies last, as the game's life support gauge shows it.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`LifeSupport <http-KSA.LifeSupport>` object.

   :resjson KSA.LifeSupportLevel value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/life-support/(id)/time-to-next-level

   The time until :http:get:`/ksa/life-support/(id)/level` next changes, in seconds. Infinite with no crew.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`LifeSupport <http-KSA.LifeSupport>` object.

   :resjson float value:





.. _http-KSA.LifeSupportLevel:

.. rubric:: LifeSupportLevel

How long the crew's supplies last. See :http:get:`/ksa/life-support/(id)/level`.

Passed and returned as the name of one of its values:


``"ok"``
   More than a day of supplies, or no crew.


``"low"``
   A day of supplies or less.


``"critical"``
   An hour of supplies or less.


``"out"``
   A supply has run out.



.. _http-KSA.LifeSupportSupply:

.. rubric:: LifeSupportSupply

A supply the crew needs. See :http:get:`/ksa/life-support/(id)/limiting-supply`.

Passed and returned as the name of one of its values:


``"air"``
   Oxygen to breathe.


``"scrubber"``
   Lithium hydroxide canisters, which scrub carbon dioxide from the air.


``"water"``
   Drinking water.


``"food"``
   Meals.



.. _http-KSA.HabitatZone:

.. rubric:: HabitatZone

The connected habitable parts of a vehicle, which share their crew's supplies.
Obtained from :http:get:`/ksa/vehicle/(id)/habitat-zones` or :http:get:`/ksa/habitat/(id)/zone`.

.. note::

   Parts join a zone through connections the crew can pass, such as an open docking
   port hatch.


A ``HabitatZone`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.


.. http:get:: /ksa/habitat-zone/(id)/vehicle

   The vehicle the zone is in.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`HabitatZone <http-KSA.HabitatZone>` object.

   :resjson KSA.Vehicle value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/habitat-zone/(id)/parts

   The parts in the zone.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`HabitatZone <http-KSA.HabitatZone>` object.

   :resjson list<KSA.Part> value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/habitat-zone/(id)/habitats

   The habitats in the zone.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`HabitatZone <http-KSA.HabitatZone>` object.

   :resjson list<KSA.Habitat> value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/habitat-zone/(id)/seats

   The crew seats in the zone.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`HabitatZone <http-KSA.HabitatZone>` object.

   :resjson list<KSA.Seat> value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/habitat-zone/(id)/crew

   The kittens in the zone's seats.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`HabitatZone <http-KSA.HabitatZone>` object.

   :resjson list<KSA.Kitten> value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/habitat-zone/(id)/linked-zones

   The zones that join this one when closed docking port hatches between them open.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`HabitatZone <http-KSA.HabitatZone>` object.

   :resjson list<KSA.HabitatZone> value:




.. http:post:: /ksa/habitat-zone/(id)/hatches-to

   The closed docking port hatches that join this zone to a linked zone when they
   open.

   .. note::

      Throws an exception if the zone is not linked to this one.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`HabitatZone <http-KSA.HabitatZone>` object.

   :reqjson KSA.HabitatZone zone: A zone in :http:get:`/ksa/habitat-zone/(id)/linked-zones`.
   :resjson list<KSA.DockingPort> value:





.. http:get:: /ksa/habitat-zone/(id)/habitable-volume

   The volume the crew can live in, in cubic meters.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`HabitatZone <http-KSA.HabitatZone>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/habitat-zone/(id)/storage-volume

   The volume for stored supplies, in cubic meters.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`HabitatZone <http-KSA.HabitatZone>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/habitat-zone/(id)/life-support

   How long the zone's supplies last its crew.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`HabitatZone <http-KSA.HabitatZone>` object.

   :resjson KSA.LifeSupport value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/habitat-zone/(id)/mission-supplies

   Whether the zone's supplies last its crew through the vehicle's mission. ``null``
   without a feasible mission with time left, or while
   :http:get:`/ksa/life-support-enabled` is false.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`HabitatZone <http-KSA.HabitatZone>` object.

   :resjson KSA.MissionSupplies? value:




.. http:post:: /ksa/habitat-zone/(id)/planned-kittens

   The number of kittens planned in the zone for a leg of the vehicle's mission. The
   plan defaults to the kittens seated in the zone.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`HabitatZone <http-KSA.HabitatZone>` object.

   :reqjson KSA.MissionLeg leg: The leg.
   :resjson int32 value:






.. _http-KSA.Mission:

.. rubric:: Mission

The mission planned for a vehicle in the editor's life support window. Obtained from
:http:get:`/ksa/vehicle/(id)/mission`.

.. note::

   The game times the legs of the mission from the plan, and checks the supplies of each
   habitat zone against them. See :http:get:`/ksa/habitat-zone/(id)/mission-supplies`.


A ``Mission`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.


.. http:get:: /ksa/mission/(id)/destination

   Where the mission goes.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Mission <http-KSA.Mission>` object.

   :resjson KSA.MissionDestination value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/mission/(id)/body

   The body the mission goes to, or ``null`` if it has no destination. For a low
   orbit, this is the home body.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Mission <http-KSA.Mission>` object.

   :resjson KSA.Body? value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/mission/(id)/arrival

   What the mission does at its destination.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Mission <http-KSA.Mission>` object.

   :resjson KSA.MissionArrival value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/mission/(id)/trip

   Whether the mission comes back.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Mission <http-KSA.Mission>` object.

   :resjson KSA.MissionTrip value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/mission/(id)/stay

   The time planned at the destination, in seconds.

   .. note::

      The game rounds a stay before a return up to the next transfer window home. See
      :http:get:`/ksa/mission/(id)/stay-time`.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Mission <http-KSA.Mission>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/mission/(id)/custom

   Whether the mission has a length set by the player, in place of legs timed from
   its destination. See :http:get:`/ksa/mission/(id)/custom-length`.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Mission <http-KSA.Mission>` object.

   :resjson bool value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/mission/(id)/custom-length

   The length of a mission with :http:get:`/ksa/mission/(id)/custom` set, in seconds.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Mission <http-KSA.Mission>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/mission/(id)/feasible

   Whether the game can time the legs of the mission. A mission with no destination,
   or a flyby with no way back, cannot be timed.

   .. note::

      The leg times are zero when this is false.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Mission <http-KSA.Mission>` object.

   :resjson bool value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/mission/(id)/out-time

   The planned time of the way out, in seconds.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Mission <http-KSA.Mission>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/mission/(id)/stay-time

   The planned time at the destination, in seconds.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Mission <http-KSA.Mission>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/mission/(id)/back-time

   The planned time of the way back, in seconds. Zero for a one way trip.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Mission <http-KSA.Mission>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/mission/(id)/return-wait

   The time from arriving at the destination until the next transfer window home, in
   seconds. Zero when there is no wait.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Mission <http-KSA.Mission>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/mission/(id)/total-time

   The planned length of the mission, in seconds.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Mission <http-KSA.Mission>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/mission/(id)/time-left

   The time left in the mission as planned, counted from the vehicle's launch, in

   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Mission <http-KSA.Mission>` object.

   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Mission <http-KSA.Mission>` object.

   :resjson double value:





.. _http-KSA.MissionDestination:

.. rubric:: MissionDestination

Passed and returned as the name of one of its values:


``"none"``
   No destination.


``"low_orbit"``

.. _http-KSA.MissionArrival:

Passed and returned as the name of one of its values:


``"flyby"``
   Flies past it.


``"orbit"``
   Orbits it.


``"land"``
   Lands on it.



.. _http-KSA.MissionTrip:

.. rubric:: MissionTrip

Whether a mission comes back. See :http:get:`/ksa/mission/(id)/trip`.

Passed and returned as the name of one of its values:


``"return"``
   Goes and comes back.


``"one_way"``
   Goes and stays.

.. _http-KSA.MissionSupplies:

.. rubric:: MissionSupplies

Whether the supplies of a habitat zone last its crew through the vehicle's mission, as
the life support window shows it. Obtained from :http:get:`/ksa/habitat-zone/(id)/mission-supplies`.

.. note::

   The check takes the legs left in the mission as planned, counted from the vehicle's
   launch, and the kittens planned for each leg. The times are from now.


A ``MissionSupplies`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.


.. http:get:: /ksa/mission-supplies/(id)/verdict

   Whether the supplies last the mission.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`MissionSupplies <http-KSA.MissionSupplies>` object.

   :resjson KSA.MissionSupplyVerdict value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/mission-supplies/(id)/short-by

   The time by which the first supply to run out falls short of the end of the
   mission, in seconds. Zero when the supplies last.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`MissionSupplies <http-KSA.MissionSupplies>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/mission-supplies/(id)/first-supply

   The supply that runs out first.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`MissionSupplies <http-KSA.MissionSupplies>` object.

   :resjson KSA.LifeSupportSupply value:




.. http:post:: /ksa/mission-supplies/(id)/time-left

   The time until a supply runs out with the crew planned for each leg, in seconds.
   Infinite if it lasts the mission.

   .. note::

      Air lasts while the vehicle is in breathable air.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`MissionSupplies <http-KSA.MissionSupplies>` object.

   :reqjson KSA.LifeSupportSupply supply: The supply.
   :resjson float value:





.. http:get:: /ksa/mission-supplies/(id)/end

   The time until the zone has no crew left in the mission, in seconds.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`MissionSupplies <http-KSA.MissionSupplies>` object.

   :resjson float value:





.. _http-KSA.MissionSupplyVerdict:

.. rubric:: MissionSupplyVerdict

Whether a habitat zone has the supplies for a mission. See :http:get:`/ksa/mission-supplies/(id)/verdict`.

Passed and returned as the name of one of its values:


``"unused"``
   The zone has no crew during the mission.


``"enough"``
   The supplies last the mission.


``"short"``
   A supply runs out before the end of the mission.



.. _http-KSA.Habitat:

.. rubric:: Habitat

A habitable or storage space in a part, which holds meals and carbon dioxide scrubber
canisters. Obtained from :http:get:`/ksa/part/(id)/habitats`.

A ``Habitat`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.


.. http:get:: /ksa/habitat/(id)/part

   The part the habitat is in.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Habitat <http-KSA.Habitat>` object.

   :resjson KSA.Part value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/habitat/(id)/zone

   The habitat zone the habitat is in.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Habitat <http-KSA.Habitat>` object.

   :resjson KSA.HabitatZone value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/habitat/(id)/habitable-volume

   The volume the crew can live in, in cubic meters.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Habitat <http-KSA.Habitat>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/habitat/(id)/storage-volume

   The volume for stored supplies, in cubic meters.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Habitat <http-KSA.Habitat>` object.

   :resjson float value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/habitat/(id)/meals

   The number of meals in the habitat.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Habitat <http-KSA.Habitat>` object.

   :resjson int32 value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/habitat/(id)/canisters

   The number of carbon dioxide scrubber canisters in the habitat.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Habitat <http-KSA.Habitat>` object.

   :resjson int32 value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/habitat/(id)/meal-capacity

   The number of meals the habitat holds when full.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Habitat <http-KSA.Habitat>` object.

   :resjson int32 value:




.. http:get:: /ksa/habitat/(id)/canister-capacity

   The number of carbon dioxide scrubber canisters the habitat holds when full.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Habitat <http-KSA.Habitat>` object.

   :resjson int32 value:




.. http:post:: /ksa/habitat/(id)/transfer

   Moves meals and canisters to another habitat in the same zone. Moves fewer when
   this habitat holds fewer, or the other has less room.

   .. note::

      A kitten in the zone moves the supplies. Throws an exception if life support is
      disabled, the habitats are in different zones, or the zone has no crew able to
      move them.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Habitat <http-KSA.Habitat>` object.

   :reqjson KSA.Habitat to: The habitat to move the supplies to.
   :reqjson int32 meals: The number of meals to move. Optional, defaults to ``0``.
   :reqjson int32 canisters: The number of canisters to move. Optional, defaults to ``0``.
