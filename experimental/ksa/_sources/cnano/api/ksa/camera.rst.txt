Camera
======


.. type:: krpc_KSA_Camera_t

   The game's main camera. Obtained from :func:`krpc_KSA_Camera`.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Camera_Mode(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_CameraMode_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Camera_t instance)
   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Camera_set_Mode(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Camera_t instance, krpc_KSA_CameraMode_t value)

      The mode of the camera.

   

      .. note::

         Setting :macro:`KRPC_KSA_CAMERAMODE_IVA` while the camera is not focused on a vehicle
         with a crew seat sets :macro:`KRPC_KSA_CAMERAMODE_ORBIT` instead, as the game does. A
         change of mode releases the active vehicle's held rotation and translation inputs.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Camera_Azimuth(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Camera_t instance)
   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Camera_set_Azimuth(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Camera_t instance, float value)

      The angle of the camera around the focused object, in degrees. Throws an exception
      in any mode other than orbit and map.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Camera_Elevation(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Camera_t instance)
   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Camera_set_Elevation(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Camera_t instance, float value)

      The angle of the camera above the plane it orbits in, in degrees, between -90 and
      90. Throws an exception in any mode other than orbit and map.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Camera_Distance(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Camera_t instance)
   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Camera_set_Distance(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Camera_t instance, float value)

      The distance from the camera to the focused object, in meters. Throws an exception
      in any mode other than orbit and map.

   

      .. note::

         The camera moves smoothly to a new distance, and stays at least the focused object's
         radius from it in map mode, and half its radius in orbit mode.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Camera_FieldOfView(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Camera_t instance)
   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Camera_set_FieldOfView(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Camera_t instance, float value)

      The camera's vertical field of view, in degrees, between 15 and 120.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Camera_FocusedVehicle(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Vehicle_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Camera_t instance)
   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Camera_set_FocusedVehicle(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Camera_t instance, krpc_KSA_Vehicle_t value)

      Returns the vehicle the camera is focused on, or ``nullptr`` if it is focused on
      something else, and sets the focus to a vehicle.

   

      .. note::

         Setting the focus leaves the active vehicle as it is.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Camera_FocusedBody(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Body_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Camera_t instance)
   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Camera_set_FocusedBody(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Camera_t instance, krpc_KSA_Body_t value)

      Returns the body the camera is focused on, or ``nullptr`` if it is focused on
      something else, and sets the focus to a body.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Camera_FocusedKitten(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Camera_t instance)
   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Camera_set_FocusedKitten(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Camera_t instance, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t value)

      Returns the kitten on EVA the camera is focused on, or ``nullptr`` if it is focused
      on something else, and sets the focus to a kitten on EVA.

   

      .. note::

         Throws an exception if the kitten is not on EVA.



.. type:: krpc_KSA_CameraMode_t

   The mode of the camera. See :func:`krpc_KSA_Camera_Mode`.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_CAMERAMODE_ORBIT

      Orbits the focused object.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_CAMERAMODE_FREE

      Flies freely.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_CAMERAMODE_MAP

      The map view.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_CAMERAMODE_IVA

      Inside the vehicle, from a crew seat.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_CAMERAMODE_FIXED

      Fixed to a part of the vehicle, such as a docking port.
