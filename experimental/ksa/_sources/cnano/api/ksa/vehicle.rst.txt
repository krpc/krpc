Vehicle
=======


.. type:: krpc_KSA_Vehicle_t

   A vehicle in the current system. Obtained from :func:`krpc_KSA_ActiveVehicle`
   or :func:`krpc_KSA_Vehicles`.

   .. note::

      Docking and undocking replace the vehicles involved, so an object for one of them
      throws an exception afterwards.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Vehicle_Name(krpc_connection_t connection, char * * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Vehicle_t instance)
   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Vehicle_set_Name(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Vehicle_t instance, const char * value)

      The name of the vehicle, which is unique in the system.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Vehicle_Destroy(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Vehicle_t instance)

      Destroy the vehicle, killing its crew, as abandoning the vehicle does. Returns once
      the vehicle is gone.


   

      .. note::

         If the vehicle is the active vehicle, the game hands control to a nearby vehicle.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Vehicle_CanRecover(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Vehicle_t instance)

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Vehicle_Recover(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Vehicle_t instance)

      Recover the vehicle, ending its crew's missions, as the game's recover button does.
      Returns once the vehicle is gone.


   

      .. note::

         Throws an exception if the vehicle cannot be recovered, see :func:`krpc_KSA_Vehicle_CanRecover`.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Vehicle_AddBurn(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Burn_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Vehicle_t instance, double ut, float prograde, float normal, float radial)

      Plan a burn, and add it to the vehicle's burn plan.

      :Parameters:

       * **ut** -- Universal time of the burn, in seconds.
       * **prograde** -- Change in velocity in the prograde direction, in meters per second.
       * **normal** -- Change in velocity along the orbit normal, in meters per second.
       * **radial** -- Change in velocity radially outward, in meters per second.

      :returns: The burn.
   

      .. note::

         Returns once the flight computer has timed the burn, if it is the next one. Throws
         an exception if the time is in the past, or beyond the vehicle's predicted
         trajectory.

.. type:: krpc_KSA_Situation_t

   The situation of a vehicle: its contact with the surface, and whether the game is
   simulating it with physics or propagating it along its orbit.
   See :func:`krpc_KSA_Vehicle_Situation`.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_SITUATION_MANEUVERING

      No surface contact, simulated with physics.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_SITUATION_FREEFALL

      No surface contact, on rails.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_SITUATION_ROLLING

      On terrain, simulated with physics.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_SITUATION_LANDED

      At rest on terrain.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_SITUATION_SAILING

      In the ocean, simulated with physics.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_SITUATION_FLOATING

      At rest in the ocean.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_SITUATION_DRAGGING

      On terrain and in the ocean, simulated with physics.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_SITUATION_BOTTOMED

      At rest on terrain and in the ocean.



.. type:: krpc_KSA_VehicleDesign_t
