Crew
====


.. type:: krpc_KSA_Kitten_t

   A kitten in the agency's roster. Obtained from :func:`krpc_KSA_Kittens`,
   :func:`krpc_KSA_Vehicle_Crew` or :func:`krpc_KSA_Seat_Kitten`.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_Name(krpc_connection_t connection, char * * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)

      The kitten's name, which is unique in the roster.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_RosterIndex(krpc_connection_t connection, int32_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)

      The kitten's position in the agency's roster, from 0. See :func:`krpc_KSA_Kittens`.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_Vehicle(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Vehicle_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)

      The vehicle the kitten is in, or ``nullptr`` if it is not in a seat.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_Seat(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Seat_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)

      The seat the kitten is in, or ``nullptr`` if it is not in a seat.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_KilledInAction(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)

      Whether the kitten has been killed in action.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_MissionCount(krpc_connection_t connection, int32_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)

      The number of missions the kitten has flown, including the current one.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_MissionTime(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)

      The time the kitten has spent on missions, including the current one, in seconds.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_DistanceTraveled(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)

      The distance the kitten has traveled on missions, in meters.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_FastestSpeed(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)

      The kitten's highest speed on a mission, in meters per second.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_AcuteDose(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)

      The kitten's acute radiation dose, in sieverts. It heals over time, and causes
      radiation sickness from 1 sievert and death from 4 sieverts.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_LifetimeDose(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)

      The kitten's lifetime radiation dose, in sieverts. The kitten retires after a
      mission that takes it past 0.6 sieverts.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_DoseRate(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)

      The radiation dose rate the kitten receives, in sieverts per second. The vehicle's
      shielding reduces it in a seat, and on EVA the kitten is unshielded. Zero when the
      kitten is not on a mission.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_RadiationSickness(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)

      Whether the kitten has radiation sickness, which keeps it from flying.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_TimeUntilFitToFly(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)

      The time until the kitten has recovered from radiation sickness and can fly, resting
      at home, in seconds. Zero if the kitten does not have radiation sickness.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_RecoveryTime(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)

      The time until the kitten's acute radiation dose has healed below 1 millisievert,
      resting at home, in seconds.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_Retired(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)

      Whether the kitten has retired, having reached its lifetime radiation dose limit.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_OnEva(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)

      Whether the kitten is on EVA.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_GoOnEva(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)

      Leave the kitten's vehicle through its EVA hatch, and return once the kitten is on
      EVA. The kitten becomes the controlled kitten, see :func:`krpc_KSA_ActiveKitten`.


   

      .. note::

         Throws an exception if the kitten is not in a seat, or the vehicle has no EVA hatch.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_Board(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Vehicle_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)

      Board the nearest vehicle with a free seat, and return once the kitten is in the
      seat. The vehicle becomes the controlled vehicle.


      :returns: The vehicle the kitten boarded.
   

      .. note::

         Throws an exception if the kitten is not on EVA, or no EVA hatch with a free seat is
         within one meter.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_LocomotionMode(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_LocomotionMode_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance)

      How the kitten on EVA is moving. Throws an exception if the kitten is not on EVA.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_Position(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_tuple_double_double_double_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance, krpc_KSA_ReferenceFrame_t referenceFrame)

      The position of the kitten, in meters. For a kitten in a seat, the position of the
      seat's part. Throws an exception if the kitten is neither in a seat nor on EVA.

      :Parameters:

       * **referenceFrame** -- The reference frame of the position.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Kitten_Velocity(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_tuple_double_double_double_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t instance, krpc_KSA_ReferenceFrame_t referenceFrame)

      The velocity of the kitten, in meters per second. For a kitten in a seat, the
      velocity of the seat's part. Throws an exception if the kitten is neither in a seat
      nor on EVA.

      :Parameters:

       * **referenceFrame** -- The reference frame of the velocity.

   



.. type:: krpc_KSA_LocomotionMode_t

   How a kitten on EVA is moving. See :func:`krpc_KSA_Kitten_LocomotionMode`.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_LOCOMOTIONMODE_MMU

      Flying with the maneuvering unit, away from any surface.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_LOCOMOTIONMODE_GROUNDED

      Standing or walking on a surface.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_LOCOMOTIONMODE_AIRBORNE

      In the air above a surface, such as during a jump.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_LOCOMOTIONMODE_TUMBLING

      Tumbling after a fall or an impact.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_LOCOMOTIONMODE_RIGHTING

      Getting back up after tumbling.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_LOCOMOTIONMODE_LADDER

      Holding a ladder or grab rail.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_LOCOMOTIONMODE_SWIMMING

      Swimming in an ocean.



.. type:: krpc_KSA_Seat_t

   A crew seat. Obtained from :func:`krpc_KSA_Part_Seats`.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Seat_Part(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Part_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Seat_t instance)

      The part the seat is in.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Seat_Kitten(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Seat_t instance)
   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Seat_set_Kitten(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Seat_t instance, krpc_KSA_Kitten_t value)

      Returns the kitten in the seat, or ``nullptr`` if the seat is empty, and sets the
      kitten in the seat. Setting ``nullptr`` empties the seat.

   

      .. note::

         A kitten in another seat of the vehicle moves to this one. Throws an exception if
         the kitten is killed in action, on EVA, or in another vehicle.
