Flight
======


.. type:: krpc_KSA_Flight_t

   Flight telemetry for a vehicle, relative to the surface of the body it orbits.
   Obtained from :func:`krpc_KSA_Vehicle_Flight`.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Flight_MeanAltitude(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Flight_t instance)

      The altitude above the mean radius of the body, in meters.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Flight_SurfaceAltitude(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Flight_t instance)

      The altitude above the terrain or the ocean surface, whichever is higher, in meters.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Flight_BedrockAltitude(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Flight_t instance)

      The altitude above the terrain, in meters. Over the sea, this is the altitude above
      the sea floor.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Flight_Elevation(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Flight_t instance)

      The height of the terrain below the vehicle, in meters above the mean radius of the
      body. Below the sea, this is the height of the sea floor.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Flight_TimeToImpact(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Flight_t instance)

      The time until the vehicle hits the surface on its current trajectory, in seconds.
      Not a number if its trajectory does not hit the surface.

      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         The game predicts the trajectory on a worker thread, so a change to it shows here
         shortly after.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Flight_Latitude(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Flight_t instance)

      The latitude of the vehicle, in degrees.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Flight_Longitude(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Flight_t instance)

      The longitude of the vehicle, in degrees.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Flight_Speed(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Flight_t instance)

      The speed of the vehicle relative to the surface, in meters per second.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Flight_HorizontalSpeed(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Flight_t instance)

      The horizontal speed of the vehicle relative to the surface, in meters per second.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Flight_VerticalSpeed(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Flight_t instance)

      The vertical speed of the vehicle relative to the surface, in meters per second.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Flight_Pitch(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Flight_t instance)

      The pitch of the vehicle relative to the horizon, in degrees.
      A value between -90° and +90°.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Flight_Heading(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Flight_t instance)

      The heading of the vehicle (its angle relative to north), in degrees.
      A value between 0° and 360°.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Flight_Roll(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Flight_t instance)

      The roll of the vehicle relative to the horizon, in degrees.
      A value between -180° and +180°.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Flight_StaticPressure(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Flight_t instance)

      The static atmospheric pressure at the vehicle, in pascals.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Flight_AtmosphereDensity(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Flight_t instance)

      The density of the atmosphere at the vehicle, in kilograms per cubic meter.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Flight_DynamicPressure(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Flight_t instance)

      The dynamic pressure acting on the vehicle, in pascals.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Flight_GForce(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Flight_t instance)

      The acceleration of the vehicle, excluding gravity, in multiples of standard
      gravity.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Flight_AngleOfAttack(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Flight_t instance)

      The angle between the direction the vehicle points in and its velocity relative to
      the surface, in the vehicle's pitch plane, in degrees. Positive when the nose is
      above the velocity.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Flight_SideslipAngle(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Flight_t instance)

      The angle between the direction the vehicle points in and its velocity relative to
      the surface, in the vehicle's yaw plane, in degrees. Positive when the nose is to
      the right of the velocity.

      :Game Scenes: Flight
