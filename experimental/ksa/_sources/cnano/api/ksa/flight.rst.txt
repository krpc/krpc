   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Flight_SimulateAerodynamicForceAt(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_tuple_double_double_double_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Flight_t instance, krpc_KSA_Body_t body, const krpc_tuple_double_double_double_t * position, const krpc_tuple_double_double_double_t * velocity, krpc_KSA_ReferenceFrame_t referenceFrame)

      The aerodynamic force the vehicle would have at a given position and velocity, in

      :Parameters:

       * **body** -- The body whose atmosphere the vehicle flies through.

       * **velocity** -- The velocity, in meters per second.
       * **referenceFrame** -- The reference frame of the position, velocity and force.

      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         The atmosphere moves with the body's rotation.
