Body
====


.. type:: krpc_KSA_Body_t

   A planet, moon or star. See :func:`krpc_KSA_Bodies`.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_Name(krpc_connection_t connection, char * * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance)

      The name of the body.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_IsStar(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance)

      Whether the body is a star.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_Mass(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance)

      The mass of the body, in kilograms.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_GravitationalParameter(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance)

      The standard gravitational parameter of the body, in cubic meters per second squared.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_Radius(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance)

      The radius of the body, in meters.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_AtmosphereRadius(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance)

      The radius of the top of the atmosphere, in meters. Zero for a body with no
      atmosphere.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_HasAtmosphere(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance)

      Whether the body has an atmosphere.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_SeaLevelPressure(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance)

      The atmospheric pressure at the body's mean radius, in pascals. Zero for a body
      with no atmosphere.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_SeaLevelDensity(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance)

      The density of the atmosphere at the body's mean radius, in kilograms per cubic
      meter. Zero for a body with no atmosphere.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_ScaleHeight(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance)

      The height over which the atmospheric pressure falls by a factor of ``e``, in
      meters. Zero for a body with no atmosphere.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_PressureAt(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance, double altitude)

      The atmospheric pressure at an altitude, in pascals. Zero for a body with no
      atmosphere.

      :Parameters:

       * **altitude** -- Altitude above the body's mean radius, in meters.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_DensityAt(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance, double altitude)

      The density of the atmosphere at an altitude, in kilograms per cubic meter. Zero
      for a body with no atmosphere.

      :Parameters:

       * **altitude** -- Altitude above the body's mean radius, in meters.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_HasOcean(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance)

      Whether the body has an ocean.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_OceanDensity(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance)

      The density of the ocean, in kilograms per cubic meter. Zero for a body with no
      ocean.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_SurfaceGravity(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance)

      The acceleration due to gravity at the body's mean radius, in meters per second
      squared.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_RotationalSpeed(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance)

      The rate at which the body rotates about its axis, in radians per second.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_RotationalPeriod(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance)

      The time the body takes to rotate once about its axis, relative to the stars, in
      seconds. Infinite for a body that does not rotate.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_SphereOfInfluence(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance)

      The radius of the body's sphere of influence, in meters. Not a number for a body
      that orbits nothing.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_Parent(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Body_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance)

      The body this body orbits, or ``nullptr`` if it orbits nothing.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_Satellites(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_object_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance)

      The bodies that orbit this body.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_Orbit(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance)

      The orbit of the body, or ``nullptr`` if it orbits nothing.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_SurfaceHeight(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance, double latitude, double longitude)

      The height of the terrain or the sea surface, whichever is higher, at a point on
      the body, in meters above the body's mean radius.

      :Parameters:

       * **latitude** -- Latitude, in degrees.
       * **longitude** -- Longitude, in degrees.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_BedrockHeight(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance, double latitude, double longitude)

      The height of the terrain at a point on the body, in meters above the body's mean
      radius. Below the sea, this is the height of the sea floor.

      :Parameters:

       * **latitude** -- Latitude, in degrees.
       * **longitude** -- Longitude, in degrees.

   

      .. note::

         Zero for a body with no terrain, such as a gas giant.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_LaunchSites(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_string_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance)

      The names of the launch sites on the body. See :func:`krpc_KSA_LaunchVehicle`.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_Biomes(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_string_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance)

      The names of the body's biomes. Empty for a body with no biomes.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_BiomeAt(krpc_connection_t connection, char * * returnValue, bool * returnValueIsNull, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance, double latitude, double longitude)

      The biome at a point on the body, or ``nullptr`` if the body has no biomes.

      :Parameters:

       * **latitude** -- Latitude, in degrees.
       * **longitude** -- Longitude, in degrees.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_ReferenceFrame(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_ReferenceFrame_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance)

      The reference frame fixed to the body, with its origin at the body's center. The
      z axis points to the north pole, the x axis to latitude and longitude zero, and the
      y axis to latitude zero and longitude 90 degrees east. The frame rotates with the
      body.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_NonRotatingReferenceFrame(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_ReferenceFrame_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance)

      The reference frame with its origin at the body's center that does not rotate. The
      z axis points to the north pole, and the x and y axes are fixed relative to the
      stars.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_Position(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_tuple_double_double_double_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance, krpc_KSA_ReferenceFrame_t referenceFrame)

      The position of the center of the body, in meters.

      :Parameters:

       * **referenceFrame** -- The reference frame of the position.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_Velocity(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_tuple_double_double_double_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance, krpc_KSA_ReferenceFrame_t referenceFrame)

      The velocity of the body, in meters per second.

      :Parameters:

       * **referenceFrame** -- The reference frame of the velocity.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Body_Rotation(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_tuple_double_double_double_double_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Body_t instance, krpc_KSA_ReferenceFrame_t referenceFrame)
