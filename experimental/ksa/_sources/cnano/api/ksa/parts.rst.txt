Parts
=====


.. type:: krpc_KSA_Part_t

   A part of a vehicle. Obtained from :func:`krpc_KSA_Vehicle_Parts`.

   .. note::

      A part keeps its object when the vehicle it belongs to changes, for example when a
      decoupler separates it. Throws an exception once the part no longer exists.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Name(krpc_connection_t connection, char * * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)

      The id of the part's template.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Title(krpc_connection_t connection, char * * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)

      The name of the part, as shown in the game.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Vehicle(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Vehicle_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)

      The vehicle the part belongs to.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Parent(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Part_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)

      The part this part is attached to, or ``nullptr`` for the root part.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Children(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_object_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)

      The parts attached to this part.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Sequence(krpc_connection_t connection, int32_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)

      The number of the sequence that activates the part, or -1 if no sequence does.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Mass(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)

      The mass of the part, including its propellant, in kilograms.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_DryMass(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)

      The mass of the part without its propellant, in kilograms.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Resources(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_object_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)

      The resources the part holds, one for each substance.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Tanks(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_object_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)

      The part's propellant tanks. Empty if it has none.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Batteries(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_object_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)

      The part's batteries.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_SolarPanels(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_object_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)

      The part's solar panels.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Generators(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_object_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)

      The part's generators.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_PowerConsumers(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_object_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)

      The part's power consumers.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Lights(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_object_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)

      The part's lights.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Deployables(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_object_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)

      The part's deployable sections. A part can have several, such as the doors of a
      service module.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_MagneticScoops(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_object_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)

      The part's magnetic scoops. Empty if it has none.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Position(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_tuple_double_double_double_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance, krpc_KSA_ReferenceFrame_t referenceFrame)

      The position of the part's origin, in meters.

      :Parameters:

       * **referenceFrame** -- The reference frame of the position.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Velocity(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_tuple_double_double_double_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance, krpc_KSA_ReferenceFrame_t referenceFrame)

      The velocity of the part's origin, in meters per second.

      :Parameters:

       * **referenceFrame** -- The reference frame of the velocity.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Rotation(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_tuple_double_double_double_double_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance, krpc_KSA_ReferenceFrame_t referenceFrame)

      The rotation of the part's axes, relative to the given frame.

      :Parameters:

       * **referenceFrame** -- The reference frame of the rotation.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_RCS(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_RCS_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)

      The part's RCS thrusters, or ``nullptr`` if it has none.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Decoupler(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Decoupler_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)

      The part's decoupler, or ``nullptr`` if it has none.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Parachute(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Parachute_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)

      The part's parachute, or ``nullptr`` if it has none.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_DockingPort(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_DockingPort_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)

      The part's docking port, or ``nullptr`` if it has none.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Seats(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_object_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)

      The part's crew seats.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Part_Crew(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_object_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Part_t instance)

      The kittens in the part's seats.

   



.. type:: krpc_KSA_Engine_t

   An engine. Obtained from :func:`krpc_KSA_Part_Engine`.

   .. note::

      An active engine fires when the vehicle's engines are on, at the vehicle's throttle.
      See :func:`krpc_KSA_Control_EngineOn` and :func:`krpc_KSA_Control_Throttle`.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Engine_Part(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Part_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Engine_t instance)

      The part the engine is in.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Engine_Gimbals(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_object_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Engine_t instance)

      The gimbals that steer the engine's nozzles. Empty if the engine has none.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Engine_Active(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Engine_t instance)
   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Engine_set_Active(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Engine_t instance, bool value)

      Whether the engine is active, as when its sequence is activated. Setting it returns
      once the change has applied.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Engine_HasPropellant(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Engine_t instance)

      Whether the engine has propellant to burn.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Engine_MinimumThrottle(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Engine_t instance)

      The lowest throttle the engine runs at, between 0 and 1.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Engine_Propellants(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_list_string_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Engine_t instance)

      The names of the substances the engine burns. See :func:`krpc_KSA_Resource_Name`.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Engine_Thrust(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Engine_t instance)

      The thrust of the engine at its current throttle and the current atmospheric
      pressure, in Newtons. Zero when it is not firing.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Engine_AvailableThrust(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Engine_t instance)

      The thrust of the engine at full throttle and the current atmospheric pressure, in
      Newtons. Zero when it is inactive or has no propellant.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Engine_SpecificImpulse(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Engine_t instance)

      The specific impulse of the engine at the current atmospheric pressure, in seconds.
      Zero when it is inactive or has no propellant.

   



.. type:: krpc_KSA_Gimbal_t

   A gimbal that steers an engine nozzle. Obtained from :func:`krpc_KSA_Part_Gimbals` or
   :func:`krpc_KSA_Engine_Gimbals`.

   .. note::

      A gimbal turns about the y and z axes of its mount, which :func:`krpc_KSA_Gimbal_Rotation` gives.
      The flight computer steers it while an engine it belongs to is :func:`krpc_KSA_Gimbal_Active`.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Gimbal_Part(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Part_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Gimbal_t instance)

      The part the gimbal is in.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Gimbal_AngleY(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Gimbal_t instance)

      The angle of the gimbal about its y axis, in degrees.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Gimbal_AngleZ(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Gimbal_t instance)

      The angle of the gimbal about its z axis, in degrees.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Gimbal_MaxAngleY(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Gimbal_t instance)

      The largest angle of the gimbal about its y axis, in degrees. Zero if it does not
      turn about that axis.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Gimbal_MaxAngleZ(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Gimbal_t instance)

      The largest angle of the gimbal about its z axis, in degrees. Zero if it does not
      turn about that axis.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Gimbal_Active(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Gimbal_t instance)

      Whether an engine the gimbal steers is active.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Gimbal_Rotation(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_tuple_double_double_double_double_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Gimbal_t instance, krpc_KSA_ReferenceFrame_t referenceFrame)

      The rotation of the gimbal's mount, whose y and z axes the gimbal turns about.

      :Parameters:

       * **referenceFrame** -- The reference frame of the rotation.

   .. note::

      Active thrusters fire when the vehicle's RCS is on. See :func:`krpc_KSA_Control_RCS`.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_RCS_Part(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Part_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_RCS_t instance)

      The part the thrusters are in.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_RCS_Active(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_RCS_t instance)
   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_RCS_set_Active(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_RCS_t instance, bool value)

      Whether the thrusters are active. Setting it returns once the change has applied.

   



.. type:: krpc_KSA_Decoupler_t

   A decoupler. Obtained from :func:`krpc_KSA_Part_Decoupler`.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Decoupler_Part(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Part_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Decoupler_t instance)

      The part the decoupler is in.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Decoupler_Enabled(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Decoupler_t instance)

      Whether the decoupler can fire.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Decoupler_Decoupled(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Decoupler_t instance)

.. type:: krpc_KSA_Parachute_t

   A parachute. Obtained from :func:`krpc_KSA_Part_Parachute`.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Parachute_Part(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Part_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Parachute_t instance)

      The part the parachute is in.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Parachute_State(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_ParachuteState_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Parachute_t instance)

      The state of the parachute.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Parachute_Fill(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Parachute_t instance)

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Parachute_DeployAltitude(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Parachute_t instance)

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Parachute_Deploy(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Parachute_t instance)

      Arm the parachute, so that it deploys at :func:`krpc_KSA_Parachute_DeployAltitude`. Returns once

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Parachute_Cut(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Parachute_t instance)

      Cut the parachute away. Returns once it is cut. Throws an exception if it cannot
      be cut. See :func:`krpc_KSA_Parachute_CanCut`.


   



.. type:: krpc_KSA_ParachuteState_t

   The state of a parachute. See :func:`krpc_KSA_Parachute_State`.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_PARACHUTESTATE_STOWED

      Packed away.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_PARACHUTESTATE_ARMED

      Waiting to deploy at :func:`krpc_KSA_Parachute_DeployAltitude`.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_PARACHUTESTATE_LINESTRETCH

      Leaving the pack.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_PARACHUTESTATE_REEFED

      Partly open, to limit the opening load.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_PARACHUTESTATE_DISREEFING

      Opening from reefed to full.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_PARACHUTESTATE_FULL

      Fully open.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_PARACHUTESTATE_CUT

      Cut away.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_PARACHUTESTATE_RIPPED

      Torn by too much dynamic pressure.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_PARACHUTESTATE_SEVEREDLINES

      Lost after its lines broke under too much load.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_DockingPort_Part(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Part_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_DockingPort_t instance)

      The part the docking port is in.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_DockingPort_Docked(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_DockingPort_t instance)

      Whether the port is docked.

.. type:: krpc_KSA_Deployable_t

   A part that deploys and retracts, such as a landing leg, a solar panel or a service
   module door. Obtained from :func:`krpc_KSA_Part_Deployables`.

   .. note::

      Deploying or retracting a part moves its symmetric copies with it, as the part's menu
      in the game does.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Deployable_Part(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Part_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Deployable_t instance)

      The part the deployable is in.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Deployable_State(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_DeployableState_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Deployable_t instance)

      The state of the part.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Deployable_Deploy(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Deployable_t instance)

      Deploy the part. Returns once it has started to move.


   

      .. note::

         Throws an exception if the part is broken.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Deployable_Retract(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Deployable_t instance)

      Retract the part. Returns once it has started to move.


   

      .. note::

         Throws an exception if the part is broken.

   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_DEPLOYABLESTATE_RETRACTED

   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_DEPLOYABLESTATE_DEPLOYING

   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_DEPLOYABLESTATE_RETRACTING
