Orbit
=====


.. type:: krpc_KSA_Orbit_t

   An orbit. Obtained from :func:`krpc_KSA_Vehicle_Orbit`, :func:`krpc_KSA_Body_Orbit`,
   :func:`krpc_KSA_Burn_Orbit` or :func:`krpc_KSA_Orbit_NextOrbit`.

   .. note::

      An orbit follows the game's prediction as the trajectory changes. A predicted orbit, from
      :func:`krpc_KSA_Orbit_NextOrbit` or :func:`krpc_KSA_Burn_Orbit`, is a position in the vehicle's or the
      burn's predicted trajectory, and throws an exception while the trajectory is too short to
      reach it.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_Body(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Body_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance)

      The body being orbited.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_Apoapsis(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance)

      The apoapsis of the orbit, in meters, from the center of the body being orbited.
      Not a number for a parabolic or hyperbolic orbit.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_Periapsis(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance)

      The periapsis of the orbit, in meters, from the center of the body being orbited.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_ApoapsisAltitude(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance)

      The apoapsis of the orbit, in meters, above the mean radius of the body being orbited.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_PeriapsisAltitude(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance)

      The periapsis of the orbit, in meters, above the mean radius of the body being orbited.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_SemiMajorAxis(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance)

      The semi-major axis of the orbit, in meters. Negative for a hyperbolic orbit.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_SemiMinorAxis(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance)

      The semi-minor axis of the orbit, in meters. Not a number for a parabolic or
      hyperbolic orbit.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_Speed(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance)

      The current orbital speed, in meters per second.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_Radius(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance)

      The current distance from the center of the body being orbited, in meters.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_Period(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance)

      The orbital period, in seconds. Not a number for a parabolic or hyperbolic orbit.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_MeanMotion(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance)

      The mean angular motion of the orbit, in radians per second.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_SpecificEnergy(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance)

      The specific orbital energy, the sum of the kinetic and potential energy per unit
      mass, in joules per kilogram. Negative for an elliptical orbit.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_TimeToApoapsis(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance)

      The time until the next apoapsis, in seconds. Not a number for a parabolic or
      hyperbolic orbit.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_TimeToPeriapsis(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance)

      The time until the next periapsis, in seconds. Negative once a parabolic or
      hyperbolic orbit has passed it.

      The `eccentricity <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbital_eccentricity>`_
      of the orbit.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_Inclination(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance)

      The `inclination <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbital_inclination>`_
      of the orbit, in radians, against the equator of the body being orbited.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_LongitudeOfAscendingNode(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance)

      The `longitude of the ascending node <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longitude_of_the_ascending_node>`_, in radians.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_ArgumentOfPeriapsis(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance)

      The `argument of periapsis <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argument_of_periapsis>`_, in radians.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_TrueAnomaly(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance)

      The current `true anomaly <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_anomaly>`_,
      in radians.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_MeanAnomaly(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance)

      The current `mean anomaly <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mean_anomaly>`_,
      in radians.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_EccentricAnomaly(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance)

      The current `eccentric anomaly <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eccentric_anomaly>`_, in radians. For a hyperbolic orbit, this is the hyperbolic anomaly.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_TrueAnomalyAtUT(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance, double ut)

      The true anomaly at the given time, in radians.

      :Parameters:

       * **ut** -- Universal time, in seconds.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_UTAtTrueAnomaly(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance, double trueAnomaly)

      The next universal time, from now, at which the orbit reaches the given true
      anomaly, in seconds. Not a number if it never does, such as beyond the asymptote of
      a hyperbolic orbit.

      :Parameters:

       * **trueAnomaly** -- True anomaly, in radians.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_RadiusAt(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance, double ut)

      The distance from the center of the body being orbited at the given time, in
      meters.

      :Parameters:

       * **ut** -- Universal time, in seconds.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_PositionAt(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_tuple_double_double_double_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance, double ut, krpc_KSA_ReferenceFrame_t referenceFrame)

      The position on the orbit at the given time, in meters, relative to where the body
      being orbited is now.

      :Parameters:

       * **ut** -- Universal time, in seconds.
       * **referenceFrame** -- The reference frame of the position.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_VelocityAt(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_tuple_double_double_double_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance, double ut, krpc_KSA_ReferenceFrame_t referenceFrame)

      The orbital velocity at the given time, in meters per second, relative to the body
      being orbited and along the axes of the given frame.

      :Parameters:

       * **ut** -- Universal time, in seconds.
       * **referenceFrame** -- The reference frame of the velocity.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_RelativeInclination(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t orbit)

      The inclination of the orbit relative to another orbit, in radians.

      :Parameters:

       * **orbit** -- The other orbit.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_TrueAnomalyAtAN(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t orbit)

      The true anomaly of the ascending node with another orbit, in radians.

      :Parameters:

       * **orbit** -- The other orbit.

   

      .. note::

         Throws an exception if the orbits are around different bodies.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_TrueAnomalyAtDN(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t orbit)

      The true anomaly of the descending node with another orbit, in radians.

      :Parameters:

       * **orbit** -- The other orbit.

   

      .. note::

         Throws an exception if the orbits are around different bodies.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_TimeOfClosestApproach(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance)

      The universal time of the closest approach on this orbit to the vehicle's target,
      in seconds. Not a number if there is none.

   

      .. note::

         Only the vehicle's current orbit has a target. The game predicts the trajectory on a
         worker thread, so a new target shows here shortly after it is set.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_DistanceAtClosestApproach(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance)

      The distance at the closest approach on this orbit to the vehicle's target, in
      meters. Not a number if there is none.

   

      .. note::

         Only the vehicle's current orbit has a target. The game predicts the trajectory on a
         worker thread, so a new target shows here shortly after it is set.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_StartUT(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance)

      The universal time at which this orbit starts, in seconds. Not a number for the
      orbit of a body.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_EndUT(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance)

      The universal time at which this orbit ends, in seconds. Infinite for an orbit that
      does not end, such as the orbit of a body.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_EndTransition(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_OrbitTransition_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance)

      number if it does neither.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Orbit_NextOrbit(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t instance)

      ``nullptr`` if there is none.

   



.. type:: krpc_KSA_OrbitTransition_t

   How an orbit ends. See :func:`krpc_KSA_Orbit_EndTransition`.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_ORBITTRANSITION_NONE

      The orbit does not end.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_ORBITTRANSITION_ENCOUNTER

      The orbit enters the sphere of influence of another body.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_ORBITTRANSITION_ESCAPE

      The orbit leaves the sphere of influence of the body being orbited.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_ORBITTRANSITION_IMPACT

      The orbit hits the surface of the body being orbited.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_ORBITTRANSITION_BURN

      The orbit ends at a planned burn.
