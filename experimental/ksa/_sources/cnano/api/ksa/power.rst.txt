      A generator runs while its :func:`krpc_KSA_Generator_Engine` is active, and produces power in
      proportion to the engine's throttle.

      The engine that drives the generator, or ``nullptr`` if it has none.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Generator_Active(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Generator_t instance)

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Generator_Power(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Generator_t instance)

.. type:: krpc_KSA_PowerConsumer_t

   A power consumer, which uses electrical power, such as a light. Obtained from
   :func:`krpc_KSA_Part_PowerConsumers`.

   .. macro:: KRPC_CLASS_KSA_POWERCONSUMER

      The service and class ids of the class, for :func:`krpc_KRPC_Type_ClassTypeById`.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_PowerConsumer_Part(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Part_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_PowerConsumer_t instance)

      The part the power consumer is in.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_PowerConsumer_Active(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_PowerConsumer_t instance)

      Whether the part is using power.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_PowerConsumer_Power(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_PowerConsumer_t instance)

      The power the part uses, in watts.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_PowerConsumer_HasPowerSwitch(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_PowerConsumer_t instance)

      Whether the part has a power switch, as a probe core does.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_PowerConsumer_Mode(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_PowerMode_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_PowerConsumer_t instance)
   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_PowerConsumer_set_Mode(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_PowerConsumer_t instance, krpc_KSA_PowerMode_t value)

      The setting of the power switch.

   

      .. note::

         Throws an exception if the part has no power switch.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_PowerConsumer_StandbyInStorms(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_PowerConsumer_t instance)
   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_PowerConsumer_set_StandbyInStorms(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_PowerConsumer_t instance, bool value)

      Whether the part goes on standby, drawing no power, while a radiation storm is under
      way at the vehicle.

   

      .. note::

         The game takes a storm of level 1 or worse on the S scale, as
         :func:`krpc_KSA_Flight_RadiationStormLevel` gives. Throws an exception if the part has
         no power switch.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_PowerConsumer_Status(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_PowerStatus_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_PowerConsumer_t instance)

      The power status of the part.

   

      .. note::

         Throws an exception if the part has no power switch.

.. type:: krpc_KSA_PowerForecast_t

   The charge, draw and supply of the electrical circuit a part is on, and how long its
   batteries last. Obtained from :func:`krpc_KSA_Part_PowerForecast`.

   .. note::

      A circuit is the parts joined by connections that carry electricity. This is the
      forecast the game shows for a probe core.

   .. macro:: KRPC_CLASS_KSA_POWERFORECAST

      The service and class ids of the class, for :func:`krpc_KRPC_Type_ClassTypeById`.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_PowerForecast_HasBattery(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_PowerForecast_t instance)

      Whether the circuit has a battery.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_PowerForecast_Charge(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_PowerForecast_t instance)

      The energy stored in the circuit's batteries, in joules.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_PowerForecast_Draw(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_PowerForecast_t instance)

      The power the parts on the circuit draw, in watts.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_PowerForecast_Supply(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_PowerForecast_t instance)

      The power the solar panels and generators on the circuit supply, in watts.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_PowerForecast_TimeLeft(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_PowerForecast_t instance)

      The time until the batteries run out, in seconds. Infinite while the supply meets
      the draw, and zero when the batteries are empty or there are none.

   



.. type:: krpc_KSA_Avionics_t

   The avionics of a probe core, which radiation wears out and upsets. Obtained from
   :func:`krpc_KSA_Part_Avionics` or :func:`krpc_KSA_Vehicle_Avionics`.

   .. note::

      The avionics take a dose only while :func:`krpc_KSA_RadiationEnabled` is true. Switched
      off or on standby, they take half the dose and no upsets.

   .. macro:: KRPC_CLASS_KSA_AVIONICS

      The service and class ids of the class, for :func:`krpc_KRPC_Type_ClassTypeById`.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Avionics_Part(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Part_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Avionics_t instance)

      The part the avionics are in.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Avionics_Health(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_AvionicsHealth_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Avionics_t instance)

      The health of the avionics.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Avionics_Dose(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Avionics_t instance)

      The radiation dose the avionics have taken, in grays of silicon.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Avionics_Tolerance(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Avionics_t instance)

      The dose at which the avionics fail, in grays of silicon.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Avionics_DoseRate(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Avionics_t instance)

      The rate at which the avionics take a dose, in grays of silicon per second,
      averaged over about an hour. Not a number until the avionics have taken a dose.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Avionics_TimeLeft(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Avionics_t instance)

      The time until the avionics fail at :func:`krpc_KSA_Avionics_DoseRate`, in seconds. Infinite
      while they take no dose, and zero once they have failed.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Avionics_Rebooting(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Avionics_t instance)

      Whether the avionics are rebooting after a radiation upset.

   .. macro:: KRPC_ENUMERATION_KSA_AVIONICSHEALTH
