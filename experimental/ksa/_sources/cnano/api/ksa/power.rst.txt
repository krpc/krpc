.. type:: krpc_KSA_SolarPanel_t

   A solar panel, which turns sunlight into electrical power. Obtained from :func:`krpc_KSA_Part_SolarPanels`.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_SolarPanel_Part(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Part_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_SolarPanel_t instance)

      The part the solar panel is in.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_SolarPanel_Active(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_SolarPanel_t instance)

      Whether the solar panel can produce power, because it is deployed or does not deploy.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_SolarPanel_Power(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_SolarPanel_t instance)

      The power the solar panel produces, in watts.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_SolarPanel_SunAngle(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_SolarPanel_t instance)

      The angle between the solar panel's face and the direction to the sun, in degrees.
      Zero when the panel faces the sun.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_SolarPanel_Occluded(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_SolarPanel_t instance)

      Whether a body blocks the sunlight to the solar panel.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_SolarPanel_Deployable(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Deployable_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_SolarPanel_t instance)

      The deployable section that opens the solar panel, or ``nullptr`` for a panel that
      does not deploy.

   



.. type:: krpc_KSA_Generator_t

   A generator, which produces electrical power. Obtained from :func:`krpc_KSA_Part_Generators`.

   .. note::

      A generator runs while its :func:`krpc_KSA_Generator_Engine` is active, and produces power in
      proportion to the engine's throttle.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Generator_Part(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Part_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Generator_t instance)

      The part the generator is in.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Generator_Engine(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Engine_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Generator_t instance)

      The engine that drives the generator, or ``nullptr`` if it has none.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Generator_Active(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Generator_t instance)

      Whether the generator is producing power.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Generator_Power(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Generator_t instance)

      The power the generator produces, in watts.

   



.. type:: krpc_KSA_PowerConsumer_t

   :func:`krpc_KSA_Part_PowerConsumers`.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_PowerConsumer_Part(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Part_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_PowerConsumer_t instance)

      The part the power consumer is in.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_PowerConsumer_Active(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_PowerConsumer_t instance)

      Whether the part is using power.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_PowerConsumer_Power(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_PowerConsumer_t instance)

      The power the part uses, in watts.

   



.. type:: krpc_KSA_Light_t

   A light. Obtained from :func:`krpc_KSA_Part_Lights`.

   .. note::

      The vehicle's lights switch on and off together, see :func:`krpc_KSA_Control_Lights`.
      Cabin lights are always on.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Light_Part(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Part_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Light_t instance)

      The part the light is in.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Light_Active(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Light_t instance)

      Whether the light is on.
