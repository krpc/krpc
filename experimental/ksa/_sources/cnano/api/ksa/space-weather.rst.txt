Space Weather
=============


.. type:: krpc_KSA_CoronalMassEjection_t

   A coronal mass ejection: a cloud of plasma thrown out by a star. One that reaches a body
   with a magnetosphere can cause a geomagnetic storm. See
   :func:`krpc_KSA_Body_RecentCoronalMassEjections` and
   :func:`krpc_KSA_Flight_IncomingCoronalMassEjections`.

   .. macro:: KRPC_CLASS_KSA_CORONALMASSEJECTION

      The service and class ids of the class, for :func:`krpc_KRPC_Type_ClassTypeById`.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_CoronalMassEjection_Star(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Body_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_CoronalMassEjection_t instance)

      The star that threw out the ejection.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_CoronalMassEjection_UT(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_CoronalMassEjection_t instance)

      The universal time at which the ejection left the star, in seconds.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_CoronalMassEjection_Speed(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_CoronalMassEjection_t instance)

      The speed of the ejection as it leaves the star, in :math:`\mathrm{m/s}`.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_CoronalMassEjection_ArrivalTime(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_CoronalMassEjection_t instance, krpc_KSA_Vehicle_t vehicle)

      The universal time at which the shock of the ejection is forecast to reach the
      vehicle, in seconds. Not a number if the ejection misses the vehicle, or has
      already reached it.

      :Parameters:

       * **vehicle** -- The vehicle.

   

      .. note::

         The forecast the game's space weather window shows. It is uncertain by up to
         :func:`krpc_KSA_CoronalMassEjection_ArrivalUncertainty`, which shrinks as the ejection nears.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_CoronalMassEjection_ArrivalUncertainty(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_CoronalMassEjection_t instance, krpc_KSA_Vehicle_t vehicle)

      The uncertainty of :func:`krpc_KSA_CoronalMassEjection_ArrivalTime`, in seconds. Not a number if the
      ejection misses the vehicle, or has already reached it.

      :Parameters:

       * **vehicle** -- The vehicle.
