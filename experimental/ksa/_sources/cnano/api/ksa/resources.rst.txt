Resources
=========


.. type:: krpc_KSA_Resource_t

   A substance held in tanks, such as a propellant, in a part or across a whole vehicle.
   Obtained from :func:`krpc_KSA_Part_Resources` or :func:`krpc_KSA_Vehicle_Resources`.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Resource_Name(krpc_connection_t connection, char * * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Resource_t instance)

      The name of the substance.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Resource_Amount(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Resource_t instance)

      The mass of the substance held, in kilograms.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Resource_Max(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Resource_t instance)

      The mass of the substance the tanks can hold, in kilograms.

   



.. type:: krpc_KSA_Tank_t

   A propellant tank. Obtained from :func:`krpc_KSA_Part_Tanks` or :func:`krpc_KSA_Vehicle_Tanks`.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Tank_Part(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Part_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Tank_t instance)

      The part the tank is in.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Tank_Volume(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Tank_t instance)

      The volume of the tank, in cubic meters.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Tank_RatedPressure(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Tank_t instance)

      The pressure the tank stores gas at, in pascals. Zero if the tank cannot store gas.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Tank_FilledFraction(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Tank_t instance)

      The fraction of the tank's volume that is filled, between 0 and 1.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Tank_PropellantUseEnabled(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Tank_t instance)
   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Tank_set_PropellantUseEnabled(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Tank_t instance, bool value)

      Whether engines can draw propellant from the tank.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Tank_TransferMode(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_TankTransferMode_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Tank_t instance)
   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Tank_set_TransferMode(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Tank_t instance, krpc_KSA_TankTransferMode_t value)

      The direction the tank transfers propellant in.

   

      .. note::

         Propellant flows from tanks transferring out to tanks of the same vehicle transferring
         in, and the transfer draws battery power. Setting a transfer throws an exception if
         the running version of KSA does not support it.



.. type:: krpc_KSA_TankTransferMode_t

   The direction a tank transfers propellant in. See :func:`krpc_KSA_Tank_TransferMode`.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_TANKTRANSFERMODE_NONE

      No transfer.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_TANKTRANSFERMODE_IN

      Into the tank, from tanks transferring out.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_TANKTRANSFERMODE_OUT

      Out of the tank, to tanks transferring in.



.. type:: krpc_KSA_FuelLink_t

   A fuel line between two parts of a vehicle, which lets propellant flow between them.
   Obtained from :func:`krpc_KSA_Vehicle_FuelLinks`.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_FuelLink_PartA(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Part_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_FuelLink_t instance)

      One of the parts the link joins.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_FuelLink_PartB(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Part_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_FuelLink_t instance)

      The other part the link joins.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_FuelLink_Flow(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_FuelLinkFlow_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_FuelLink_t instance)
   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_FuelLink_set_Flow(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_FuelLink_t instance, krpc_KSA_FuelLinkFlow_t value)

      The direction propellant flows through the link.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_FuelLink_Enabled(krpc_connection_t connection, bool * returnValue, krpc_KSA_FuelLink_t instance)
   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_FuelLink_set_Enabled(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_FuelLink_t instance, bool value)

      Whether propellant can flow through the link.

   



.. type:: krpc_KSA_FuelLinkFlow_t

   The direction propellant flows through a fuel link. See :func:`krpc_KSA_FuelLink_Flow`.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_FUELLINKFLOW_ATOB

      From :func:`krpc_KSA_FuelLink_PartA` to :func:`krpc_KSA_FuelLink_PartB`.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_FUELLINKFLOW_BTOA

      From :func:`krpc_KSA_FuelLink_PartB` to :func:`krpc_KSA_FuelLink_PartA`.


   .. macro:: KRPC_KSA_FUELLINKFLOW_BOTH

      Either way.
