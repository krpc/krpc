Life Support
============


.. type:: krpc_KSA_LifeSupport_t

   How long the supplies of a vehicle, a habitat zone or a kitten's suit last. Obtained
   from :func:`krpc_KSA_Vehicle_LifeSupport`, :func:`krpc_KSA_HabitatZone_LifeSupport` or
   :func:`krpc_KSA_Kitten_LifeSupport`.

   .. note::

      The times count down only while :func:`krpc_KSA_LifeSupportEnabled` is true. Air
      lasts forever in breathable air outside the vehicle.

   .. macro:: KRPC_CLASS_KSA_LIFESUPPORT

      The service and class ids of the class, for :func:`krpc_KRPC_Type_ClassTypeById`.

.. type:: krpc_KSA_Mission_t

   :func:`krpc_KSA_Vehicle_Mission`.

      habitat zone against them. See :func:`krpc_KSA_HabitatZone_MissionSupplies`.

      The service and class ids of the class, for :func:`krpc_KRPC_Type_ClassTypeById`.

      The body the mission goes to, or ``nullptr`` if it has no destination. For a low
      orbit, this is the home body.

.. type:: krpc_KSA_MissionSupplies_t

   Whether the supplies of a habitat zone last its crew through the vehicle's mission, as
   the life support window shows it. Obtained from :func:`krpc_KSA_HabitatZone_MissionSupplies`.

   .. note::

      The check takes the legs left in the mission as planned, counted from the vehicle's
      launch, and the kittens planned for each leg. The times are from now.

      mission, in seconds. Zero when the supplies last.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_MissionSupplies_FirstSupply(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_LifeSupportSupply_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_MissionSupplies_t instance)

      this habitat holds fewer, or the other has less room.

         disabled, the habitats are in different zones, or the zone has no crew able to
         move them.
