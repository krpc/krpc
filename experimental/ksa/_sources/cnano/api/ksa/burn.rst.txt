Burn
====


.. type:: krpc_KSA_Burn_t

   A planned burn, the game's equivalent of a maneuver node. Obtained from
   :func:`krpc_KSA_Vehicle_Burns` or :func:`krpc_KSA_Vehicle_AddBurn`.

   .. note::

      The change in velocity is given along the vehicle's orbit at the time of the burn:
      prograde, along the orbit normal, and radially outward. An object for a burn throws an
      exception once the burn is removed. The flight computer removes a burn it completes
      when another burn follows it, and keeps the last one.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Burn_UT(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Burn_t instance)

      The universal time of the burn, in seconds. The burn is centered on this time.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Burn_TimeTo(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Burn_t instance)

      The time until :func:`krpc_KSA_Burn_UT`, in seconds. Negative once that time has passed.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Burn_Prograde(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Burn_t instance)
   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Burn_set_Prograde(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Burn_t instance, float value)

      The change in velocity in the prograde direction, in meters per second.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Burn_Normal(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Burn_t instance)
   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Burn_set_Normal(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Burn_t instance, float value)

      The change in velocity along the orbit normal, in meters per second.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Burn_Radial(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Burn_t instance)
   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Burn_set_Radial(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Burn_t instance, float value)

      The change in velocity radially outward, in meters per second.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Burn_DeltaV(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Burn_t instance)

      The magnitude of the change in velocity, in meters per second.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Burn_RemainingDeltaV(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Burn_t instance)

      The change in velocity still to burn, in meters per second. Less than
      :func:`krpc_KSA_Burn_DeltaV` once the vehicle has started the burn.

   

      .. note::

         The flight computer tracks the progress of the next burn only. For later burns this
         is :func:`krpc_KSA_Burn_DeltaV`.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Burn_IgnitionTime(krpc_connection_t connection, double * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Burn_t instance)

      The universal time at which to start the burn, in seconds. Not a number for a burn
      other than the next one, and until the flight computer has timed it.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Burn_BurnTime(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Burn_t instance)

      The time the remaining burn takes with the active engines, in seconds. Not a number
      for a burn other than the next one, and until the flight computer has timed it.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Burn_Orbit(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Orbit_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Burn_t instance)

      The orbit after the burn.

   

      .. note::

         The game predicts it in the solver step after the burn changes, so it reflects a
         change to the burn one update later.

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Burn_OrbitAverageDoseRate(krpc_connection_t connection, float * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Burn_t instance)

      The radiation dose rate averaged over the orbit after the burn, in sieverts per
      second. Not a number if the body has no magnetosphere, or the orbit dips below the
      surface.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Burn_ReferenceFrame(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_ReferenceFrame_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Burn_t instance)

      The reference frame with its origin at the position of the burn, and axes along
      the orbit at the time of the burn. The x axis points prograde, the y axis along the
      orbit normal and the z axis radially outward, perpendicular to the x axis. The
      frame does not move or rotate relative to the body being orbited.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Burn_Position(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_tuple_double_double_double_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Burn_t instance, krpc_KSA_ReferenceFrame_t referenceFrame)

      The position of the burn, in meters, relative to where the body being orbited is
      now.

      :Parameters:

       * **referenceFrame** -- The reference frame of the position.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Burn_Direction(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_tuple_double_double_double_t * returnValue, krpc_KSA_Burn_t instance, krpc_KSA_ReferenceFrame_t referenceFrame)

      The direction of the change in velocity at the time of the burn, as a unit vector.

      :Parameters:

       * **referenceFrame** -- The reference frame of the direction.

   

   .. function:: krpc_error_t krpc_KSA_Burn_Remove(krpc_connection_t connection, krpc_KSA_Burn_t instance)

      Remove the burn from the vehicle's burn plan.
