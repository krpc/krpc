Flight
======


.. class:: Flight

   Flight telemetry for a vehicle, relative to the surface of the body it orbits.
   Obtained from :func:`Vehicle::flight`.

   .. function:: double mean_altitude()

      The altitude above the mean radius of the body, in meters.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: double surface_altitude()

      The altitude above the terrain or the ocean surface, whichever is higher, in meters.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: double bedrock_altitude()

      The altitude above the terrain, in meters. Over the sea, this is the altitude above
      the sea floor.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: double elevation()

      The height of the terrain below the vehicle, in meters above the mean radius of the
      body. Below the sea, this is the height of the sea floor.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float time_to_impact()

      The time until the vehicle hits the surface on its current trajectory, in seconds.
      Not a number if its trajectory does not hit the surface.

      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         The game predicts the trajectory on a worker thread, so a change to it shows here
         shortly after.

   .. function:: double latitude()

      The latitude of the vehicle, in degrees.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: double longitude()

      The longitude of the vehicle, in degrees.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: std::optional<std::string> biome()

      The biome below the vehicle, or ``NULL`` if the body has no biomes.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float speed()

      The speed of the vehicle relative to the surface, in meters per second.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float horizontal_speed()

      The horizontal speed of the vehicle relative to the surface, in meters per second.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float vertical_speed()

      The vertical speed of the vehicle relative to the surface, in meters per second.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float pitch()

      The pitch of the vehicle relative to the horizon, in degrees.
      A value between -90° and +90°.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float heading()

      The heading of the vehicle (its angle relative to north), in degrees.
      A value between 0° and 360°.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float roll()

      The roll of the vehicle relative to the horizon, in degrees.
      A value between -180° and +180°.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float static_pressure()

      The static atmospheric pressure at the vehicle, in pascals.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float atmosphere_density()

      The density of the atmosphere at the vehicle, in kilograms per cubic meter.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float static_air_temperature()

      The temperature of the atmosphere at the vehicle, in kelvin. Zero outside the
      atmosphere.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float speed_of_sound()

      The speed of sound in the atmosphere at the vehicle, in meters per second. Zero
      outside the atmosphere.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float mach()

      The speed of the vehicle relative to the surface, as a multiple of the speed of
      sound. Zero outside the atmosphere.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: OutsideAir outside_air()

      Whether a kitten can breathe the air outside the vehicle.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float dynamic_pressure()

      The dynamic pressure acting on the vehicle, in pascals.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: double depth()

      The depth of the vehicle's center of mass below the ocean surface, in meters. Zero
      above the surface.

      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         The ocean surface includes its waves.

   .. function:: float submerged_portion()

      The fraction of the vehicle's volume below the ocean surface, between 0 and 1.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float buoyant_force()

      The buoyant force on the vehicle from the ocean and the atmosphere, in newtons. It
      acts against gravity.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float dose_rate()

      The radiation dose rate at the vehicle, in sieverts per second. Includes any solar
      particle event under way.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float orbit_average_dose_rate()

      The radiation dose rate averaged over the vehicle's orbit, in sieverts per second.
      Includes any solar particle event under way. Not a number if the body has no
      magnetosphere, or the orbit dips below the surface.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float solar_wind_speed()

      The speed of the solar wind at the vehicle, in :math:`\mathrm{m/s}`. Zero if no star
      has a solar wind.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float solar_wind_pressure()

      The dynamic pressure of the solar wind at the vehicle, in pascals.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float solar_wind_northward_field()

      The northward component of the magnetic field carried by the solar wind at the
      vehicle, in teslas. A southward field, a negative value, drives geomagnetic storms.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float proton_flux()

      The flux of solar energetic protons at the vehicle during a solar particle event, in
      protons per square centimeter per second per steradian. Zero outside an event.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: int32_t radiation_storm_level()

      The level of the radiation storm at the vehicle, from 0 for none to 5 for the
      most severe. The S level of the game's space weather window.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: std::vector<CoronalMassEjection> incoming_coronal_mass_ejections()

      The coronal mass ejections heading for the vehicle, as the game's space weather
      window lists them.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float g_force()

      The acceleration of the vehicle, excluding gravity, in multiples of standard
      gravity.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float angle_of_attack()

      The angle between the direction the vehicle points in and its velocity relative to
      the surface, in the vehicle's pitch plane, in degrees. Positive when the nose is
      above the velocity.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float sideslip_angle()

      The angle between the direction the vehicle points in and its velocity relative to
      the surface, in the vehicle's yaw plane, in degrees. Positive when the nose is to
      the right of the velocity.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float drag()

      The part of the aerodynamic force along the airflow, in newtons.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: float lift()

      The part of the aerodynamic force across the airflow, in newtons.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: std::tuple<double, double, double> aerodynamic_force(ReferenceFrame reference_frame)

      The aerodynamic force on the vehicle, in newtons.

      :Parameters:

       * **reference_frame** -- The reference frame of the force.

      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         The game computes the force from the surfaces of the vehicle's hull.

   .. function:: std::tuple<double, double, double> aerodynamic_torque(ReferenceFrame reference_frame)

      The aerodynamic torque on the vehicle about its center of mass, in
      :math:`\mathrm{N \cdot m}`. The vector points along the axis of the torque.

      :Parameters:

       * **reference_frame** -- The reference frame of the torque.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: std::tuple<double, double, double> simulate_aerodynamic_force_at(Body body, std::tuple<double, double, double> position, std::tuple<double, double, double> velocity, ReferenceFrame reference_frame)

      The aerodynamic force the vehicle would have at a given position and velocity, in
      its current attitude and rotation, in newtons.

      :Parameters:

       * **body** -- The body whose atmosphere the vehicle flies through.
       * **position** -- The position, in meters.
       * **velocity** -- The velocity, in meters per second.
       * **reference_frame** -- The reference frame of the position, velocity and force.

      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         The atmosphere moves with the body's rotation.

   .. function:: std::tuple<double, double, double> prograde(ReferenceFrame reference_frame)

      The unit vector of the vehicle's velocity relative to the surface.

      :Parameters:

       * **reference_frame** -- The reference frame of the direction.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: std::tuple<double, double, double> normal(ReferenceFrame reference_frame)

      The unit vector normal to the plane of the vehicle's position and its velocity
      relative to the surface.

      :Parameters:

       * **reference_frame** -- The reference frame of the direction.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: std::tuple<double, double, double> radial(ReferenceFrame reference_frame)

      The unit vector pointing radially outward, perpendicular to :func:`Flight::prograde`
      and :func:`Flight::normal`.

      :Parameters:

       * **reference_frame** -- The reference frame of the direction.

      :Game Scenes: Flight



.. namespace:: krpc::services::KSA
.. enum-struct:: OutsideAir

   Whether a kitten can breathe the air outside a vehicle. See
   :func:`Flight::outside_air`.


   .. enumerator:: no_atmosphere

      Outside the atmosphere, or at a body with none.


   .. enumerator:: no_oxygen

      An atmosphere with no oxygen.


   .. enumerator:: too_thin

      An atmosphere whose oxygen pressure is too low to breathe.


   .. enumerator:: breathable

      Breathable air.
