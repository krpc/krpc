Life Support
============


.. class:: LifeSupport

   How long the supplies of a vehicle, a habitat zone or a kitten's suit last. Obtained
   from :func:`Vehicle::life_support`, :func:`HabitatZone::life_support` or
   :func:`Kitten::life_support`.

   .. note::

      The times count down only while :func:`life_support_enabled` is true. Air
      lasts forever in breathable air outside the vehicle.

   .. function:: int32_t kittens()

      The number of kittens the supplies are shared between.

   

   .. function:: float air_mass()

      The mass of oxygen in the crew's tanks, in kilograms.

   

   .. function:: float water_mass()

      The mass of water in the crew's tanks, in kilograms.

   

   .. function:: int32_t meals()

      The number of meals.

   

   .. function:: float air_time()

      The time the air lasts, in seconds. Infinite with no crew, or in breathable air.

   

   .. function:: float scrubber_time()

      The time the carbon dioxide scrubbers last, in seconds. Infinite with no crew.

   

   .. function:: float water_time()

      The time the water lasts, in seconds. Infinite with no crew.

   

   .. function:: float food_time()

      The time the food lasts, in seconds. Infinite with no crew.

   

   .. function:: float time()

      The time until the first supply runs out, in seconds. A suit counts its air and
      scrubber only. Infinite with no crew.

   

   .. function:: LifeSupportSupply limiting_supply()

      The supply that runs out first. Air when every time is infinite, as with no crew.

   

   .. function:: LifeSupportLevel level()

      How long the supplies last, as the game's life support gauge shows it.

   

   .. function:: float time_to_next_level()

      The time until :func:`LifeSupport::level` next changes, in seconds. Infinite with no crew.

.. class:: HabitatZone

   The connected habitable parts of a vehicle, which share their crew's supplies.
   Obtained from :func:`Vehicle::habitat_zones` or :func:`Habitat::zone`.

   .. note::

      Parts join a zone through connections the crew can pass, such as an open docking
      port hatch.

   .. function:: Vehicle vehicle()

      The vehicle the zone is in.

   

   .. function:: std::vector<Part> parts()

      The parts in the zone.

   

   .. function:: std::vector<Habitat> habitats()

      The habitats in the zone.

   

   .. function:: std::vector<Seat> seats()

      The crew seats in the zone.

   

   .. function:: std::vector<Kitten> crew()

      The kittens in the zone's seats.

   

   .. function:: std::vector<HabitatZone> linked_zones()

      The zones that join this one when closed docking port hatches between them open.

   

   .. function:: std::vector<DockingPort> hatches_to(HabitatZone zone)

      The closed docking port hatches that join this zone to a linked zone when they
      open.

      :Parameters:

       * **zone** -- A zone in :func:`HabitatZone::linked_zones`.

   

      .. note::

         Throws an exception if the zone is not linked to this one.

   .. function:: float habitable_volume()

      The volume the crew can live in, in cubic meters.

   

   .. function:: float storage_volume()

      The volume for stored supplies, in cubic meters.

   

   .. function:: LifeSupport life_support()

      How long the zone's supplies last its crew.

   

   .. function:: std::optional<MissionSupplies> mission_supplies()

      Whether the zone's supplies last its crew through the vehicle's mission. ``NULL``
      without a feasible mission with time left, or while
      :func:`life_support_enabled` is false.

   

   .. function:: int32_t planned_kittens(MissionLeg leg)

      The number of kittens planned in the zone for a leg of the vehicle's mission. The
      plan defaults to the kittens seated in the zone.

      :Parameters:

       * **leg** -- The leg.

   



.. class:: Mission

   The mission planned for a vehicle in the editor's life support window. Obtained from
   :func:`Vehicle::mission`.

   .. note::

      The game times the legs of the mission from the plan, and checks the supplies of each
      habitat zone against them. See :func:`HabitatZone::mission_supplies`.

   .. function:: MissionDestination destination()

      Where the mission goes.

   

   .. function:: std::optional<Body> body()

      The body the mission goes to, or ``NULL`` if it has no destination. For a low
      orbit, this is the home body.

   

   .. function:: MissionArrival arrival()

      What the mission does at its destination.

   

   .. function:: MissionTrip trip()

      Whether the mission comes back.

   

   .. function:: float stay()

      The time planned at the destination, in seconds.

   

      .. note::

         The game rounds a stay before a return up to the next transfer window home. See
         :func:`Mission::stay_time`.

   .. function:: bool custom()

      Whether the mission has a length set by the player, in place of legs timed from
      its destination. See :func:`Mission::custom_length`.

   

   .. function:: float custom_length()

      The length of a mission with :func:`Mission::custom` set, in seconds.

   

   .. function:: bool feasible()

      Whether the game can time the legs of the mission. A mission with no destination,
      or a flyby with no way back, cannot be timed.

   

      .. note::

         The leg times are zero when this is false.

   .. function:: float out_time()

      The planned time of the way out, in seconds.

   

   .. function:: float stay_time()

      The planned time at the destination, in seconds.

   

   .. function:: float back_time()

      The planned time of the way back, in seconds. Zero for a one way trip.

   

   .. function:: float return_wait()

      The time from arriving at the destination until the next transfer window home, in
      seconds. Zero when there is no wait.

   

   .. function:: float total_time()

      The planned length of the mission, in seconds.

   

   .. function:: float time_left()

      The time left in the mission as planned, counted from the vehicle's launch, in
      seconds.

   

   .. function:: double arrival_ut()

      The universal time the vehicle arrived at the destination body, in seconds. Not a
      number until it has.

   

      .. note::

         The game records the arrival for a mission to another body.

   .. function:: double departure_ut()

      The universal time the vehicle left the destination body, in seconds. Not a number
      until it has.

.. class:: MissionSupplies

   Whether the supplies of a habitat zone last its crew through the vehicle's mission, as
   the life support window shows it. Obtained from :func:`HabitatZone::mission_supplies`.

   .. note::

      The check takes the legs left in the mission as planned, counted from the vehicle's
      launch, and the kittens planned for each leg. The times are from now.

   .. function:: MissionSupplyVerdict verdict()

      Whether the supplies last the mission.

   

   .. function:: float short_by()

      The time by which the first supply to run out falls short of the end of the
      mission, in seconds. Zero when the supplies last.

   

   .. function:: LifeSupportSupply first_supply()

      The supply that runs out first.

   

   .. function:: float time_left(LifeSupportSupply supply)

      The time until a supply runs out with the crew planned for each leg, in seconds.
      Infinite if it lasts the mission.

      :Parameters:

       * **supply** -- The supply.

   

      .. note::

         Air lasts while the vehicle is in breathable air.

   .. function:: float end()

      The time until the zone has no crew left in the mission, in seconds.

   .. enumerator:: enough

   .. enumerator:: short_

.. class:: Habitat

   A habitable or storage space in a part, which holds meals and carbon dioxide scrubber
   canisters. Obtained from :func:`Part::habitats`.

   .. function:: Part part()

      The part the habitat is in.

   

   .. function:: HabitatZone zone()

      The habitat zone the habitat is in.

   

   .. function:: float habitable_volume()

      The volume the crew can live in, in cubic meters.

   

   .. function:: float storage_volume()

      The volume for stored supplies, in cubic meters.

   

   .. function:: int32_t meals()

      The number of meals in the habitat.

   

   .. function:: int32_t canisters()

      The number of carbon dioxide scrubber canisters in the habitat.

   

   .. function:: int32_t meal_capacity()

      The number of meals the habitat holds when full.

   

   .. function:: int32_t canister_capacity()

      The number of carbon dioxide scrubber canisters the habitat holds when full.

   

   .. function:: void transfer(Habitat to, int32_t meals = 0, int32_t canisters = 0)

      Moves meals and canisters to another habitat in the same zone. Moves fewer when
      this habitat holds fewer, or the other has less room.

      :Parameters:

       * **to** -- The habitat to move the supplies to.
       * **meals** -- The number of meals to move.
       * **canisters** -- The number of canisters to move.

   

      .. note::

         A kitten in the zone moves the supplies. Throws an exception if life support is
         disabled, the habitats are in different zones, or the zone has no crew able to
         move them.
