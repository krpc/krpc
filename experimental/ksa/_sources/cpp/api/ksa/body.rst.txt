Body
====


.. class:: Body

   A planet, moon or star. See :func:`bodies`.

   .. function:: std::string name()

      The name of the body.

   

   .. function:: double mass()

      The mass of the body, in kilograms.

   

   .. function:: double gravitational_parameter()

      The standard gravitational parameter of the body, in cubic meters per second squared.

   

   .. function:: double radius()

      The radius of the body, in meters.

   

   .. function:: double atmosphere_radius()

      The radius of the top of the atmosphere, in meters. Zero for a body with no
      atmosphere.

   

   .. function:: double surface_gravity()

      The acceleration due to gravity at the body's mean radius, in meters per second
      squared.

   

   .. function:: double rotational_speed()

      The rate at which the body rotates about its axis, in radians per second.

   

   .. function:: double rotational_period()

      The time the body takes to rotate once about its axis, relative to the stars, in
      seconds. Infinite for a body that does not rotate.

   

   .. function:: double sphere_of_influence()

      The radius of the body's sphere of influence, in meters. Not a number for a body
      that orbits nothing.

   

   .. function:: std::optional<Body> parent()

      The body this body orbits, or ``NULL`` if it orbits nothing.

   

   .. function:: std::vector<Body> satellites()

      The bodies that orbit this body.

   

   .. function:: std::optional<Orbit> orbit()

      The orbit of the body, or ``NULL`` if it orbits nothing.

   

   .. function:: double surface_height(double latitude, double longitude)

      The height of the terrain or the sea surface, whichever is higher, at a point on
      the body, in meters above the body's mean radius.

      :Parameters:

       * **latitude** -- Latitude, in degrees.
       * **longitude** -- Longitude, in degrees.

   

   .. function:: double bedrock_height(double latitude, double longitude)

      The height of the terrain at a point on the body, in meters above the body's mean
      radius. Below the sea, this is the height of the sea floor.

      :Parameters:

       * **latitude** -- Latitude, in degrees.
       * **longitude** -- Longitude, in degrees.

   

      .. note::

         Zero for a body with no terrain, such as a gas giant.

   .. function:: std::vector<std::string> launch_sites()

      The names of the launch sites on the body. See :func:`launch_vehicle`.

   

   .. function:: ReferenceFrame reference_frame()

      The reference frame fixed to the body, with its origin at the body's center. The
      z axis points to the north pole, the x axis to latitude and longitude zero, and the
      y axis to latitude zero and longitude 90 degrees east. The frame rotates with the
      body.

   

   .. function:: ReferenceFrame non_rotating_reference_frame()

      The reference frame with its origin at the body's center that does not rotate. The
      z axis points to the north pole, and the x and y axes are fixed relative to the
      stars.

   

   .. function:: std::tuple<double, double, double> position(ReferenceFrame reference_frame)

      The position of the center of the body, in meters.

      :Parameters:

       * **reference_frame** -- The reference frame of the position.

   

   .. function:: std::tuple<double, double, double> velocity(ReferenceFrame reference_frame)

      The velocity of the body, in meters per second.

      :Parameters:

       * **reference_frame** -- The reference frame of the velocity.

   

   .. function:: std::tuple<double, double, double, double> rotation(ReferenceFrame reference_frame)

      The rotation of :func:`Body::reference_frame` relative to the given frame.

      :Parameters:

       * **reference_frame** -- The reference frame of the rotation.

   

   .. function:: std::tuple<double, double, double> angular_velocity(ReferenceFrame reference_frame)

      The angular velocity of the body, in radians per second. The vector points along
      the axis of rotation, and its length is the rate of rotation.

      :Parameters:

       * **reference_frame** -- The reference frame of the angular velocity.

   

   .. function:: std::tuple<double, double, double> surface_position(double latitude, double longitude, ReferenceFrame reference_frame)

      The position of a point on the surface, at the body's mean radius, in meters.

      :Parameters:

       * **latitude** -- Latitude, in degrees.
       * **longitude** -- Longitude, in degrees.
       * **reference_frame** -- The reference frame of the position.
