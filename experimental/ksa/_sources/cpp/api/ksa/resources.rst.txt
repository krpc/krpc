Resources
=========


.. class:: Resource

   A substance held in tanks, such as a propellant, in a part or across a whole vehicle.
   Obtained from :func:`Part::resources` or :func:`Vehicle::resources`.

   .. function:: std::string name()

      The name of the substance.

   

   .. function:: float amount()

      The mass of the substance held, in kilograms.

   

   .. function:: float max()

      The mass of the substance the tanks can hold, in kilograms.

   



.. class:: Tank

   A propellant tank. Obtained from :func:`Part::tanks` or :func:`Vehicle::tanks`.

   .. function:: Part part()

      The part the tank is in.

   

   .. function:: float volume()

      The volume of the tank, in cubic meters.

   

   .. function:: float rated_pressure()

      The pressure the tank stores gas at, in pascals. Zero if the tank cannot store gas.

   

   .. function:: float filled_fraction()

      The fraction of the tank's volume that is filled, between 0 and 1.

   

   .. function:: bool propellant_use_enabled()
   .. function:: void set_propellant_use_enabled(bool value)

      Whether engines can draw propellant from the tank.

   

   .. function:: TankTransferMode transfer_mode()
   .. function:: void set_transfer_mode(TankTransferMode value)

      The direction the tank transfers propellant in.

   

      .. note::

         Propellant flows from tanks transferring out to tanks of the same vehicle transferring
         in, and the transfer draws battery power. Setting a transfer throws an exception if
         the running version of KSA does not support it.



.. namespace:: krpc::services::KSA
.. enum-struct:: TankTransferMode

   The direction a tank transfers propellant in. See :func:`Tank::transfer_mode`.


   .. enumerator:: none

      No transfer.


   .. enumerator:: in

      Into the tank, from tanks transferring out.


   .. enumerator:: out

      Out of the tank, to tanks transferring in.



.. class:: FuelLink

   A fuel line between two parts of a vehicle, which lets propellant flow between them.
   Obtained from :func:`Vehicle::fuel_links`.

   .. function:: Part part_a()

      One of the parts the link joins.

   

   .. function:: Part part_b()

      The other part the link joins.

   

   .. function:: FuelLinkFlow flow()
   .. function:: void set_flow(FuelLinkFlow value)

      The direction propellant flows through the link.

   

   .. function:: bool enabled()
   .. function:: void set_enabled(bool value)

      Whether propellant can flow through the link.

   



.. namespace:: krpc::services::KSA
.. enum-struct:: FuelLinkFlow

   The direction propellant flows through a fuel link. See :func:`FuelLink::flow`.


   .. enumerator:: a_to_b

      From :func:`FuelLink::part_a` to :func:`FuelLink::part_b`.


   .. enumerator:: b_to_a

      From :func:`FuelLink::part_b` to :func:`FuelLink::part_a`.


   .. enumerator:: both

      Either way.
