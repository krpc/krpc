Orbit
=====


.. class:: Orbit

   An orbit. Obtained from :func:`Vehicle::orbit`, :func:`Body::orbit`,
   :func:`Burn::orbit` or :func:`Orbit::next_orbit`.

   .. note::

      A vehicle's orbit follows the vehicle as its trajectory changes. A predicted orbit, from
      :func:`Orbit::next_orbit` or :func:`Burn::orbit`, is the prediction at the time it
      was obtained.

   .. function:: Body body()

      The body being orbited.

   

   .. function:: double apoapsis()

      The apoapsis of the orbit, in meters, from the center of the body being orbited.
      Not a number for a parabolic or hyperbolic orbit.

   

   .. function:: double periapsis()

      The periapsis of the orbit, in meters, from the center of the body being orbited.

   

   .. function:: double apoapsis_altitude()

      The apoapsis of the orbit, in meters, above the mean radius of the body being orbited.

   

   .. function:: double periapsis_altitude()

      The periapsis of the orbit, in meters, above the mean radius of the body being orbited.

   

   .. function:: double semi_major_axis()

      The semi-major axis of the orbit, in meters. Negative for a hyperbolic orbit.

   

   .. function:: double speed()

      The current orbital speed, in meters per second.

   

   .. function:: double radius()

      The current distance from the center of the body being orbited, in meters.

   

   .. function:: double period()

      The orbital period, in seconds. Not a number for a parabolic or hyperbolic orbit.

   

   .. function:: double time_to_apoapsis()

      The time until the next apoapsis, in seconds. Not a number for a parabolic or
      hyperbolic orbit.

   

   .. function:: double time_to_periapsis()

      The time until the next periapsis, in seconds. Negative once a parabolic or
      hyperbolic orbit has passed it.

   

   .. function:: double eccentricity()

      The `eccentricity <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbital_eccentricity>`_
      of the orbit.

   

   .. function:: double inclination()

      The `inclination <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbital_inclination>`_
      of the orbit, in radians, against the equator of the body being orbited.

   

   .. function:: double longitude_of_ascending_node()

      The `longitude of the ascending node <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longitude_of_the_ascending_node>`_, in radians.

   

   .. function:: double argument_of_periapsis()

      The `argument of periapsis <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argument_of_periapsis>`_, in radians.

   

   .. function:: double true_anomaly()

      The current `true anomaly <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_anomaly>`_,
      in radians.

   

   .. function:: double mean_anomaly()

      The current `mean anomaly <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mean_anomaly>`_,
      in radians.

   

   .. function:: double true_anomaly_at_ut(double ut)

      The true anomaly at the given time, in radians.

      :Parameters:

       * **ut** -- Universal time, in seconds.

   

   .. function:: double ut_at_true_anomaly(double true_anomaly)

      The next universal time, from now, at which the orbit reaches the given true
      anomaly, in seconds. Not a number if it never does, such as beyond the asymptote of
      a hyperbolic orbit.

      :Parameters:

       * **true_anomaly** -- True anomaly, in radians.

   

   .. function:: double radius_at(double ut)

      The distance from the center of the body being orbited at the given time, in
      meters.

      :Parameters:

       * **ut** -- Universal time, in seconds.

   

   .. function:: std::tuple<double, double, double> position_at(double ut, ReferenceFrame reference_frame)

      The position on the orbit at the given time, in meters, relative to where the body
      being orbited is now.

      :Parameters:

       * **ut** -- Universal time, in seconds.
       * **reference_frame** -- The reference frame of the position.

   

   .. function:: std::tuple<double, double, double> velocity_at(double ut, ReferenceFrame reference_frame)

      The orbital velocity at the given time, in meters per second, relative to the body
      being orbited and along the axes of the given frame.

      :Parameters:

       * **ut** -- Universal time, in seconds.
       * **reference_frame** -- The reference frame of the velocity.

   

   .. function:: double start_ut()

      The universal time at which this orbit starts, in seconds. Not a number for the
      orbit of a body.

   

   .. function:: double end_ut()

      The universal time at which this orbit ends, in seconds. Infinite for an orbit that
      does not end, such as the orbit of a body.

   

   .. function:: OrbitTransition end_transition()

      How the orbit ends.

   

   .. function:: double time_to_soi_change()

      The time until the orbit enters or leaves a sphere of influence, in seconds. Not a
      number if it does neither.

   

   .. function:: std::optional<Orbit> next_orbit()

      The orbit that follows this one after it enters or leaves a sphere of influence, or
      ``NULL`` if there is none.

   



.. namespace:: krpc::services::KSA
.. enum-struct:: OrbitTransition

   How an orbit ends. See :func:`Orbit::end_transition`.


   .. enumerator:: none

      The orbit does not end.


   .. enumerator:: encounter

      The orbit enters the sphere of influence of another body.


   .. enumerator:: escape

      The orbit leaves the sphere of influence of the body being orbited.


   .. enumerator:: impact

      The orbit hits the surface of the body being orbited.


   .. enumerator:: burn

      The orbit ends at a planned burn.
