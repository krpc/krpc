ManeuverPlanner
===============


.. class:: ManeuverPlanner

   The Maneuver Planner window, which makes maneuver nodes for its operations, and
   executes them.

   .. note::

      MechJeb shares the operations, and most of their settings, between vessels.

Operations

OperationAdvancedTransfer
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^


.. class:: OperationAdvancedTransfer

   An interplanetary transfer to the target body, at the departure that needs the least
   delta-v before :func:`OperationAdvancedTransfer::max_arrival_time`.

   .. note::

      MechJeb searches for the departure in the background. The first call to
      ``MakeNodes`` starts the search, and throws an :class:`OperationException`
      until it finishes. See :func:`OperationAdvancedTransfer::searching`.

   .. function:: double max_arrival_time()
   .. function:: void set_max_arrival_time(double value)

      The latest arrival time, as a time from now, in seconds.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: bool include_capture_burn()
   .. function:: void set_include_capture_burn(bool value)

      Whether to plan a capture burn at the destination.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: double periapsis_height()
   .. function:: void set_periapsis_height(double value)

      The altitude of the periapsis at the destination, in meters.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: bool searching()

      Whether MechJeb is searching for the departure.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: int32_t search_progress()

      The progress of the search for the departure, as a percentage.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: std::string error_message()

      The warning or error from the last call to ``MakeNodes``, or an empty string.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: std::vector<SpaceCenter::Node> make_nodes()

      Make the maneuver nodes of the operation. Throws an :class:`OperationException`
      if the operation cannot make them.


      :returns: The maneuver nodes.
      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         The nodes follow the vessel's last maneuver node, if it has one. A warning may be
         left in ``ErrorMessage``.

OperationResonantOrbit
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^


.. class:: OperationResonantOrbit

   Change to a resonant orbit, to place satellites in a constellation.

   .. note::

      Start from an orbit in the plane of the constellation. To deploy satellites, set the
      denominator to their number. A numerator one less than the denominator is the cheapest,
      though not the fastest. The two numbers must have no common factor.

   .. function:: std::string error_message()

      The warning or error from the last call to ``MakeNodes``, or an empty string.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: std::vector<SpaceCenter::Node> make_nodes()

      Make the maneuver nodes of the operation. Throws an :class:`OperationException`
      if the operation cannot make them.


      :returns: The maneuver nodes.
      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         The nodes follow the vessel's last maneuver node, if it has one. A warning may be
         left in ``ErrorMessage``.

   .. function:: int32_t resonance_denominator()
   .. function:: void set_resonance_denominator(int32_t value)

      The denominator of the ratio of the new orbital period to the current one.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: int32_t resonance_numerator()
   .. function:: void set_resonance_numerator(int32_t value)

      The numerator of the ratio of the new orbital period to the current one.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: TimeSelector time_selector()

      The time of the maneuver.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

OperationTransfer
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^


.. class:: OperationTransfer

   A bi-impulsive (Hohmann) transfer to the target, within one sphere of influence. It
   suits a rendezvous with a vessel or a transfer to a moon.

   .. note::

      Despite the name, the transfer is often uni-impulsive. It happens at the selected time,
      or at the optimum time.

   .. function:: std::string error_message()

      The warning or error from the last call to ``MakeNodes``, or an empty string.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: std::vector<SpaceCenter::Node> make_nodes()

      Make the maneuver nodes of the operation. Throws an :class:`OperationException`
      if the operation cannot make them.


      :returns: The maneuver nodes.
      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         The nodes follow the vessel's last maneuver node, if it has one. A warning may be
         left in ``ErrorMessage``.

   .. function:: bool capture()
   .. function:: void set_capture(bool value)

      Whether the transfer ends in the target's orbit. Without capture, it is an impact or
      a flyby.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: bool plan_capture()
   .. function:: void set_plan_capture(bool value)

      Whether to make a second node for the capture burn.

      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         Requires :func:`OperationTransfer::capture`.

   .. function:: bool rendezvous()
   .. function:: void set_rendezvous(bool value)

      Whether to meet the target, rather than reach its orbit.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: double lag_time()
   .. function:: void set_lag_time(double value)

      The time by which the vessel arrives behind the target, in seconds.

      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         Requires :func:`OperationTransfer::rendezvous`.

   .. function:: bool coplanar()
   .. function:: void set_coplanar(bool value)

      Whether the transfer stays in the vessel's orbital plane.

      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. function:: TimeSelector time_selector()

      The time of the maneuver.

      :Game Scenes: Flight
