ManeuverPlanner
===============


.. _http-MechJeb.ManeuverPlanner:

.. rubric:: ManeuverPlanner

The Maneuver Planner window, which makes maneuver nodes for its operations, and
executes them.

.. note::

   MechJeb shares the operations, and most of their settings, between vessels.


A ``ManeuverPlanner`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.

   The operation that transfers to another planet, at the departure that needs the
   least delta-v.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`ManeuverPlanner <http-MechJeb.ManeuverPlanner>` object.

   :resjson MechJeb.OperationAdvancedTransfer value:

.. _http-MechJeb.OperationException:

.. rubric:: OperationException

Thrown when an operation cannot make its nodes, such as when it needs a target and
there is none.

Returned as an error whose ``service`` and ``name`` are ``"MechJeb"`` and ``"OperationException"``.

.. _http-MechJeb.TimeReference:

.. rubric:: TimeReference

The time reference of a maneuver. See :http:get:`/mech-jeb/time-selector/(id)/time-reference`.

Passed and returned as the name of one of its values:


``"computed"``
   At the optimum time.


``"x_from_now"``
   After :http:get:`/mech-jeb/time-selector/(id)/lead-time`.


``"apoapsis"``
   At the next apoapsis.


``"periapsis"``
   At the next periapsis.


``"altitude"``
   At :http:get:`/mech-jeb/time-selector/(id)/circularize-altitude`.


``"eq_ascending"``
   At the equatorial ascending node.


``"eq_descending"``
   At the equatorial descending node.


``"rel_ascending"``
   At the next ascending node with the target.


``"rel_descending"``
   At the next descending node with the target.


``"closest_approach"``
   At the closest approach to the target.


``"eq_highest_ad"``
   At the cheaper of the equatorial ascending and descending nodes.


``"eq_nearest_ad"``
   At the nearer of the equatorial ascending and descending nodes.


``"rel_highest_ad"``
   At the cheaper of the ascending and descending nodes with the target.


``"rel_nearest_ad"``
   At the nearer of the ascending and descending nodes with the target.



Operations
----------

OperationAdvancedTransfer
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^


.. _http-MechJeb.OperationAdvancedTransfer:

.. rubric:: OperationAdvancedTransfer

An interplanetary transfer to the target body, at the departure that needs the least
delta-v before :http:get:`/mech-jeb/operation-advanced-transfer/(id)/max-arrival-time`.

.. note::

   MechJeb searches for the departure in the background. The first call to
   ``MakeNodes`` starts the search, and throws an :ref:`OperationException <http-MechJeb.OperationException>`
   until it finishes. See :http:get:`/mech-jeb/operation-advanced-transfer/(id)/searching`.


A ``OperationAdvancedTransfer`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.


.. http:get:: /mech-jeb/operation-advanced-transfer/(id)/max-arrival-time

   The latest arrival time, as a time from now, in seconds.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`OperationAdvancedTransfer <http-MechJeb.OperationAdvancedTransfer>` object.

   :resjson double value:

.. http:put:: /mech-jeb/operation-advanced-transfer/(id)/max-arrival-time

   The latest arrival time, as a time from now, in seconds.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`OperationAdvancedTransfer <http-MechJeb.OperationAdvancedTransfer>` object.

   :reqjson double value: The new value.




.. http:get:: /mech-jeb/operation-advanced-transfer/(id)/include-capture-burn

   Whether to plan a capture burn at the destination.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`OperationAdvancedTransfer <http-MechJeb.OperationAdvancedTransfer>` object.

   :resjson bool value:

.. http:put:: /mech-jeb/operation-advanced-transfer/(id)/include-capture-burn

   Whether to plan a capture burn at the destination.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`OperationAdvancedTransfer <http-MechJeb.OperationAdvancedTransfer>` object.

   :reqjson bool value: The new value.




.. http:get:: /mech-jeb/operation-advanced-transfer/(id)/periapsis-height

   The altitude of the periapsis at the destination, in meters.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`OperationAdvancedTransfer <http-MechJeb.OperationAdvancedTransfer>` object.

   :resjson double value:

.. http:put:: /mech-jeb/operation-advanced-transfer/(id)/periapsis-height

   The altitude of the periapsis at the destination, in meters.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`OperationAdvancedTransfer <http-MechJeb.OperationAdvancedTransfer>` object.

   :reqjson double value: The new value.




.. http:get:: /mech-jeb/operation-advanced-transfer/(id)/searching

   Whether MechJeb is searching for the departure.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`OperationAdvancedTransfer <http-MechJeb.OperationAdvancedTransfer>` object.

   :resjson bool value:




.. http:get:: /mech-jeb/operation-advanced-transfer/(id)/search-progress

   The progress of the search for the departure, as a percentage.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`OperationAdvancedTransfer <http-MechJeb.OperationAdvancedTransfer>` object.

   :resjson int32 value:




.. http:get:: /mech-jeb/operation-advanced-transfer/(id)/error-message

   The warning or error from the last call to ``MakeNodes``, or an empty string.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`OperationAdvancedTransfer <http-MechJeb.OperationAdvancedTransfer>` object.

   :resjson string value:




.. http:post:: /mech-jeb/operation-advanced-transfer/(id)/make-nodes

   Make the maneuver nodes of the operation. Throws an :ref:`OperationException <http-MechJeb.OperationException>`
   if the operation cannot make them.

   .. note::

      The nodes follow the vessel's last maneuver node, if it has one. A warning may be
      left in ``ErrorMessage``.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`OperationAdvancedTransfer <http-MechJeb.OperationAdvancedTransfer>` object.

   :resjson list<SpaceCenter.Node> value: The maneuver nodes.

OperationCircularize
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^


.. _http-MechJeb.OperationCircularize:

.. rubric:: OperationCircularize

Circularize the orbit.

.. note::

   To raise the periapsis to the apoapsis, set the time to ``"apoapsis"``, and to lower the apoapsis to the periapsis, set it to ``"periapsis"``. These are the cheapest.


A ``OperationCircularize`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.

      left in ``ErrorMessage``.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`OperationCircularize <http-MechJeb.OperationCircularize>` object.
