Orbit
=====


.. _http-SpaceCenter.Orbit:

.. rubric:: Orbit

Describes an orbit. For example, the orbit of a vessel, obtained by calling
:http:get:`/space-center/vessel/(id)/orbit`, or a celestial body, obtained by calling
:http:get:`/space-center/celestial-body/(id)/orbit`.

A ``Orbit`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.


.. http:post:: /space-center/orbit/create-from-position-and-velocity

   Create the orbit that passes through a given position at a given velocity.
   The orbit coasts freely under the gravity of the body being orbited, so it
   describes where an object left to fall from that position and velocity would
   be at any later time.

   .. note::

      The orbit is a single conic around *body*. It never changes
      sphere of influence, so :http:get:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/next-orbit` is ``null``, and no
      atmosphere slows it. A vessel held on rails follows its own orbit exactly,
      while one inside the physics bubble is simulated and drifts from it a little.

      :http:get:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/radius`, :http:get:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/speed`, :http:post:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/position`,
      :http:post:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/velocity` and the like describe the orbit at
      *ut* and stay there. Use :http:post:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/radius-at`,
      :http:post:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/speed-at`, :http:post:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/position-at`, :http:post:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/velocity-at` and
      :http:post:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/true-anomaly-at-ut` for another time, and
      :http:get:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/reference-frame` and :http:get:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/orbital-reference-frame` to follow
      the orbit as time passes.

      The orbit is held until :http:post:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/remove` is called on it or the client
      that created it disconnects. Remove each one created by a script that creates
      them repeatedly, for example once per update.


   :reqjson SpaceCenter.CelestialBody body: The celestial body being orbited.
   :reqjson tuple<double, double, double> position: The position, as a position vector.
   :reqjson tuple<double, double, double> velocity: The velocity, as a vector pointing in the direction of travel, whose magnitude is the speed in :math:`\mathrm{m/s}`.
   :reqjson double ut: The universal time, in seconds, at which the object is at *position* traveling at *velocity*.
   :reqjson SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame? reference_frame: The reference frame that *position* and *velocity* are in. Defaults to :http:get:`/space-center/celestial-body/(id)/non-rotating-reference-frame` of *body*. Optional, defaults to ``null``.
   :resjson SpaceCenter.Orbit value:





.. http:post:: /space-center/orbit/create-from-orbital-elements

   Create the orbit with the given
   `orbital elements <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbital_elements>`_
   around a given body. The orbit coasts freely under the gravity of that body,
   so it describes where an object on it would be at any time.

   .. note::

      The orbit is a single conic around *body*. It never changes
      sphere of influence, so :http:get:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/next-orbit` is ``null``, and no
      atmosphere slows it.

      The angles are measured against the reference plane and direction that
      :http:get:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/inclination`, :http:get:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/longitude-of-ascending-node` and
      :http:get:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/argument-of-periapsis` report them against.
      :http:post:`/space-center/orbit/reference-plane-normal` and :http:post:`/space-center/orbit/reference-plane-direction`
      give these as vectors in a reference frame.

      :http:get:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/radius`, :http:get:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/speed`, :http:post:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/position`,
      :http:post:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/velocity` and the like describe the orbit at
      *epoch* and stay there. Use :http:post:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/radius-at`,
      :http:post:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/speed-at`, :http:post:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/position-at`, :http:post:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/velocity-at` and
      :http:post:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/true-anomaly-at-ut` for another time, and
      :http:get:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/reference-frame` and :http:get:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/orbital-reference-frame` to follow
      the orbit as time passes.

      The orbit is held until :http:post:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/remove` is called on it or the client
      that created it disconnects. Remove each one created by a script that creates
      them repeatedly, for example once per update.


   :reqjson SpaceCenter.CelestialBody body: The celestial body being orbited.
   :reqjson double semi_major_axis: The semi-major axis of the orbit, in meters. Positive for an ellipse and negative for a hyperbola.
   :reqjson double eccentricity: The eccentricity of the orbit. Below one for an ellipse and above one for a hyperbola.
   :reqjson double inclination: The inclination of the orbit, in radians.
   :reqjson double longitude_of_ascending_node: The longitude of the ascending node, in radians.
   :reqjson double argument_of_periapsis: The argument of periapsis, in radians.
   :reqjson double mean_anomaly_at_epoch: The mean anomaly at *epoch*, in radians.
   :reqjson double epoch: The universal time, in seconds, that *mean_anomaly_at_epoch* is measured at.
   :resjson SpaceCenter.Orbit value:





.. http:post:: /space-center/orbit/(id)/remove

   Remove the orbit, releasing the memory the server holds for it.

   .. note::

      Only an orbit created by :http:post:`/space-center/orbit/create-from-position-and-velocity` or
      :http:post:`/space-center/orbit/create-from-orbital-elements` can be removed. Every other orbit
      belongs to something in the game, and the server holds one of each.

      Any further use of this object throws an exception, as does use of a
      reference frame defined against it. An orbit belongs to the client that
      created it, and is removed when that client disconnects.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Orbit <http-SpaceCenter.Orbit>` object.

.. http:get:: /space-center/orbit/(id)/reference-frame

   The reference frame that moves along the orbit, and is orientated in a fixed
   direction.

   * The origin is at the point the orbit has reached at the
     current time.
   * The axes do not rotate. They point in the same fixed
     directions as those of
     :http:get:`/space-center/celestial-body/(id)/non-rotating-reference-frame` of
     :http:get:`/space-center/orbit/(id)/body`.

   .. note::

      For the orbit of a vessel, the origin is the point the vessel's orbit has
      reached rather than the vessel's own center of mass. The two are a few meters
      apart, as a vessel inside the physics bubble is simulated rather than being
      held on its orbit.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Orbit <http-SpaceCenter.Orbit>` object.

   :resjson SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame value:




.. http:get:: /space-center/orbit/(id)/orbital-reference-frame

   The reference frame that moves along the orbit, and is orientated with its
   prograde/normal/radial directions.

   * The origin is at the point the orbit has reached at the
     current time.
   * The axes rotate with the orbital prograde/normal/radial
     directions.
   * The x-axis points in the orbital anti-radial direction.
   * The y-axis points in the orbital prograde direction.
   * The z-axis points in the orbital normal direction.

   .. note::

      For the orbit of a vessel, the origin is the point the vessel's orbit has
      reached rather than the vessel's own center of mass, which is what
      :http:get:`/space-center/vessel/(id)/orbital-reference-frame` is centered on.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Orbit <http-SpaceCenter.Orbit>` object.

   :resjson SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame value:

.. http:get:: /space-center/orbit/(id)/specific-energy

   The specific orbital energy, in :math:`\mathrm{J/kg}`.

   .. note::

      This is the sum of the orbit's specific kinetic and potential energy,
      :math:`v^{2}/2 - \mu/r`, where :math:`v` is the orbital speed,
      :math:`\mu` the gravitational parameter of the body and :math:`r` the
      orbital radius. It is negative for a bound (elliptical) orbit.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Orbit <http-SpaceCenter.Orbit>` object.

   :resjson double value:
