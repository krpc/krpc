CelestialBody
=============


.. _http-SpaceCenter.CelestialBody:

.. rubric:: CelestialBody

Represents a celestial body (such as a planet or moon).
See :http:get:`/space-center/bodies`.

A ``CelestialBody`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.

.. http:post:: /space-center/celestial-body/(id)/surface-reference-frame

   Create a reference frame fixed to the surface at the given latitude and longitude.

   * The origin is the given altitude above the surface, at the
     given latitude and longitude.
   * The axes rotate with the body.
   * The x-axis points out from the center of the body.
   * The y-axis points north.
   * The z-axis points east.

   .. note::

      Each call returns a new frame, as for :http:post:`/space-center/reference-frame/create-relative`.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`CelestialBody <http-SpaceCenter.CelestialBody>` object.

   :reqjson double latitude: Latitude in degrees.
   :reqjson double longitude: Longitude in degrees.
   :reqjson double altitude: Altitude of the origin above the surface, in meters. Optional, defaults to ``0.0``.
   :resjson SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame value:

.. http:post:: /space-center/celestial-body/(id)/density-at

   Gets the air density, in :math:`\mathrm{kg/m^{3}}`, for the specified
   altitude above sea level, in meters.

   .. note::

      This is an approximation, because actual calculations, taking sun exposure into account
      to compute air temperature, require us to know the exact point on the body where the
      density is to be computed (knowing the altitude is not enough).
      However, the difference is small for high altitudes, so it makes very little difference
      for trajectory prediction.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`CelestialBody <http-SpaceCenter.CelestialBody>` object.

   :reqjson double altitude:
   :resjson double value:

.. http:get:: /space-center/celestial-body/(id)/reference-frame

   The reference frame that is fixed relative to the celestial body.

   * The origin is at the center of the body.
   * The axes rotate with the body.
   * The x-axis points from the center of the body
     towards the intersection of the prime meridian and equator (the
     position at 0° longitude, 0° latitude).
   * The y-axis points from the center of the body
     towards the north pole.
   * The z-axis points from the center of the body
     towards the equator at 90°E longitude.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`CelestialBody <http-SpaceCenter.CelestialBody>` object.

   :resjson SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame value:

.. figure:: /images/reference-frames/celestial-body.png
   :align: center

   Celestial body reference frame origin and axes. The equator is shown in
   blue, and the prime meridian in red.




.. http:get:: /space-center/celestial-body/(id)/non-rotating-reference-frame

   The reference frame that is fixed relative to this celestial body, and
   orientated in a fixed direction (it does not rotate with the body).

   * The origin is at the center of the body.
   * The axes do not rotate.
   * The x-axis points in an arbitrary direction through the
     equator.
   * The y-axis points from the center of the body towards
     the north pole.
   * The z-axis points in an arbitrary direction through the
     equator.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`CelestialBody <http-SpaceCenter.CelestialBody>` object.

   :resjson SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame value:




.. http:get:: /space-center/celestial-body/(id)/orbital-reference-frame

   The reference frame that is fixed relative to this celestial body, but
   orientated with the body's orbital prograde/normal/radial directions.

   * The origin is at the center of the body.
   * The axes rotate with the orbital prograde/normal/radial
     directions.
   * The x-axis points in the orbital anti-radial direction.
   * The y-axis points in the orbital prograde direction.
   * The z-axis points in the orbital normal direction.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`CelestialBody <http-SpaceCenter.CelestialBody>` object.

   :resjson SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame value:
