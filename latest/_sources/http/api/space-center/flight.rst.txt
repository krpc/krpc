Flight
======


.. _http-SpaceCenter.Flight:

.. rubric:: Flight

Used to get flight telemetry for a vessel, by calling :http:post:`/space-center/vessel/(id)/flight`.
All of the information returned by this class is given in the reference frame
passed to that method.
Obtained by calling :http:post:`/space-center/vessel/(id)/flight`.

.. note::

   To get orbital information, such as the apoapsis or inclination, see :ref:`Orbit <http-SpaceCenter.Orbit>`.


A ``Flight`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.

   mass, in reference frame :ref:`ReferenceFrame <http-SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame>`. The magnitude is in
   :math:`\mathrm{N \cdot m}`.

   .. note::

      This is the live counterpart to :http:get:`/space-center/flight/(id)/aerodynamic-force`: it is the torque
      the aerodynamics model applied on the current physics frame, taken about the
      center of mass, rather than one re-simulated for hypothetical conditions the way
      :http:post:`/space-center/flight/(id)/simulate-aerodynamic-torque-at` does. It is intended for validating the

   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Flight <http-SpaceCenter.Flight>` object.

   :resjson tuple<double, double, double> value: A vector pointing along the axis of the torque, with its magnitude equal to the strength of the torque in :math:`\mathrm{N \cdot m}`.




.. http:post:: /space-center/flight/(id)/simulate-aerodynamic-force-at

   Simulate and return the total aerodynamic forces acting on the vessel,
   if it were traveling with the given velocity, at the given position and
   orientation, in the atmosphere of the given celestial body.

   .. note::

      The position, velocity and rotation arguments, and the returned force, are all
      expressed in reference frame :ref:`ReferenceFrame <http-SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame>`. The result is the
      force the vessel would experience if it were placed at that position and
      orientation with the air flowing past it at that velocity; it is the force at
      the requested orientation, not the force in the vessel's current orientation.
      Atmospheric temperature and density are evaluated at the current universal time.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Flight <http-SpaceCenter.Flight>` object.

   :reqjson SpaceCenter.CelestialBody body: The celestial body whose atmosphere the forces are simulated in.
   :reqjson tuple<double, double, double> position: The position of the vessel, in reference frame :ref:`ReferenceFrame <http-SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame>`.
   :reqjson tuple<double, double, double> velocity: The velocity of the vessel, in reference frame :ref:`ReferenceFrame <http-SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame>`.
   :reqjson tuple<double, double, double, double> rotation: The orientation of the vessel, in reference frame :ref:`ReferenceFrame <http-SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame>`, in the same form as :http:post:`/space-center/vessel/(id)/rotation`. The angle of attack and sideslip follow from this orientation relative to the velocity; the roll component sets the direction of any aerodynamic lift. Pass the vessel's current rotation to evaluate the force at its current orientation.
   :resjson tuple<double, double, double> value: A vector pointing in the direction that the force acts, with its magnitude equal to the strength of the force in Newtons, in reference frame :ref:`ReferenceFrame <http-SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame>`.





.. http:post:: /space-center/flight/(id)/simulate-aerodynamic-torque-at

   Simulate and return the total aerodynamic torque acting on the vessel about its
   center of mass, if it were traveling with the given velocity, at the given
   position, orientation and angular velocity, in the atmosphere of the given
   celestial body.

   .. note::

      The position, velocity, rotation and angular velocity arguments, and the returned
      torque, are all expressed in reference frame :ref:`ReferenceFrame <http-SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame>`. When
      Ferram Aerospace Research is installed the angular velocity argument is ignored.
      Atmospheric temperature and density are evaluated at the current universal time.

      This is the ideal rigid-body aerodynamic torque, summed from the per-part forces
      about the center of mass. A vessel may not visibly rotate by the full amount when
      a large aerodynamic force acts on a small part far from the center of mass, because
      the game applies each part's force to that part and propagates it through the joints
      rather than to the vessel as a rigid body.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Flight <http-SpaceCenter.Flight>` object.

   :reqjson SpaceCenter.CelestialBody body: The celestial body whose atmosphere the torque is simulated in.
   :reqjson tuple<double, double, double> position: The position of the vessel, in reference frame :ref:`ReferenceFrame <http-SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame>`.
   :reqjson tuple<double, double, double> velocity: The velocity of the vessel, in reference frame :ref:`ReferenceFrame <http-SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame>`.
   :reqjson tuple<double, double, double, double> rotation: The orientation of the vessel, in reference frame :ref:`ReferenceFrame <http-SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame>`, in the same form as :http:post:`/space-center/vessel/(id)/rotation`. Pass the vessel's current rotation to evaluate the torque at its current orientation.
   :reqjson tuple<double, double, double> angular_velocity: The angular velocity of the vessel, in reference frame :ref:`ReferenceFrame <http-SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame>`. This adds the solid-body rotation term to each part's local airflow and the per-part rigid-body angular drag the game applies, together giving the aerodynamic damping torque. Pass a zero vector to evaluate the static torque.
   :resjson tuple<double, double, double> value: A vector pointing along the axis of the torque, with its magnitude equal to the strength of the torque in :math:`\mathrm{N \cdot m}`, in reference frame :ref:`ReferenceFrame <http-SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame>`.





.. http:post:: /space-center/flight/(id)/simulate-aerodynamic-wrench-at

   Simulate and return the total aerodynamic force and torque acting on the vessel,
   if its center of mass were traveling with the given velocity, at the given position,
   orientation and angular velocity, in the atmosphere of the given celestial body.

   .. note::

      The position and velocity describe the hypothetical center-of-mass state. The
      position, velocity, rotation and angular velocity arguments, and both returned
      vectors, are expressed in reference frame :ref:`ReferenceFrame <http-SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame>`.
      For future-state prediction, :http:get:`/space-center/celestial-body/(id)/non-rotating-reference-frame`
      is recommended so that the spatial state has unambiguous inertial semantics.

      This is an instantaneous rigid-body result based on the vessel's current parts,
      drag cubes and control-surface state. When Ferram Aerospace Research is installed
      the angular velocity and *ut* arguments are ignored.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Flight <http-SpaceCenter.Flight>` object.

   :reqjson SpaceCenter.CelestialBody body: The celestial body whose atmosphere the wrench is simulated in.
   :reqjson tuple<double, double, double> position: The position of the vessel's center of mass, in reference frame :ref:`ReferenceFrame <http-SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame>`.
   :reqjson tuple<double, double, double> velocity: The velocity of the vessel's center of mass, in reference frame :ref:`ReferenceFrame <http-SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame>`.
   :reqjson tuple<double, double, double, double> rotation: The orientation of the vessel, in reference frame :ref:`ReferenceFrame <http-SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame>`, in the same form as :http:post:`/space-center/vessel/(id)/rotation`. Pass the vessel's current rotation to evaluate the wrench at its current orientation.
   :reqjson tuple<double, double, double> angular_velocity: The angular velocity of the vessel, in reference frame :ref:`ReferenceFrame <http-SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame>`. This adds the solid-body rotation term to each part's local airflow and the per-part rigid-body angular drag the game applies, together giving the aerodynamic damping force and torque. Pass a zero vector to evaluate the static wrench relative to the reference frame.
   :reqjson double ut: The universal time used for the atmospheric ephemeris. It selects the body/Sun geometry used for temperature and density, but does not change or propagate :ref:`ReferenceFrame <http-SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame>` or any of the state arguments.
   :resjson SpaceCenter.Wrench value: The aerodynamic force, and the aerodynamic torque about the vessel's center of mass. Both are vectors in reference frame :ref:`ReferenceFrame <http-SpaceCenter.ReferenceFrame>`.





.. http:get:: /space-center/flight/(id)/lift

   The `aerodynamic lift <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerodynamic_force>`_
   currently acting on the vessel.

   .. note::

      The part of :http:get:`/space-center/flight/(id)/aerodynamic-force` that acts across the air stream and
      towards the top of the vessel: perpendicular to the air stream, in the plane the
      vessel's nose and its top sit in. A vessel flying with sideslip is also pushed out
      of that plane, and that part of the force is :http:get:`/space-center/flight/(id)/side-force`. Zero for a
      vessel flying exactly sideways, which has no such plane to speak of.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Flight <http-SpaceCenter.Flight>` object.

   :resjson tuple<double, double, double> value: A vector pointing in the direction that the force acts, with its magnitude equal to the strength of the force in Newtons.




.. http:get:: /space-center/flight/(id)/side-force

   The aerodynamic side force currently acting on the vessel.

   .. note::

      The part of :http:get:`/space-center/flight/(id)/aerodynamic-force` that acts across the air stream and out
      of the vessel's side, which is what the air does to a vessel flying with sideslip.
      It is perpendicular to both :http:get:`/space-center/flight/(id)/lift` and :http:get:`/space-center/flight/(id)/drag`, and the
      three of them sum to :http:get:`/space-center/flight/(id)/aerodynamic-force`.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Flight <http-SpaceCenter.Flight>` object.
