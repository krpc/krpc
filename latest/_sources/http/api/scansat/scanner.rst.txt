Scanner
=======


.. _http-ScanSat.Scanner:

.. rubric:: Scanner

A SCANsat scanner on a part. Obtained by calling :http:post:`/scan-sat/scanner` or
:http:post:`/scan-sat/scanners`.

A ``Scanner`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.


.. http:get:: /scan-sat/scanner/(id)/part

   The part containing this scanner.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Scanner <http-ScanSat.Scanner>` object.

   :resjson SpaceCenter.Part value:




.. http:get:: /scan-sat/scanner/(id)/scan-types

   The scan types of the scanner.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Scanner <http-ScanSat.Scanner>` object.

   :resjson ScanSat.ScanType value:




.. http:get:: /scan-sat/scanner/(id)/active

   Returns whether the scanner is switched on, and switches it on or off, as the buttons
   of the part do.

   .. note::

      A resource scanner only switches on when :http:get:`/scan-sat/instant-resource-scan`
      is off, or :http:get:`/scan-sat/stock-resource-scan` is off.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Scanner <http-ScanSat.Scanner>` object.

   :resjson bool value:

.. http:put:: /scan-sat/scanner/(id)/active

   .. note::

   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`Scanner <http-ScanSat.Scanner>` object.

   :reqjson bool value: The new value.

.. _http-ScanSat.ScannerState:

.. rubric:: ScannerState

The state of a scanner. See :http:get:`/scan-sat/scanner/(id)/state`.

Passed and returned as the name of one of its values:


``"off"``
   Switched off.


``"out_of_range"``
   Switched on, and outside its minimum and maximum altitudes.


``"in_darkness"``
   Switched on, and paused as it needs light and the ground below it is in darkness.


``"in_range"``
   Scanning between its minimum and best altitudes, with a reduced field of view.


``"in_best_range"``
   Scanning between its best and maximum altitudes, with its full field of view.
