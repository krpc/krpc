.. type:: krpc_MechJeb_AscentType_t

   The ascent path. See :func:`krpc_MechJeb_AscentGuidance_AscentType`.

   .. macro:: KRPC_ENUMERATION_MECHJEB_ASCENTTYPE

      The service and enumeration ids of the enumeration, for
      :func:`krpc_KRPC_Type_EnumerationTypeById` and :func:`krpc_KRPC_Expression_ConstantEnumById`.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_ASCENTTYPE_CLASSIC

      The classic ascent path. See :func:`krpc_MechJeb_AscentGuidance_ClassicPath`.

      Primer Vector Guidance, for RSS/RO. See :func:`krpc_MechJeb_AscentGuidance_PVGSettings`.



.. type:: krpc_MechJeb_AscentLaunchMode_t

   The launch mode of the ascent autopilot. See :func:`krpc_MechJeb_AscentGuidance_LaunchMode`.

   .. macro:: KRPC_ENUMERATION_MECHJEB_ASCENTLAUNCHMODE

      The service and enumeration ids of the enumeration, for
      :func:`krpc_KRPC_Type_EnumerationTypeById` and :func:`krpc_KRPC_Expression_ConstantEnumById`.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_ASCENTLAUNCHMODE_NORMAL

      The autopilot is not performing a timed launch.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_ASCENTLAUNCHMODE_RENDEZVOUS

      A timed launch to rendezvous with the target vessel.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_ASCENTLAUNCHMODE_TARGETPLANE

      A timed launch into the plane of the target.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_ASCENTLAUNCHMODE_MATCHLAN

      A timed launch to the target's longitude of the ascending node. See
      :func:`krpc_MechJeb_AscentGuidance_LaunchToMatchLan`.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_ASCENTLAUNCHMODE_LAN

      A timed launch to a longitude of the ascending node. See
      :func:`krpc_MechJeb_AscentGuidance_LaunchToLan`.


   .. macro:: KRPC_MECHJEB_ASCENTLAUNCHMODE_UNKNOWN

      A timed launch of another kind, started from MechJeb's window.
