RoverWaypoint
-------------


.. type:: krpc_MechJeb_RoverWaypoint_t

   A waypoint of the :type:`krpc_MechJeb_RoverAutopilot_t`, as the Waypoints window shows it.

   .. note::

      The object is destroyed once the waypoints are cleared or replaced, as by
      :func:`krpc_MechJeb_RoverAutopilot_ClearWaypoints` and :func:`krpc_MechJeb_RoverAutopilot_DriveToTarget`.

   .. macro:: KRPC_CLASS_MECHJEB_ROVERWAYPOINT

      The service and class ids of the class, for :func:`krpc_KRPC_Type_ClassTypeById`.
