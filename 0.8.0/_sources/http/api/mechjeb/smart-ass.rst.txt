SmartASS
========


.. _http-MechJeb.SmartASS:

.. rubric:: SmartASS

The Smart A.S.S. window, which points the vessel in a chosen direction.

A ``SmartASS`` object is passed and returned as its identifier, a number. A path that acts on
an object takes it as ``(id)``.

   Throws an exception if set to ``"automatic"``.


   :param id: The identifier of the :ref:`SmartASS <http-MechJeb.SmartASS>` object.

   :resjson MechJeb.SmartASSInterfaceMode value:

.. _http-MechJeb.SmartASSInterfaceMode:

.. rubric:: SmartASSInterfaceMode

The mode of the Smart A.S.S. window. See :http:get:`/mech-jeb/smart-ass/(id)/interface-mode`.

Passed and returned as the name of one of its values:


``"orbital"``
   The orbital modes.


``"surface"``
   The surface modes.


``"target"``
   The target modes.


``"advanced"``
   The advanced mode.


``"automatic"``
   Set by MechJeb's autopilots. Cannot be set.



.. _http-MechJeb.SmartASSAutopilotMode:

.. rubric:: SmartASSAutopilotMode

The autopilot mode of Smart A.S.S. See :http:get:`/mech-jeb/smart-ass/(id)/autopilot-mode`.

Passed and returned as the name of one of its values:


``"off"``
   Off.


``"kill_rot"``
   Stop the vessel's rotation.


``"node"``
   Point at the next maneuver node.


``"surface"``
   Point in the direction set by :http:get:`/mech-jeb/smart-ass/(id)/surface-heading`,
   :http:get:`/mech-jeb/smart-ass/(id)/surface-pitch` and :http:get:`/mech-jeb/smart-ass/(id)/surface-roll`.


``"prograde"``
   Point orbital prograde.


``"retrograde"``
   Point orbital retrograde.


``"normal_plus"``
   Point orbit normal.


``"normal_minus"``
   Point orbit anti-normal.


``"radial_plus"``
   Point radially outward.


``"radial_minus"``
   Point radially inward.


``"relative_plus"``
   Point along the velocity relative to the target.


``"relative_minus"``
   Point against the velocity relative to the target.


``"target_plus"``
   Point at the target.


``"target_minus"``
   Point away from the target.


``"parallel_plus"``
   Point parallel to the target's orientation. For a docking port, along its axis
   and away from it.


``"parallel_minus"``
   Point antiparallel to the target's orientation. For a docking port, along its axis
   and toward it.


``"advanced"``
   Point in the direction set by :http:get:`/mech-jeb/smart-ass/(id)/advanced-reference` and
   :http:get:`/mech-jeb/smart-ass/(id)/advanced-direction`.


``"automatic"``
   Set by MechJeb's autopilots. Cannot be set.


``"surface_prograde"``
   Point along the surface velocity.


``"surface_retrograde"``
   Point against the surface velocity.


``"horizontal_plus"``
   Point along the horizontal surface velocity.


``"horizontal_minus"``
   Point against the horizontal surface velocity.


``"vertical_plus"``
   Point up, normal to the surface.



.. _http-MechJeb.AttitudeReference:

.. rubric:: AttitudeReference

A reference frame of Smart A.S.S. See :http:get:`/mech-jeb/smart-ass/(id)/advanced-reference`.

Passed and returned as the name of one of its values:


``"inertial"``
   The world coordinate system.


``"orbit"``
   Forward is prograde, left is normal and up is radial out.


``"orbit_horizontal"``
   Forward is the orbital velocity projected onto the surface, and up is the surface normal.


``"surface_north"``
   Forward is north, left is west and up is the surface normal.


``"surface_velocity"``
   Forward is the surface velocity, and up is the surface normal perpendicular to it.


``"target"``
   Forward is toward the target, and up is the vessel's heading perpendicular to it.


``"relative_velocity"``
   Forward is the velocity relative to the target.


``"target_orientation"``
   Forward is the direction the target faces, and up is the target's up.


``"maneuver_node"``
   Forward is the burn direction of the next maneuver node.


``"sun"``
   Forward is the orbital velocity of the body that orbits the sun, and up is radial
   out of that orbit.


``"surface_horizontal"``
   Forward is the horizontal surface velocity, and up is the surface normal.
