ManeuverPlanner
===============


.. class:: ManeuverPlanner

   The Maneuver Planner window, which makes maneuver nodes for its operations, and
   executes them.

   .. note::

      MechJeb shares the operations, and most of their settings, between vessels.

      The operation that transfers to another planet, at the departure that needs the

      At the next descending node with the target.


   .. attribute:: closestApproach

      At the closest approach to the target.


   .. attribute:: eqHighestAd

      At the cheaper of the equatorial ascending and descending nodes.


   .. attribute:: eqNearestAd

      At the nearer of the equatorial ascending and descending nodes.


   .. attribute:: relHighestAd

      At the cheaper of the ascending and descending nodes with the target.


   .. attribute:: relNearestAd

      At the nearer of the ascending and descending nodes with the target.



Operations
----------

OperationAdvancedTransfer
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^


.. class:: OperationAdvancedTransfer

   An interplanetary transfer to the target body, at the departure that needs the least
   delta-v before :meth:`MechJeb.OperationAdvancedTransfer.getMaxArrivalTime`.

   .. note::

      MechJeb searches for the departure in the background. The first call to
      ``MakeNodes`` starts the search, and throws an :class:`MechJeb.OperationException`
      until it finishes. See :meth:`MechJeb.OperationAdvancedTransfer.getSearching`.

   .. method:: getMaxArrivalTime()
               setMaxArrivalTime(value)

      The latest arrival time, as a time from now, in seconds.

      :Property: Can be read or written
      :rtype: ``number``
      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: getIncludeCaptureBurn()
               setIncludeCaptureBurn(value)

      Whether to plan a capture burn at the destination.

      :Property: Can be read or written
      :rtype: ``boolean``
      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: getPeriapsisHeight()
               setPeriapsisHeight(value)

      The altitude of the periapsis at the destination, in meters.

      :Property: Can be read or written
      :rtype: ``number``
      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: getSearching()

      Whether MechJeb is searching for the departure.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``boolean``
      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: getSearchProgress()

      The progress of the search for the departure, as a percentage.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``number``
      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: getErrorMessage()

      The warning or error from the last call to ``MakeNodes``, or an empty string.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``string``
      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: makeNodes()

      Make the maneuver nodes of the operation. Throws an :class:`MechJeb.OperationException`
      if the operation cannot make them.

      :returns: The maneuver nodes.
      :rtype: ``Array`` of :class:`SpaceCenter.Node`
      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         The nodes follow the vessel's last maneuver node, if it has one. A warning may be
         left in ``ErrorMessage``.

^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

   Circularize the orbit.

   .. note::

      To raise the periapsis to the apoapsis, set the time to :attr:`MechJeb.TimeReference.apoapsis`, and to lower the apoapsis to the periapsis, set it to :attr:`MechJeb.TimeReference.periapsis`. These are the cheapest.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set

OperationResonantOrbit
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^


.. class:: OperationResonantOrbit

   Change to a resonant orbit, to place satellites in a constellation.

   .. note::

      Start from an orbit in the plane of the constellation. To deploy satellites, set the
      denominator to their number. A numerator one less than the denominator is the cheapest,
      though not the fastest. The two numbers must have no common factor.

   .. method:: getErrorMessage()

      The warning or error from the last call to ``MakeNodes``, or an empty string.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``string``
      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: makeNodes()

      Make the maneuver nodes of the operation. Throws an :class:`MechJeb.OperationException`
      if the operation cannot make them.

      :returns: The maneuver nodes.
      :rtype: ``Array`` of :class:`SpaceCenter.Node`
      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         The nodes follow the vessel's last maneuver node, if it has one. A warning may be

OperationTransfer
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^


.. class:: OperationTransfer

   A bi-impulsive (Hohmann) transfer to the target, within one sphere of influence. It
   suits a rendezvous with a vessel or a transfer to a moon.

   .. note::

      Despite the name, the transfer is often uni-impulsive. It happens at the selected time,
      or at the optimum time.

   .. method:: getErrorMessage()

      The warning or error from the last call to ``MakeNodes``, or an empty string.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: ``string``
      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: makeNodes()

      Make the maneuver nodes of the operation. Throws an :class:`MechJeb.OperationException`
      if the operation cannot make them.

      :returns: The maneuver nodes.
      :rtype: ``Array`` of :class:`SpaceCenter.Node`
      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         The nodes follow the vessel's last maneuver node, if it has one. A warning may be
         left in ``ErrorMessage``.

   .. method:: getCapture()
               setCapture(value)

      Whether the transfer ends in the target's orbit. Without capture, it is an impact or
      a flyby.

      :Property: Can be read or written
      :rtype: ``boolean``
      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: getPlanCapture()
               setPlanCapture(value)

      Whether to make a second node for the capture burn.

      :Property: Can be read or written
      :rtype: ``boolean``
      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         Requires :meth:`MechJeb.OperationTransfer.getCapture`.

   .. method:: getRendezvous()
               setRendezvous(value)

      Whether to meet the target, rather than reach its orbit.

      :Property: Can be read or written
      :rtype: ``boolean``
      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: getLagTime()
               setLagTime(value)

      The time by which the vessel arrives behind the target, in seconds.

      :Property: Can be read or written
      :rtype: ``number``
      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         Requires :meth:`MechJeb.OperationTransfer.getRendezvous`.

   .. method:: getCoplanar()
               setCoplanar(value)

      Whether the transfer stays in the vessel's orbital plane.

      :Property: Can be read or written
      :rtype: ``boolean``
      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: getTimeSelector()

      The time of the maneuver.

      :Property: Read-only, cannot be set
      :rtype: :class:`MechJeb.TimeSelector`
      :Game Scenes: Flight
