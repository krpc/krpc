SmartASS
========


.. class:: SmartASS

   The Smart A.S.S. window, which points the vessel in a chosen direction.

   .. method:: getInterfaceMode()
               setInterfaceMode(value)

      The mode of the window, which selects its buttons and nothing else.
      Throws an exception if set to :attr:`MechJeb.SmartASSInterfaceMode.automatic`.

      :Property: Can be read or written
      :rtype: :class:`MechJeb.SmartASSInterfaceMode`
      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: getAutopilotMode()
               setAutopilotMode(value)

      The autopilot mode. Throws an exception if set to
      :attr:`MechJeb.SmartASSAutopilotMode.automatic`.

      :Property: Can be read or written
      :rtype: :class:`MechJeb.SmartASSAutopilotMode`
      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         Call :meth:`MechJeb.SmartASS.update` to apply a change.

   .. method:: update(resetPID)

      Apply the settings.

      :param resetPID: Whether to reset the attitude controller. Use it only if the vessel does not follow the new settings.
      :type resetPID: ``boolean``
      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: getForceYaw()
               setForceYaw(value)

      Whether the autopilot controls the yaw in the :attr:`MechJeb.SmartASSAutopilotMode.surface`,
      :attr:`MechJeb.SmartASSAutopilotMode.surfacePrograde` and
      :attr:`MechJeb.SmartASSAutopilotMode.surfaceRetrograde` modes.

      :Property: Can be read or written
      :rtype: ``boolean``
      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: getForcePitch()
               setForcePitch(value)

      Whether the autopilot controls the pitch in the :attr:`MechJeb.SmartASSAutopilotMode.surface`,
      :attr:`MechJeb.SmartASSAutopilotMode.surfacePrograde` and
      :attr:`MechJeb.SmartASSAutopilotMode.surfaceRetrograde` modes.

      :Property: Can be read or written
      :rtype: ``boolean``
      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: getForceRoll()
               setForceRoll(value)

      Whether the autopilot controls the roll.

      :Property: Can be read or written
      :rtype: ``boolean``
      :Game Scenes: Flight

   .. method:: getRoll()
               setRoll(value)

      The roll, in degrees. 0 is top side up.

      :Property: Can be read or written
      :rtype: ``number``
      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         Requires :meth:`MechJeb.SmartASS.getForceRoll`, and applies in every mode but
         :attr:`MechJeb.SmartASSAutopilotMode.killRot` and the surface modes.

   .. method:: getSurfaceHeading()
               setSurfaceHeading(value)

      The heading, in degrees.

      :Property: Can be read or written
      :rtype: ``number``
      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         Requires :meth:`MechJeb.SmartASS.getForceYaw`, and applies in the :attr:`MechJeb.SmartASSAutopilotMode.surface`
         mode.

   .. method:: getSurfacePitch()
               setSurfacePitch(value)

      The pitch above the horizon, in degrees. 90 is straight up.

      :Property: Can be read or written
      :rtype: ``number``
      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         Requires :meth:`MechJeb.SmartASS.getForcePitch`, and applies in the :attr:`MechJeb.SmartASSAutopilotMode.surface`
         mode.

   .. method:: getSurfaceRoll()
               setSurfaceRoll(value)

      The roll, in degrees. 0 is top side up.

      :Property: Can be read or written
      :rtype: ``number``
      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         Requires :meth:`MechJeb.SmartASS.getForceRoll`, and applies in the :attr:`MechJeb.SmartASSAutopilotMode.surface`
         mode.

   .. method:: getSurfaceVelYaw()
               setSurfaceVelYaw(value)

      The yaw from the surface velocity, in degrees.

      :Property: Can be read or written
      :rtype: ``number``
      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         Requires :meth:`MechJeb.SmartASS.getForceYaw`, and applies in the
         :attr:`MechJeb.SmartASSAutopilotMode.surfacePrograde` and
         :attr:`MechJeb.SmartASSAutopilotMode.surfaceRetrograde` modes.

   .. method:: getSurfaceVelPitch()
               setSurfaceVelPitch(value)

      The pitch from the surface velocity, in degrees.

      :Property: Can be read or written
      :rtype: ``number``
      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         Requires :meth:`MechJeb.SmartASS.getForcePitch`, and applies in the
         :attr:`MechJeb.SmartASSAutopilotMode.surfacePrograde` and
         :attr:`MechJeb.SmartASSAutopilotMode.surfaceRetrograde` modes.

   .. method:: getSurfaceVelRoll()
               setSurfaceVelRoll(value)

      The roll, in degrees. 0 is top side up.

      :Property: Can be read or written
      :rtype: ``number``
      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         Requires :meth:`MechJeb.SmartASS.getForceRoll`, and applies in the
         :attr:`MechJeb.SmartASSAutopilotMode.surfacePrograde` and
         :attr:`MechJeb.SmartASSAutopilotMode.surfaceRetrograde` modes.

   .. method:: getAdvancedReference()
               setAdvancedReference(value)

      The reference frame of :meth:`MechJeb.SmartASS.getAdvancedDirection`. Throws an exception if
      MechJeb uses a reference this enumeration lacks.

      :Property: Can be read or written
      :rtype: :class:`MechJeb.AttitudeReference`
      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         Applies in the :attr:`MechJeb.SmartASSAutopilotMode.advanced` mode.

   .. method:: getAdvancedDirection()
               setAdvancedDirection(value)

      The direction in :meth:`MechJeb.SmartASS.getAdvancedReference`.

      :Property: Can be read or written
      :rtype: :class:`MechJeb.Direction`
      :Game Scenes: Flight


      .. note::

         Applies in the :attr:`MechJeb.SmartASSAutopilotMode.advanced` mode.



.. class:: SmartASSInterfaceMode

   The mode of the Smart A.S.S. window. See :meth:`MechJeb.SmartASS.getInterfaceMode`.


   .. attribute:: orbital

      The orbital modes.


   .. attribute:: surface

      The surface modes.


   .. attribute:: target

      The target modes.


   .. attribute:: advanced

      The advanced mode.


   .. attribute:: automatic

      Set by MechJeb's autopilots. Cannot be set.



.. class:: SmartASSAutopilotMode

   The autopilot mode of Smart A.S.S. See :meth:`MechJeb.SmartASS.getAutopilotMode`.


   .. attribute:: off

      Off.


   .. attribute:: killRot

      Stop the vessel's rotation.


   .. attribute:: node

      Point at the next maneuver node.


   .. attribute:: surface

      Point in the direction set by :meth:`MechJeb.SmartASS.getSurfaceHeading`,
      :meth:`MechJeb.SmartASS.getSurfacePitch` and :meth:`MechJeb.SmartASS.getSurfaceRoll`.


   .. attribute:: prograde

      Point orbital prograde.


   .. attribute:: retrograde

      Point orbital retrograde.


   .. attribute:: normalPlus

      Point orbit normal.


   .. attribute:: normalMinus

      Point orbit anti-normal.


   .. attribute:: radialPlus

      Point radially outward.


   .. attribute:: radialMinus

      Point radially inward.


   .. attribute:: relativePlus

      Point along the velocity relative to the target.


   .. attribute:: relativeMinus

      Point against the velocity relative to the target.


   .. attribute:: targetPlus

      Point at the target.


   .. attribute:: targetMinus

      Point away from the target.


   .. attribute:: parallelPlus

      Point parallel to the target's orientation. For a docking port, along its axis
      and away from it.


   .. attribute:: parallelMinus

      Point antiparallel to the target's orientation. For a docking port, along its axis
      and toward it.


   .. attribute:: advanced

      Point in the direction set by :meth:`MechJeb.SmartASS.getAdvancedReference` and
      :meth:`MechJeb.SmartASS.getAdvancedDirection`.


   .. attribute:: automatic

      Set by MechJeb's autopilots. Cannot be set.


   .. attribute:: surfacePrograde

      Point along the surface velocity.


   .. attribute:: surfaceRetrograde

      Point against the surface velocity.


   .. attribute:: horizontalPlus

      Point along the horizontal surface velocity.


   .. attribute:: horizontalMinus

      Point against the horizontal surface velocity.


   .. attribute:: verticalPlus

      Point up, normal to the surface.



.. class:: AttitudeReference

   A reference frame of Smart A.S.S. See :meth:`MechJeb.SmartASS.getAdvancedReference`.


   .. attribute:: inertial

      The world coordinate system.


   .. attribute:: orbit

      Forward is prograde, left is normal and up is radial out.


   .. attribute:: orbitHorizontal

      Forward is the orbital velocity projected onto the surface, and up is the surface normal.


   .. attribute:: surfaceNorth

      Forward is north, left is west and up is the surface normal.


   .. attribute:: surfaceVelocity

      Forward is the surface velocity, and up is the surface normal perpendicular to it.


   .. attribute:: target

      Forward is toward the target, and up is the vessel's heading perpendicular to it.


   .. attribute:: relativeVelocity

      Forward is the velocity relative to the target.


   .. attribute:: targetOrientation

      Forward is the direction the target faces, and up is the target's up.


   .. attribute:: maneuverNode

      Forward is the burn direction of the next maneuver node.


   .. attribute:: sun

      Forward is the orbital velocity of the body that orbits the sun, and up is radial
      out of that orbit.


   .. attribute:: surfaceHorizontal

      Forward is the horizontal surface velocity, and up is the surface normal.



.. class:: Direction

   A direction of Smart A.S.S. See :meth:`MechJeb.SmartASS.getAdvancedDirection`.


   .. attribute:: forward

      Forward.


   .. attribute:: back

      Back.


   .. attribute:: up

      Up.


   .. attribute:: down

      Down.


   .. attribute:: right

      Right.


   .. attribute:: left

      Left.
